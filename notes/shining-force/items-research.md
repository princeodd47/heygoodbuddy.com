# Shining Force items research

Working notes behind `docs/games/shining-force/items.md`. This folder is outside `docs/`,
so it never gets published to the site.

Everything below is our own summary. Don't copy from sources.

**How to use:** full-game page; the user has given no progress for this
game, so nothing is held back. If the user reports something different
in-game, the in-game value wins. Fix the page and these notes.

Data lives in `data/shining-force/items.yaml`.

## Sources (researched 2026-10-26)

- Dark_Vortex (Quan Jin), Shining Force Guide and Walkthrough v1.0, GameFAQs genesis faqs/51880: shop items with heal amounts, chest contents.
- almarsguides Shining Force items list is for the GBA remake (Resurrection
  of the Dark Dragon), not used for values.

## Open questions

- Stat item amounts: guide 14731 says +1 to 2 for all five; GLSicherman
  (2837) says +2 flat for attack/defense/agility/move and 1d2 for max HP.
  Page says +1 to 2. Confirm in-game if it matters.
- Shower of Cure: only 14731 gives a number ("around 40 HP to entire
  party"); 22966 says it heals everyone on the map. Page says about 40 HP.

## Resolved 2026-10-27

- Hidden costumes and joke items: the US save-hacking guide (7586) lists
  Sugoi Mizugi, Kitui Huku, Kenji, Teppou, Kaku-Chan and Yougi in the US
  item table, so they're in the US release. Locations from DvD (63031);
  now on the page as their own group.

## Held back

- Kindan No Hako (Forbidden Box): in the ROM but not findable in normal
  play, per DvD and 7586.

## Already on the page

- Shop items, chest-only items with locations, key items.
