# Flesh and Blood Effects research

Working notes behind `docs/games/flesh-and-blood/effects.md`. This folder is outside `docs/`,
so it never gets published to the site.

This page was split out of the Rules page on 2026-11-21. Sources, how to check
them, card-example rules and what was left out are in `rules-research.md`
and apply here too.

## Already on the page

Optional / conditional / targeted effects, effect failure, events (0 is no event, fail to find, multi-events), continuous effects and staging, replacement order and prevention types, triggered effects.

## Quiz

Topic `effects` in `data/flesh-and-blood/quiz.yaml`: r22–r30, r66–r74
(18 questions). r66–r74 were checked against the CR text on 2026-11-21. Their
cards are the page's own examples (Mark of the Huntsman, Heart of Fyendal,
Parable of Humility, Memorial Ground, Remembrance, Steadfast, Merciful
Retribution).

Heart of Fyendal's condition is checked when its pitch trigger resolves, not
when it's pitched (CR 6.1.3: a conditional discrete effect is checked when it
would be generated). The page said "when it's pitched" until 2026-11-21.
