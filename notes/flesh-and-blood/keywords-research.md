## Quiz

Topic `keywords` in `data/flesh-and-blood/quiz.yaml`: r03, r04, r07, r08,
r31–r35, r89–r94 (15 questions). r89–r94 were checked against the CR text on
2026-11-21. Each tags the card from the keyword table's Example column.

Sharpen removes *all* +1 power counters at the beginning of the end phase,
not just the one it added (CR 8.5.58). The table row says so as of 2026-11-21.

Not asked on purpose: whether Ward is optional. CR 8.3.20 has no "may"
(Spellvoid and Arcane Barrier do), but i05's release note talks about
choosing to use it. Settle this from an official ruling before writing a
question.
