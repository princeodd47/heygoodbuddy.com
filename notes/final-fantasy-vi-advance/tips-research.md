# FF6 Advance Tips: research notes

## Combos (2026-11-18)

The user gave three combos (with some SNES names); the Tips tab's Combos
section has them under GBA names, from data.json equipment:

- Offering = Master's Scroll ("Fight hits four times per turn, at half
  damage each"); Atma Weapon = Ultima Weapon; Gem Box = Soul of Thamasa
  (Dualcast; equipped Espers can't be summoned); Economizer = Celestriad
  (every spell 1 MP, Lores and summons too).
- Locke: Genji Glove + Master's Scroll + Ultima Weapon + Valiant Knife.
  Equipment notes: Ultima Weapon ignores defense, strongest at full HP,
  weaker below level 64; Valiant Knife ignores defense and adds missing HP
  to each hit. They peak at opposite HP, which the card says.
- Setzer: Master's Scroll + Fixed Dice in the back row. Dice damage =
  product of the three dice × 2 × level (× the number again on triples), per
  the equipment note. "Not weakened in the back row" is the user's advice.
- Quick + Soul of Thamasa + Celestriad: one damage spell + Quick, then two
  Quick turns of two spells each = five damage spells. Quick: 99 MP, "the
  caster takes two turns in a row" (Esper data).
- Added after the user asked (from data.json equipment notes):
  Dragoon Boots ("Fight becomes Jump") + Dragon Horn ("Jump lands several
  times"); Umaro's Berserker Ring (SNES Rage Ring: Throw that ignores
  defense; absorbs Fire, nullifies Lightning) + Blizzard Orb (Snowstorm;
  absorbs Ice, nullifies Fire); Hero's Ring + Earring (see the 1.17 notes in
  walkthrough-research.md).
- Not added: Vanish + Doom-type status bugs (GBA behaviour unconfirmed);
  elemental-absorb combos (armor absorbs aren't in the guide's data).

## Front and back row (2026-11-19)

From the user: the back row takes half physical damage and deals half
damage with Fight and commands like it; Blitz, Tools and Bushido aren't
affected; all of Setzer's weapons deal full damage from the back row.

- Added from general FF6 knowledge (not from the user): Throw, Jump and
  magic-type commands (spells, Lores, Dance, Slot, summons) also ignore the
  row; magic damage taken is the same in either row; Mug and the Genji
  Glove / Master's Scroll extra hits are Fight, so they're halved.
- The weapon list on the page is generated from data.json equipment notes
  ("Full damage from the back row."), shuriken left out (they're Throw
  items). Dice and Fixed Dice were missing that note; added, per the user.
- Not added: back attacks swapping rows, and whether Gau's Rage attacks
  are halved (unconfirmed for this guide).
