# FF Resonance Colosseum research

Notes behind `docs/games/final-fantasy-resonance/colosseum.md`.

## Sources

- meowdb (NiaDB): `/db/final-fantasy-resonance/colosseum` (lineups, rewards,
  leagues), `/colosseum/beginner-league-battle-N` (Fledgling = "beginner"
  slug), `/areas/colosseum-2016` (shop stock and which Proof each item
  needs), enemy pages for stats.
- vorxu.com/final-fantasy-resonance/colosseum: shop prices and limits.
- allthings.how/final-fantasy-resonance-colosseum: Receptionist,
  Quartermaster "room to the right", retries, refights.
- RPG Site walkthrough: Colosseum opens when Grandshelt reopens in ch. 4,
  before the capital. Others say the Zoldaad bulletin board announces it.
- PowerPyx: Tablet Shard 58 in the Colosseum, west on the first path.

## Open questions

- Healing between battles, items allowed, fleeing, EXP/drops from fights:
  no source says.
- Remedy is listed twice on meowdb (once no Proof, once Fledgling). The page
  puts it under Fledgling.
- Ancient Greatsword has 1 attack and no description. Its use is unknown
  (maybe forging). Not explained on the page.
- Enemy levels from meowdb are above the recommended level (Battle 5 is all
  lv 37 at rec. 32). Unverified in-game.

## Held back

- Leagues after Fledgling: Brawler (rec. 40–48, opens after Fledgling 5),
  Gladiator (55–70), Champion (75–99, Omega last). Rewards include Magicite,
  Adamantite, Ancient Cuirass, Magicite Cryst, Reflect/Barrier/Protect Ring,
  Super Ribbon, Lorica Sancta/Apocalyptica, Dragon Seal, Omega Badge. Repeat
  rewards: Turbo Ether (Brawler), X-Potion (Gladiator), X-Ether (Champion).
- Shop stock behind the Brawler's and Gladiator's Proofs (X-Potion, Phoenix
  Pinion, Magicite 50,000, Magicite Cryst 250,000, Indomitable, Murakumo,
  Hermes Sandals, Circlet of Enlightenment, etc.).
- Gaia Hammer breaks the desert Sealing Stone (Titan). The page only quotes
  its description. Espers are on the page only once the user has them.
