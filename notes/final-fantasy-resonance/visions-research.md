`data/final-fantasy-resonance/visions.yaml`.

## Sources

- ffresonance.wiki: all-visions-locations, sanctum-questions,
  best-visions-to-awaken, tablet-shards-magicite

## Open questions

- **Onion Knight's Sanctum:** guides disagree on the southwest vs eastern tip
  of Dirnado. The page says "on the edge of Dirnado". Ask the user.
- Zidane onward are `status: unverified` in the YAML.
- **Medicinal Mission** (Olderion, the Aquapolis) is listed as a Magicite Shard
  source but isn't on the page. Check whether it's available on the first
  Olderion visit.

## Held back

- **Clive:** Sanctum on Grandshelt per one guide. Not in the user's Vision
  list (possibly a pre-order or later unlock). Don't add unless the user has
  it.
- **Shantotto:** Sanctum of Light east of Mozul Station (encounter-map
  coordinates 1925, 1351). Reachable, but the user hasn't found it. Don't
  add or hint until they report it.
- **Other Magicite sources now reachable but not on the page:** Olderion jump
  rope 60 jumps (Magicite 2★; 30 = Mist X-Potion, 40 = Swift Badge),
  Arise, Mechabo! follow-up (kill three Sandworms, back to Verun: Magicite
  2★ + Mecha Behemoth), Weapons Factory chest (Magicite, after the
  current step), Underground Tunnel chest NE of the Crimson Pot (Magicite
  Shard, next story step).
- **Magicite (2★, green):** appears from the end of chapter 3. Priority advice
  is in `tips-research.md`.
- 26 Visions in total. Seven are side-quest rewards in chapters 4–7. Later
  ones named by sources: Shantotto (Zoldaad), Cecil (Zoldaad), Vaan (N
  Mysidia, ch. 5), Victoria (Mysidia side quest, ch. 5), Amelia, Y'shtola.
- Zidane's Treasure Hunter (Awakening 3). **Never name it before the user
  has it.**

## Already on the page

Added 2026-10-29: Cecil (Sanctum SE of Capital Station, coordinates 1706,
1595) and Y'shtola (far east of Zoldaad, near the Weapons Factory, 2040,
1754), from meowdb's encounter-map data and RPG Site. Magicite Shard sources:
Colosseum Fledgling Battles 1 and 4, the Colosseum shop (10,000 gil after
the Fledgling League) and Cactuar's rare steal (meowdb cactuar-38).

Tronn, Leah, Warrior of Light, Cloud, Terra, Zidane, Onion Knight, Noctis,
Firion, Squall, Tidus, with how to get each. Mastery and Awakening basics.
"Where to get Magicite Shards" (17 sources, through the Cave of Waters
area-1 chest, the Leviathan drop at Lake Dorr, *The Tide from the Sky* in
Town of Amore (meowdb quest the-tide-from-the-sky-request-21; also 1,500 gil)
and the Water Shrine waterfall chest).

No Vision Sanctum in Olderion past Squall/Firion/Tidus: the unlisted ones are
Bartz and Lightning (Mysidia), Clive (Grandshelt), Y'shtola (Zoldaad).
The 40-shard box gives a Magicite (2★); listed on the Tablet Shards page only.

Chests checked (meowdb) with no Magicite Shard: Old Town (Lightning Scroll I
×3, Ether, Mist Hi-Potion), Waterfall Way (Devout's Armor, Eye Drops ×2,
Phoenix Pinion, Guardian Sword, Aquatic Veil), the rest of the Cave of
Waters (Total Calisthenics, Shuriken ×3, Ice Scroll I ×2, Ninja Gear).
