# FF Resonance tips research

Working notes behind `docs/games/final-fantasy-resonance/tips.md`. This folder
is outside `docs/`, so it never gets published to the site.

Everything below is our own summary. Don't copy from sources: ffresonance.wiki
prohibits it, and video transcripts aren't ours to republish.

**How to use:** When progress moves forward, check the "Held back" sections up
to the new chapter and move whatever is now reachable onto the Tips page.
Re-check anything marked *unverified* in-game (Libra) before adding it. The
in-game value always wins.

## Open questions / conflicts

- **Metal Minituar HP:** Walkthrough Forge says 6. Sol Invictus says 3
  Casual / 9 Expert. Primalliquid says 3 / 4 / 6. The page avoids a number.
  Ask the user to Libra it.
- **Tidus mastery ranks:** Video says the best two are ranks 5 and 7, but
  doesn't say which ability is which. The page gives no rank.
- **Squall Trigger rank:** not stated.
- **Zidane Steal rank:** not stated. One video says having Zidane equipped
  also raises the rare chance, but it's unclear whether that's a gated
  ability. Left out per the stealing rule.
- **Destroyer:** Sol Invictus found one in the south Olderion Gil Snapper
  spot, but the bestiary left Destroyer out as northern. Check with the user.
- **Mist Potion farm:** NE of Sanctum of Firion (Olderion south), 22 per 100
  fights. Probably reachable now, not added yet.
- **Jump rope:** milestones at 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 60 jumps. Only the 10-jump
  Magicite Shard is on the site. Other rewards unknown.
- **Recuperate LB** passive (refills 30% LB after using one): meowdb, marked
  unconfirmed.
- **Dwarven Forge fast travel** lands next to the inn, save point, Vision
  Moogle and item shop (YouTube comment, unverified).
- **Inn cost:** 100 gil is from two sources; confirm if prices change later.

## Held back: Chapter 3 (rest of chapter)

- The user is past Lake Dorr, Waterfall Way and the Altar (2026-10-28).
  Leviathan strategy (break the Orbs first, Lightning, Shell, Esuna) is no
  longer useful, so it was dropped.
- **Archwitch Fina** joins the party around the Old Town flood (story
  summary). Not mentioned on any page.

- **Carbuncle**: bulletin board / rumor in Olderion, then Ruby Cave in the
  mountains south of Olderion (a stele starts the quest). User chose to
  leave unobtained Espers out. Don't add until they have it.
- **Water Shrine** (now on the page): Viridian Pot added to Pots; Megalodon
  and Monster Effigy (1,350 EXP each) added to farming. Monster Effigy's rare
  steal Aeolian Misanga (5% Speed accessory) is only on the Bestiary.
- **Pot mechanics:** use the item **on the Pot** (the user confirmed in-game,
  2026-10-29, that Mist Ethers can target enemies; matches meowdb). The IGN
  video's "use it on your own party" was wrong.
- Pot facts from meowdb: two of each Pot, each a one-time fight; nullify
  physical and magic; heal 99,999 each turn with "Gimme a ...!"; rare drop
  Elixir. Later Pots: Crimson (Turbo Ether; Underground Tunnel, Weapons
  Factory), Azure (Mist Turbo Ether; Arcane Library, Tower of Mysidia),
  Violet (X-Ether; Research Center Ruins, Sanctissimus Shrine), Pearly
  (Elixir; Crystal Tower ×2).
- **Green Magicite (2★)** starts appearing at the end of ch. 3. Purplatypus's
  priorities: 1) Onion Knight (Blade Torrent comes at 2★; Resonance buffs it;
  Chuck +50% Throw), 2) Noctis (Plunge, +50% chance to join the Bonus
  Phase), 3) Terra (Auto-Faith, -ga spells, Meltdown hits allies too),
  4) Cloud (Cross Slash +25% vs staggered, crit +50% vs staggered).
  Throwing scrolls is strong.
- Town of Amore shops (meowdb): Icebrand 7,700, Shiranui 7,350, Knight
  Armor 5,500, Calm Armlet 2,000, Mist Ether 2,500 and more. Not on the page.

Metal Cactuar: lv 36, flees from turn 2 (36%/turn). Metal Gigantuar: lv 52,
flees from turn 2 (46%/turn). Cactuars/Gigantuars use Needles every third
turn.

**Farming gear and abilities:**
- Colosseum opens ch. 4 (board in Zoldaad). Foe Attractor (+50% encounters,
  12,000 gil) after the Fledgling League. Warding Bell halves encounters.
  Magicite sold there for 10,000–250,000 gil depending on league. Titan's
  seal needs Fledgling Battle 5.
- Brigand's Gloves (better steal rate): 4,000 gil, airship shop, ch. 5.
- Zidane's Treasure Hunter (guaranteed rare on a successful steal): Awakening
  3. **Never name this before the user has it.**
- Adept's Armlet (double EXP): 25,000 gil, airship shop / Crystal Tower, ch. 6+.
- Vaan (Sanctum of Vaan, N Mysidia, ch. 5): Gil Farmer, Gil Toss (rank 5),
  Double Gil (rank 9).
- Victoria (ch. 5 side quest, Mysidia): +50% MRP at rank 7, Drop Rate Up
  (+25 points per roll) at rank 9.
- Mastery Pendant (double MRP): Mysidia Radar treasure No. 13 (via
  High-Power Generator, 60,000 gil).
- Amelia rank 3 **Aim** (all attacks hit for 5 turns). Gravity/Graviga cut
  % HP and always hit.
- Y'shtola Vision and Shiva are the Ice options against Gil Snappers later.
- Gil Snapper steals: Golden Shell (5,000), King's Large Golden Shell (10,000).

**Other later content:**
- Pots later: Water Shrine (ch. 3), Underground Tunnel and Weapons Factory
  (ch. 4), Arcane Library and Tower of Mysidia (ch. 5), Research Center Ruins
  and Sanctissimus Shrine (ch. 6), Crystal Tower Pearly Pots (ch. 8).
- Points of no return: only the Crystal Tower is final. Nothing is missable.
- Rematches cost 100 gil (meowdb).
- Party members later: Jake, Sakura, Nichol, and one secret Saboteur. Roles:
  Nichol Synergist (buffer, Libra), Jake and Sakura Commando.
- 9 Espers total. 26 Visions. 99 Tablet Shards (89 for the trophy).
