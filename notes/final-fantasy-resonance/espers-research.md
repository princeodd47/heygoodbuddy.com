## Held back

The user chose to list only Espers they've obtained, even ones already
reachable. 9 Espers in total.

| Esper | Where / how | Status |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **Carbuncle** | Rumor/bulletin board in Olderion → Ruby Cave in the mountains south of Olderion; a stele starts the quest | Reachable now. Don't name or hint until the user has it. |
| **Titan** | Sealed in the Grandshelt desert; needs Colosseum Fledgling Battle 5 | Not yet possible |
| **Ifrit** | Zoldaad, Esper's Dwelling | Chapter 4+ |
| **Shiva** | Mysidia, Esper's Dwelling | Chapter 5 |
| **Leviathan** | Story fight at Lake Dorr (ch. 3) doesn't give it: the user confirmed 2026-10-28 it didn't join. itemlevel.net says it joins in ch. 5 after side quest *Nichol's Duty*, back at Lake Dorr | Later (ch. 5?) |
| **Odin** | Beat Odin's Shadow (lv 45) | Later |
| **Bahamut** | Lv 50 fight | Later |

Note: Carbuncle grants party **Reflect**, which also bounces your own healing
spells. The Tips page mentions Reflect generically.
