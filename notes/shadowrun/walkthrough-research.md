## Open questions

- **Boris's fee.** Mike's walkthrough says 500 nuyen; Mike's Redmond
  Barrens map legend says 100. The page gives both.
- **Club Penumbra reputation.** RPGClassics says about 3, Mike's says at
  least 4. The page says 4.
- **Greenhouse fight.** RPGClassics treats it as the first boss; Mike's says
  it's likely too strong to win. The page warns you may not win yet.
- **Mitsuhama elevator.** RPGClassics says the right elevator, Mike's says
  the northeast corner. The page says northeast (they're probably the same).
- **Hell Hound Pelt.** RPGClassics says the Sinsearach or Renraku hell hound
  patrols; Mike's says Salish-Shidhe hell hounds or city encounters. The
  page uses Mike's.
- **Stark's hut.** RPGClassics puts it "deep within the Sinsearach" and the
  east fork on the way there leads to Stark's quest. Before I Play's
  "hut in the middle of nowhere" ally is assumed to be the same quest.
- Ogami's computer passcode and Matrix ICE details aren't given by either
  source.
- The sources spell it Sinsearch and Sinsearach, and Tarislar and Tarlisar.
  The page uses Sinsearach and Tarislar.
