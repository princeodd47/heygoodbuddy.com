# Dresspheres research

## Sources (researched 2026-10-29)

- Jegged FFX-2 dressphere pages: ability lists with AP and prerequisites,
  per character where they differ.
- Gamer Guides dressphere pages: cross-check of how each is obtained.
- Built by `tools/build_data.py` into
  `data/final-fantasy-x-2/dresspheres.yaml`. "How to get" is written in our
  own words in the script's DRESS list.

## Decisions

- 19 dresspheres including the three Specials. Festivalist and Trainer
  fall back to the Yuna/Rikku/Paine tabs; the Specials use their part
  names (Floral Fallal, Right Pistil, Left Pistil and so on).
- Ability descriptions left out deliberately (copy concerns).
