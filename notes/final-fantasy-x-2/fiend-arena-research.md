# FFX-2 Fiend Arena research

Working notes behind `docs/games/final-fantasy-x-2/fiend-arena.md` and
`data/final-fantasy-x-2/arena.yaml`.

Everything below is our own summary. Don't copy from sources.

**How to use:** the user has played FFX-2 many times and wants no spoiler
gating, so nothing is held back for progress. If the user reports something
different in-game, the in-game value wins. Fix the page and these notes.

Raw downloads (Jegged and Gamer Guides HTML) were saved to the session
scratchpad and parsed by `tools/build_data.py`; re-download to rebuild.

## Sources (researched 2026-10-29)

- Jegged, Creature Creator > Fiend Arena: unlocks, prize order, opponent
  teams with their unlock conditions, Battle Simulator rules, recruiting.
  This is the data source (HD-specific).
- Gamer Guides, Creature Create > The Colosseum: per-cup pages. Cross-check.
- Steam thread 2741975115069502725 (Hinnyuu, 2017): Chocobo Cup appears
  after a Grand Cup: Hard win; Battle Simulator only needs a team met.

## Open questions

- **Chocobo Cup unlock:** Jegged and Steam say a Grand Cup: Hard win;
  Gamer Guides says one Grand Cup win. Page uses Jegged.
- **Cactuar Cup unlock:** Jegged says Grand Cup: Hard x4; Gamer Guides
  says one Grand Cup win. Page uses Jegged.
- **Level caps:** Gamer Guides lists a "level cap" per cup (Standard 19,
  Standard Hard 29, Grand 39, Grand Hard 49, Chocobo 59, Cactuar 69, Youth
  League 69, Aeon 79, Demon/Fiend World 89). Nobody else mentions it, and
  the Steam guide grinds to the 50s in Standard Hard, so it's left off.
  Could be the opponents' level ceiling. Check in game.
- **Grand Cup first win:** Gamer Guides says Festivalist Dressphere;
  Jegged lists nothing. Noted on the page.
- **Demon Cup level-ups:** Gamer Guides says level each creature five
  times; one Gamer Guides page says three. Tales are 3 to 5 Story Levels
  (Jegged). Page just says "finish its tale".
- Gamer Guides names the cup "Demon Cup / Shinra's Cup"; Jegged uses
  Fiend Portal / Fiend World Cup / Almighty Shinra Cup. Page uses Jegged's.
