# FFX-2 creatures research

Working notes behind `docs/games/final-fantasy-x-2/creatures.md` and
`data/final-fantasy-x-2/creatures.yaml`.

Everything below is our own summary. Don't copy from sources.

**How to use:** the user has played FFX-2 many times and wants no spoiler
gating, so nothing is held back for progress. If the user reports something
different in-game, the in-game value wins. Fix the page and these notes.

Raw downloads (Jegged and Gamer Guides HTML) were saved to the session
scratchpad and parsed by `tools/build_data.py`; re-download to rebuild.

## Sources (researched 2026-10-29)

- Jegged Creature Creator > Trap Creature: per-location tables with pod,
  SP family, chapters and notes (data source); Creature History: Fayth
  fragments, release unlocks; Overview: pods, eight-creature limit.
- Gamer Guides Small/Medium/Large/Special creature lists: cross-check.

## Open questions

- Gamer Guides says Red Elemental and Tonberry are Ch. 1-5 in Mushroom
  Rock; Jegged has Red Elemental there only in Ch. 1-2. Kilika is safe
  either way.
- Gamer Guides lists Machina Hunter only in Bikanel, Chocobo also in Luca;
  Jegged adds Luca for many creatures. Jegged's Luca list is nearly every
  creature, so the page warns the odds are poor there.
- Fandom and StrategyWiki are behind Cloudflare and couldn't be read.
