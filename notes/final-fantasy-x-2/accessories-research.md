# FFX-2 accessories research

Working notes behind `docs/games/final-fantasy-x-2/accessories.md` and
`data/final-fantasy-x-2/accessories.yaml`.

Everything below is our own summary. Don't copy from sources.

**How to use:** the user has played FFX-2 many times and wants no spoiler
gating, so nothing is held back for progress. If the user reports something
different in-game, the in-game value wins. Fix the page and these notes.

Raw downloads (Jegged and Gamer Guides HTML) were saved to the session
scratchpad and parsed by `tools/build_data.py`; re-download to rebuild.

## Sources (researched 2026-10-29)

- Jegged Accessories list (128 entries): category, worn effect, sell
  price, chests, shops, steals, drops, bribes, prizes (data source).
- Feed column from Jegged Develop Creature, falling back to the
  accessory page's own feed line (Invincible).
- Gamer Guides accessory feed table: cross-check; conflicts are listed in
  creature-team-research.md.

## Open questions

- Shop prices and chest locations are Jegged's only; not cross-checked.
- Worn effects on creatures differ from the girls (Shinra's tutorial);
  the page says so but doesn't list creature-worn effects.
