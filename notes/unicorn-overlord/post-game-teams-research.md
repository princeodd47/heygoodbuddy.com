transcripts and guide text aren't ours to republish.

**How to use:** The user has finished the game, recruited everyone and has
every unique item. Goal: 10 squads (the army cap) on the hardest difficulty,
no unique item used twice, no class-change items. Must include Scarlett,
Virginia, Ilenia, Berengaria, Hilda, Amalia, Rosalinde, Yunifi and Ochlys.
If the user reports different item counts or skill names in-game, the
in-game value wins. Fix the page and these notes.

## Key facts

- Only one Limited start-of-battle skill fires per side. Turn the others off.
- **From the user:** PP and AP cap at 4. Hastened Charge (Celestial
  Talisman, 4 PP) triggers at the start of battle and counts as that
  squad's one start-of-battle skill. It uses all of Hilda's PP, so Aerial
  Wing (Heavenwyvern Reins, 1 PP, truestrike) can't fire too. The only PP
  refill found is the Wereowl skill Restore (+1 PP after an ally's passive),
  and Ramona is the only Wereowl. Dragoon Dive has a 100% hit rate but
  isn't a truestrike.
- **User decision (2026-11-10):** Ramona and Sanatio swap squads and gear.
  Sanatio wears the White Cat-Ear Hood in squad 1; Ramona (Papal Crosier)
  goes to squad 2 with Restore on Ochlys. Yahna wears the Sniper's Amber
  Lens (Hawk Eye, 2 PP: truestrike and guaranteed crit) and a Sapphire
  Pendant, replacing her Sorcerer's Medallion and Lapis. Quick Cast plus
  Hawk Eye = 4 PP. Not yet verified in-game: Hawk Eye firing on the cast
  rather than the charge start, and a Sorceress equipping the Lens.
- **From the user:** Limited Heal is a Wereowl class skill, not from Euros'
  Staff. Sanatio uses Overheal in squad 1 instead.
- Trinity Rain: Millennium Scepter (Divine Shards). Quick Cast gives max
  initiative, -50% crit. White Cat-Ear Hood's Quick Impetus lets the caster
  act again, skipping the charge. Familiar's Choker = Sorcerous Connection,
  +50% magic attack to an ally. Sergeant Keen Call = guaranteed crit.
- Berengaria: Death Spin and Vicious Torment get +75 potency on afflicted.
- Elemental Roar ignores Magic Reflect.
- Dispel is the hard counter to Glacial Rain.
- Feathersword: Dancer's Bracelet gives Powerful Call (+30% phys attack).
- **From the user:** the White and Black Cat-Ear Hoods are the same item (one
  copy each). Both give Quick Impetus: after an ally uses an active skill,
  that ally acts again. On a charge skill it skips the wait.
- Hastened Action comes from Lips's Ring (not the Magia Heart). Magia Soul
  gives Recast.
- Item spellings from the GameFAQs tables: "Broach" (Leaf, Rose, Holy),
  Vengeful Caligae, Charm of Land & Sea, Acrobat's Shoes, Pursuant's
  Bracelet, Chlorotic Wand (Fire Burst), Icestorm Staff (Blizzard), Speedhex
  Staff (Hastened Curse), Ice/Thunderous Conferral.
- Elite Standard (United Front) works best in a 2-unit squad: the bigger the
  enemy's unit-count advantage, the stronger the effect (3 more = negate 5
  hits per ally).
- Golden Eggs and the Bandit Longsword stack gold bonuses (Super Tax Bros).
