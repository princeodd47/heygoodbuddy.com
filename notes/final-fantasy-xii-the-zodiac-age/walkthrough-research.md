## Resolved 2026-11-13 (Final Fantasy Wiki, read in Chrome)

- **Silence:** both guides were right. TZA has two 100% chests, Yardang
  Labyrinth and Lhusu Mines Shaft Entry; looting one removes the other.
- **Float:** Tchita Uplands, Oliphzak Rise (not the Nameless Spring).
- **Reflectga:** Feywood, the Edge of Reason, on a trap.
- **Pharos chests:** the wiki's Zodiac tables have every item on the page
  (Gungnir, Perseus Bow, Chaperon, Holy, Black Mask, Muramasa, Zeus Mace,
  Tula, Minerva Bustier, Opal Ring, Quasimodo Boots, Sash, Dragon Mail,
  Gil Toss, Flare, Ardor, White Robes, Ultima Blade).
- **Garuda:** weak to dark (also fire, thunder, water per the wiki).
- **Zodiac Spear:** Henne Mines Special Charter Shaft, 1% spawn, always the
  spear, respawns; no Diamond Armlet needed. Or the Hunt Club (10 trophies
  each to Atak, Blok, Stok, then 999,999 gil). The Jegged missables page's
  "Phase 2 Dig, wear the Diamond Armlet" is wrong.
