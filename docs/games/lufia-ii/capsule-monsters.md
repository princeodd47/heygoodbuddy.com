# Capsule Monsters

Seven capsule monsters, one per element, listed in the order you can find
them. One fights alongside the party at a time. They're all immune to
poison, silence, paralysis and sleep.

## Where to find them

| Monster | Element | Where | When |
|---------|---------|-------|------|
| **Jelze** | Neutral | Foomy Woods, through the portal north of the Lake Cave, then left and south | After Tia joins |
| **Blaze** | Fire | Ruby Cave. Right at the first fork, bomb the wall, press four switches, bomb the next wall, take the teleporter | Before the Tarantula |
| **Flash** | Light | Northern Labyrinth. Lower-right door of the moving platform room, through a one-way mini maze | Before the Mummies |
| **Gusto** | Wind | Tower of Sacrifice. Middle door of the bomb-and-arrows room | Before Idura's second fight |
| **Sully** | Soil | Flower Mountain. The door between the second and third rows of spikes | Before the Rogue Flower |
| **Darbi** | Dark | The small tree shrine on the crescent-moon island west of the Shrine of Vengeance | Once the ship can reach the south seas, after Dankirk |
| **Zeppy** | Water | Underwater cave south of Parcelyte. Its two chests have Secret Fruit | After the submarine |

## Forms

Each monster has four normal forms and a master form (M). Once a monster
has reached a form, **CAPSULE → CHANGE** switches it back to any form it
has already had.

| Monster | Form 1 | Form 2 | Form 3 | Form 4 | M |
|---------|--------|--------|--------|--------|---|
| **Jelze** | Foomy S | Foomy M | Foomy L | Foomy H | Gold Fox |
| **Blaze** | Armor Dog | Winged Lion | Wing Lizard | Blaze Dragon | Firebird |
| **Flash** | Shaggy | Sprite | Cupid | Twinkle | Unicorn |
| **Gusto** | Hard Hat | Blue Bird | Winged Horse | Green Titan | Sky Dragon |
| **Sully** | Raddisher | Red Cap | Giant | Stonehead | Centaur |
| **Darbi** | Myconido | Wolfman | Mini Imp | Big Imp | Red Dragon |
| **Zeppy** | Red Fish | Toadie | Sea Giant | Blue Titan | Fish Head |

- **Jelze** guards a lot. Steady but not flashy.
- **Blaze** hits hard but runs away once he's taken about half his HP, so
  he's poor in boss fights until M.
- **Flash** is a healer. Weak until M, then very strong.
- **Gusto** is the fastest and dodges well.
- **Sully** is the slowest and strongest, never runs, and nothing resists
  soil. Perish Punch can kill outright.
- **Darbi** is also immune to instant death, and its own attacks can kill
  outright. Low GUT, so it runs sometimes.
- **Zeppy** has high magic resistance and its ice magic silences.

## Master forms

1. Feed the monster until it reaches **form 4**.
2. Use **CAPSULE → CHANGE** to switch it to the form below.
3. Feed it the fruit below.

| Monster | Switch to | Feed | First fruit you can get |
|---------|-----------|------|-------------------------|
| **Jelze** | Foomy H (form 4) | Secret Fruit | Alunze Castle basement chest |
| **Zeppy** | Sea Giant (form 3) | Flame Fruit | Ruby Cave chest |
| **Sully** | Stonehead (form 4) | Breeze Fruit | Green Clay drop (around Bound and in the Northern Labyrinth) |
| **Gusto** | Green Titan (form 4) | Earth Fruit | Sand Gorem drop (around Bound and in the Northern Labyrinth); Dankirk North Dungeon chest |
| **Blaze** | Wing Lizard (form 3) | Charm Fruit | Evil Fish drop (Tower of Sacrifice) |
| **Darbi** | Wolfman (form 2) | Holy Fruit | White Dragon drop (Tower of Truth) |
| **Flash** | Sprite (form 2) | Dark Fruit | Black Dragon drop (around Chaed and in Dragon Mountain) |

Keep the Secret Fruit from the Alunze basement. Zeppy's cave has two more.
The Egg Dragon's fruit wish gives 20 of every fruit.

## What to feed them

The bar under the monster's name fills with each item it eats. Better items
fill more. When it's full, the monster grows into its next form.

### Feeding order

Food that works well, in the order you can get it:

| Class | Food | Where |
|-------|------|-------|
| Form 1 → 2 | Cheap daggers and armor | Elcid, Sundletan, Alunze shops |
| Form 2 → 3 | **Apple Cider** (1,000) | Sundletan shop |
| Form 3 → 4 | **Bee Rocks** | Big Bee drops, Ruby Cave |
| Form 3 → 4 | **Bronze Swords** (5,000) | Bound Kingdom shop |
| Any form | **Magic Fruit**, 8 blocks at once | Groupie drops in Idura's fights, Ancient Cave chests |

### Tips

- **Press SELECT** on the feed menu to see what the monster wants. Feeding
  it exactly that item counts double.
- **Reset trick:** check the request, feed it, then reset and reload. The
  request stays the same, so you can see it before you spend anything.
- Feeding very expensive items raises its appetite, so each bar needs more.
  Feed cheap things or its normal fruit to bring the appetite back down.
- Each monster has a normal fruit too: Jelze Secret, Flash Holy, Gusto
  Breeze, Zeppy Charm, Darbi Dark, Sully Earth, Blaze Flame. Feeding it
  switches the monster back from M.
