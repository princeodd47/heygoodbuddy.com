# Missables

Everything you can lose for good, in story order. The Forfeit Island shop
sells every item you've ever owned, so gear left on a character who leaves
isn't gone, just expensive.

## Party members who leave

| Who | Leaves | Before then |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| **Tia** | Back at Bound, after the Northern Labyrinth | Unequip her. |
| **Dekar** | Karlloon North Shrine, before the barrier room | Unequip him. The Dekar Blade sells for 7,500. |
| **Lexis** | Mountain of No Return | Unequip him. |

Each one can only go into the Ancient Cave while they're in the party.

## Items and events

| What | Where | How not to miss it |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| **Catfish Jewel** | Big Catfish, Lake Cave | Rare drop. Save before the fight and retry. *Check in game: one guide gives 12.5%.* |
| **3,000 gold** | Alunze king's reward | Pick **Nothing**. It pays more than the other choices. |
| **Camu Jewel** | Camu, Tanbel East Tower | Rare drop. Save first. *Check in game: drop rate.* |
| **Spido Jewel** | Tarantula, Ruby Cave | Rare drop. Save first. *Check in game: drop rate.* |
| **Gades Blade** | Gades, Gordovan West Tower | Win the fight you're meant to lose (7,500 HP). Otherwise it only turns up in the Ancient Cave. |
| **Holy Wings** | Parcelyte Castle basement | The king lets you take them after the Treasure Sword Shrine. |
| **Idura in the labyrinth** | Bound Kingdom | Talk to the king before the Northern Labyrinth, or the story stalls. |
| **VIP Card** | Prince of Auralio | Talk to him a second time after the Ruby Icon. |
| **Lizard Blow, Mega Shield, Legend Helm, Holy Robe** | Dragon Mountain sliding-block room | Solve it before you leave the mountain. |
| **Last save** | Kamirno tower | Finish anything else first: Ancient Cave, Egg Dragon, casino. |

## Come back later

Not missable, but easy to forget.

| What | Where | Needs |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dragon Egg | Cave to Sundletan | Hook |
| Water Jewel | Alunze Castle basement | Hook |
| Eron Hat, Tuff Buckler | Alunze Northwest Cave | Hammer |
| Fire Dagger, Camu Armor, Pearl Brace | Tanbel East Tower | Nothing. Go before talking to Guy to grab them before Camu, or come back after. |

## Dragon Eggs

Eight eggs, for the Egg Dragon (island left of Pico, by submarine):

1. Cave to Sundletan (Hook)
2. Alunze Castle basement
3. Merix, from the carpenter's daughter
4. Northern Labyrinth
5. Northern Lighthouse
6. Flower Mountain
7. Dankirk North Dungeon (cut the lone patch of grass)
8. Divine Shrine

## Ancient Cave

- The ten Iris treasures are on the deep floors. Bring them to the woman in
  Gruberik's pub.
- Only blue chests stay with you when you leave. Everything else you find in
  there is lost. *Check in game: exact rule.*
