# Surlent

Surlent is a scholar working with Dr. Muench. His party is Legin, Sork and
later Lolo.

Mantras below are from [New Playthrough](new-playthrough.md) where one fits:

| Effect | Mantra |
|--------|--------|
| Strength down | PECOAGODWEAK |
| Defense down | PECOAGODSOFT |
| Spirit down | KIPECOAVIRUS |
| Magic defense down | PECOAGODZERO |
| Strength up | PECOAPOWERUP |
| Defense up | PECOAGAIA |
| Spirit up | PECOASUNA |
| Magic defense up | COABARRIERNA |

Elemental barriers are an element word + NAREEM, like TEONAREEM for wind.
They halve that element's damage for a few turns.

## Day 0: Eremia's Manor

Go west from the Dragon God Ruins to the manor to collect the Holy Grail. Dune
and Cid are stealing it. Eremia sends you back to Dr. Muench.

## Day 1: Oriab and Thor Volcano

- Dr. Muench is on the second floor of the inn in **Oriab**, north of the
  ruins.
- The pub teaches **AQUMEL**. The northwest house has a **Purifier** in a
  desk.
- Talk to **Castagia** in the lab in the northeast corner. He lends you the
  boat to Solon.
- **Thor Volcano:** east along the bridges. South at the fork for a Medical
  Herb. The chest further east is a mantra puzzle: **REEM**, the elemental
  barrier.
- Take the south door, cross the magma and talk to **Solon**. **Sork** joins.

## Day 2: Great Forest and Horn Cave

- **Optional:** in the **Sakkara Desert**, east of the Dragon God Ruins,
  Paymon camel riders drop the **Red Eagle**. It's very strong for now and
  attacks from the back row.
- **Great Forest:** the right path at the first fork is only a note for
  **PEACE**, so go left. Beat the plants blocking the way and grab the item
  under the second one.
- **Horn Cave:** cross the leaf bridge and go north. Dune and Cid steal the
  **Holy Robe** in the back room. Go back to Oriab.

## Day 3: Ruins of Fahl

- Talk to the doctor at the inn and the harbor, then sail to **Ompross**.
  Nurk in the southwest house takes you to the ruins by boat.
- Left door first for Tasty Water. Up the left stairs, press the button on the
  right to drop the stakes:
    - Left chest: 240 Ragu
    - Right chest: Alarum
    - Center chest: mantra puzzle, **STELLA** (absorbs HP)
- Press the button again to raise the stakes and go up. Drink from the jar on
  the far left.
- Next floor: left stairs for the **Fahl Stone**. Then the right stairs to a
  switch, the middle stairs, and up where the poles were.

### Boss: Ozunne

- **HP:** 1000 · **Weak:** water · **Drops:** Alarum
- Kill his Kukuros first. They drop Alarums too. Surlent casts PECOAMINUNA while Legin and Sork
  attack.

## Day 4: Avdol

Free the children and take the boat to **Avdol**. Watch the eclipse upstairs,
then take the elevator down when you hear the scream. Dr. Muench is dead, and
the Rudra of the Reptiles kills Surlent.

## Day 5: Museum of Magic

- Talk to the man at the table upstairs. Go north through the tunnel, east at
  the split, then south around the bend to the **Museum of Magic**.
- Talk to **Culgan** at the Lago Stone and release the seal on the Rudra of
  the Humans, then on the **Apocalypse Blade**.

## Day 6: Netherworld and Eremia's Manor

- Talk to Gomorrah and go through the hole. **Pick a body.** Leave through the
  teleporter to the south and north through the gate past the lizard town.
- **Sork** rejoins. Go through the volcano and take the boat to Oriab.
- Talk to Legin, Lolo and the doctor. **Legin and Lolo** join.
- Forge: gear for Surlent and a bow for Lolo. **Lolo in the back row, Surlent
  in the front.**
- **Eremia's Manor:** the side door. Three chests, including a **Cosmo Gun**
  for Legin and the **Hector Armor** (fire). Go to the room where the Holy Grail was.

### Boss: Lazm

- **HP:** 1500 · **Drops:** Rem Herb
- Open with strength down and defense down on him, then strength up and
  defense up on you. After that his attacks barely hurt.
- Lazm Thunder hits several times but isn't too bad.

Hausen exposes you to sunlight and you're back in the Netherworld. **Pick
another body** and go to the Museum of Magic.

## Day 7: Teleporters

- **Riza's mother** is the talking stuffed animal in the museum basement.
- Take the warp to Thor Volcano and talk to Solon. Through the right door is a
  teleporter.
- Teleport to the **Ruins of Sodom** and talk to Riza. Keep teleporting to
  **Meifa's Statue** for the **Warp Energy** and **Sleipnir**.
- Back at the volcano, warp to the Underworld, go west and warp to the
  **Vilshin Ruins**. Leave and head east.

## Day 8: Hidden Village and Great Forest

- **Lost Forest:** paths are a clock starting at nine: **west, north, east,
  south.**
- Talk to the sage in the north house and the historians in the southwest
  house.
- The sky falls and opens the **Garal Tunnel**. Take its east exit, then south
  into the Great Forest for the **Peschel Boots**. Go west and over the bridge.

### Boss: Dark Ein

- **HP:** 3000 · **Weak:** holy · **Drops:** Moon Ring
- Spirit down and strength down, then attacks and PECOAHILINA.

Go to Oriab. Castagia gives you the **Stabilizer** for the sandstorm.

Shops worth checking today:

| Where | Gear |
|-------|------|
| Oriab | **Meio Shield** (wind), **Slamath Armor** (lightning) |
| Hidden Village | **Axis Shield** (wind), **Large Shield** (dark), **HeatsealCharm** (dark) |
| Ruined City | **Beze Wing** (Speed +8, wind), **Baker's Cap** (lightning), **Rinon Helm** (fire), **Daniel's Robe** (fire), **Large Robe** (water) |

The **Jaques Mail** (holy) is in the Clock Tower if you go back for it.

## Day 9: Sakkara Desert and Ruins of Ruhle

Legin uses the Stabilizer. North for an Alarum.

### Boss: DesertRose

- **HP:** 2000 · **Weak:** lightning
- Earth mantras that can one-shot. Spirit down on it, then spirit up and magic
  defense up on you. WARHETPRA still does about 100 to everyone.

- Go to the lab in northeast Avdol (zombies again) and check the Lago Stone.
  Avdol sells the **Sultan Helm** (water) and **Daniel's Robe**.
- North around the bend and south into Thor Volcano. Solon says Culgan went
  to the **Ruins of Ruhle**.
- The party splits. Take the **Holy Grail**, then through the clock door for
  the **Rondine** and **Horn Water**. Trap Culgan in the basement.

### Boss: Culgan

- **HP:** 5000 · **Weak:** holy · **Drops:** Moon Ring
- Magic defense down, spirit down on him, spirit up on you. Surlent and Lolo
  cast PECOAHILINA, Sork and Legin attack.
- At half HP he casts SOAREEM and strong wind mantras. Put up **TEONAREEM**.

## Day 10: Sigmund Glacier and Tower of the Giants

- Roma sells the **Aegis Shield** (fire) and **Flat Shield** (lightning).
- Danelf and Babel now sell the **Cassius Mail** (fire), **Lucius Armor**
  (water), **Braham Shield**, **Beluga Shield** and **Zeland Helm**.
- North, west to Roma, then southeast to the Ruins of Sodom. The hole in the
  northeast wall has a teleporter to the **Sigmund Glacier**.
- Take the southeast path, past the chest and through the north door. Check
  the sword to open the bridge. An **Ice Queen** attacks.
- Get the **Horn Water**, go through the door and talk to the **Rudra of the
  Danans**. Through the next door, go **forward one screen and back one**,
  beat the **Ice King** and take the **Ice Sword**.
- Teleport to Mantra Peak. **Zora** lends her boat to Danelf. Go west to Vad
  and south to the Tower of the Giants.
- Tower chests: 800 Ragu, **Rem Herb**, **Alarum**, **Nuad Herb**, 1100 Ragu,
  **Huey Ring** (south door), **Horn Water**. Drink from the jar and save.

### Boss: Abilijer

- **HP:** 4924 · **Drops:** Large Mace
- High magic defense. Use attacks.
- Open with spirit down, defense down, strength down and strength up. Large
  Mace drops to 1 damage. Ogre Revenge does about 90.

Take a shard from the Lago Stone. Drop down the hole below the mantra chest for
1920 Ragu, then cast **PEACE** and leave. Teleport back to the glacier, talk to
the Rudra, then warp from Thor Volcano to the Netherworld.

## Day 11: The Ark

- Take the shard from the Lago Stone in the museum.
- Leave Thor Volcano by the east exit and go south of Avdol to the **vault**.
  **Moon Ring** on the right, the ark piece in the center.
- Let Dune and Cid use the contraption and they give you a ride. Talk to Cid
  in the engine room and fly to **Lyla**.
- Lyla sells the **Alexis Mail** (water), **Aqua Ring** (water) and
  **MontanoShield** (wind).
- Talk to the elder in north Lyla and take the ark to the **Undersea Palace**.
- Right door for 2000 Ragu, an **Alarum** and **Horn Water**. Up the stairs,
  west at the fork to the Rudra of the Mermaids. Sealed chests: **Blue
  Swallow** and **Ophelia**.

### Boss: Kidal

- **HP:** 5000 · **Weak:** fire · **Drops:** Aqua Ring
- The hardest Rudra so far. Open with spirit down, magic defense up, strength
  up and **AQUNAREEM**. Recast AQUNAREEM every few turns, then defense down.
- Surlent, Legin and Sork attack. Lolo heals every turn.

Take a shard from the Lago Stone. Dune drops you near Thor Volcano.

## Day 13: Delphi Mountain

- Follow the black paths in the vault for a **Magic Leaf**, then go to the
  Netherworld. East of the museum, Gomorrah sends you to the **Ruins of
  Tuhla**.
- The bridge is out, so leave Thor Volcano east to reach Oriab. Talk to
  Castagia, then sail to Ompross and go north to **Mount Delphi**.

## Day 14: Ruins of Tuhla

Party is around level 32. Climb the mountain and search the closed door at the
top of the ruins.

### Boss: Gandharva

- **HP:** 5500 · **Weak:** wind · **Drops:** Flight Stone
- Strong lightning mantras. Open with spirit down, magic defense down, spirit
  up, defense down and **TOUNAREEM**.
- Surlent and Lolo cast PECOAJINNA, Sork and Legin attack.

### Warp Gates

| Gate area | Exits |
|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Center: entrance · Left: area 2 · Right: area 3 |
| 2 | Right: area 1 · Bottom: **Light Sword** · Left: area 3 |
| 3 | Upper left: area 2 · **Upper right: Lezastes** · Bottom left: Rem Herb · Bottom right: area 1 |

### Boss: Lezastes

- **HP:** 4343 · **Weak:** water, but his magic defense is very high
- **Drops:** Life Leaf
- Spirit down, strength down, defense down, strength up, then attack. Lolo
  heals.

### Switch Puzzle

You can step on three switches before it resets. Go down two and left one to
save. Cross the switches for the chests, then the bottom chest's switch, and
around through the door.

The **Fool Shoes** (Speed +4, dark) are in a hidden chest near the exit,
tucked behind a wall.

### Boss: Gaya

- **HP:** 4500
- Weak to physical attacks. Spirit down, defense down, strength up, magic
  defense up, then attack.

Take the far right stairs and keep going.

### Boss: Dampti

- **HP:** 5424 · No weakness
- Some attacks can one-shot. Spirit down, magic defense down, spirit up,
  strength down.
- Sork and Legin attack, Lolo heals, Surlent alternates attacks and healing.

Down the stairs, through the south door, and insert the Humans' Lago Stone
shard.

### Boss: Saizou

- **HP:** 8309 · **Drops:** Life Leaf
- No mantra weakness. Strength down, spirit down, defense down, strength up,
  then attack. Recast them when they wear off.

Go north over the roots to **Tarach**. Buy a **Thunder Sword** for Sork. The
shop also has the **Marcus Armor** and **Octave Mail** (dark), **Hecate's
Robe** (water) and **Heavy Shield** (wind). **BoneKnights** in the
Netherworld drop the **Black Shield**. Then go north into **Shumisen**.

## Day 15: Shumisen

Party is around level 41.

- **Baalzephon** drop the **Thor Ring** (lightning, Spirit +4).
- Follow the Rudra into the right tower. **Cassius Mail** and **Daniel's
  Robe**, then the large door.
- Through the teleporter for the sealed chests, back by the left steps, then
  up the right steps.

### Final Boss: Hausen

- **HP:** 13000 · **Weak:** lightning
- **Wear wind defense on everyone.** Open with spirit down, TEONAREEM,
  defense down and strength up.
- Surlent, Legin and Sork attack, Lolo heals. Most of his attacks then do
  30–50. KAAKEPIBUL is the one to watch.
