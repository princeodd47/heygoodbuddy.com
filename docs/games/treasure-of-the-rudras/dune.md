# Dune

The final chapter opens after you finish Sion's, Surlent's and Riza's
stories. Dune leads, with Sion, Surlent and Riza.

Mantras below are from [New Playthrough](new-playthrough.md) where one fits:

| Effect | Mantra |
|--------|--------|
| Strength down | PECOAGODWEAK |
| Defense down | PECOAGODSOFT |
| Spirit down | KIPECOAVIRUS |
| Magic defense down | PECOAGODZERO |
| Strength up | PECOAPOWERUP |
| Defense up | PECOAGAIA |
| Spirit up | PECOASUNA |
| Magic defense up | COABARRIERNA |
| Fire, one | NANIKIKUEBIT |
| Water, one | KIKUEGODMINU |
| Lightning, one | NANIKUEGODUS |
| Wind, one | NANIKIKUEJIN |
| Dark, one | NANIKIKUEGOD |

Elemental barriers are an element word + NAREEM, like IGNAREEM for fire.

Most fights here go the same way: open with the debuffs and buffs listed,
then three characters attack while **Riza heals every turn**, even at full HP.

## Day 16: To the Moon

- If you finished Sion's or Surlent's story last, go southeast of town to the
  **Deep Sea Trench**, board the airship, say goodbye and fly to the moon.
- Step outside the airship and **Riza** joins. Go back in and take off.
- Brasnir's mind gets infected. Leave the room, up the nearest ladder, down
  the next, and search the beam to raise a bridge.

### Boss: Brasnir

- **HP:** 6000 · **Weak:** water · **Drops:** Amrita
- Strength up, defense down, spirit down, magic defense up.
- Under half HP: MACROPOTEY about 140 to everyone, KIDEREMNU about 160 to
  everyone, KIDEOHPIPER about 210 to one, even with debuffs.

Ramyleth takes the controls and flies you to **Urd's Gate**.

## Urd's Gate

Party is around level 45–50.

Eight black chests sit in the holes: **Daniel's Robe**, **Meio Shield**,
**Octave Mail**, **Peschel Boots**, **Dark Bow**, **Death Falcon**, **Hecate's
Robe**, **Flat Shield**. Four holes have a boss:

| Hole | Boss | HP | Weak | Drops | Fight |
|------|------|---:|------|-------|-------|
| Northwest | Setaviza | 3700 | Fire | Purifier | Spirit down, magic defense down, spirit up, magic defense up, then fire mantras. IGBIRD is cheap and hits about 660. |
| Southeast | Lazm | 4000 | None | Nuad Herb | Strength down, strength up, defense up, spirit down, then attack. |
| Northeast | Abilijer | 4000 | Fire | | Same as Lazm, or use fire mantras. |
| Southwest | Ravana | 4000 | Water | Orpheus Robe | Spirit down, spirit up, IGNAREEM, magic defense down, then water mantras. |

The door at the back center opens once all four are down.

### Boss: Hausen

- **HP:** 8500 · **Weak:** wind · **Drops:** Magic Leaf
- Spirit down, magic defense down, spirit up, and magic defense up or
  SERENAREEM. Then wind mantras with everyone.

Take the ark to **Skuld's Gate**.

## Skuld's Gate

- Go down the steps and through the door, then try to leave. You end up in a
  secret area where **every chest is a Mimic (MimicL56)**. The treasure
  respawns, but once you start the fight you have to beat them all to get
  back. **Each Mimic always drops a Magic Leaf** (max MP +10), so this is the
  place to get everyone's MP up to the 255 cap.
- Keep going through doors and **avoid the grates**. The monsters in them
  aren't worth it.
- **Capulet** drop Amrita, and **Nataraja** drop Horn Water.
- The third fixed enemy, a **TrainDragn**, drops the **Freedom**, a dagger
  strong enough to make two attackers worthwhile against Mitra.
- A room with a recovery jar and save point comes before the boss.

### Boss: Saizou

- **HP:** 10500 · **Drops:** Life Leaf
- No weakness. Spirit down or defense up, defense down, strength down,
  strength up. Recast them every five or six turns.

Saizou tells you Mitra is in the temple at Verdandi and is weak to wind.

## Verdandi's Temple

Party is around level 50–56.

- Go to the statue outside the ark, watch the scene, then find and examine
  the statue to warp to the entrance.

| Door | Contents |
|------|----------|
| Southwest | **Mirror Shield** |
| Southeast | **Pure Ring** |
| Center | Recovery jar and save statue |
| Northeast | **Keats Helm** |
| Northwest | **Zeland Helm** |

- **Odio** drop Medical Herbs and **Fulezast** drop Nuad Herbs.
- **Level here if you need to.** Ozric gives more EXP than Saizou and usually
  comes alone. Defense down, strength up and defense up, then attack, with the
  jar nearby.
- Go through the north center door.

### Boss: Chaos Ein

- **HP:** 10000 · **Weak:** dark · **Drops:** Pure Ring
- **Wear light defense.** Strength down, spirit down, spirit up, magic
  defense down, and SOANAREEM or magic defense up.
- Dark mantras until MP runs out, then attack.

Grab the two chests on either side of the statue, then go to the statue.

### Boss: Chaos Zwei

- **HP:** 6560 · **Weak:** wind · **Drops:** Nuad Herb
- Spirit down, magic defense down, spirit up, and strength down or magic
  defense up. Wind mantras from Sion, Dune and Surlent hit for 650–700.

Examine the statue when you're ready for the last fight.

## Final Chamber

Party is around level 65.

### Mitra

- **HP:** 11500 · **Weak:** lightning
- Spirit down, magic defense down, magic defense up, spirit up.
- Sion, Dune and Surlent cast lightning. KIFULLTOUREX (12 MP) hits about
  850. Riza heals with KAAYOULEFNAT (7 MP, about 140).

### Mitra, Second Form

- **HP:** 28000 · **Weak:** changes
- Strength down twice, spirit up, defense up, magic defense down.
- Her weakness changes, usually right after you hit it. Keep trying elements
  to find the new one.
- When Sion and Dune are out of MP and the buffs wear off: strength down
  twice, strength up, defense up, defense down. Then attack while Riza heals.

### Mitra, Final Form

- **HP:** 25000 · **Weak:** changes
- Open with defense down and strength up, then attack with everyone.
- Once she starts using Attack, switch to mantras: defense up, strength down
  twice, spirit up, magic defense down.
- She changes back and forth again around half HP, and after she uses Death.
