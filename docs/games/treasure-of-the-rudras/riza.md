# Riza

Riza is a young priestess from the village of Karn. Her party is Garlyle,
Pipin and later Marina.

Mantras below are from [New Playthrough](new-playthrough.md) where one fits:

| Effect | Mantra |
|--------|--------|
| Strength down | PECOAGODWEAK |
| Defense down | PECOAGODSOFT |
| Spirit down | KIPECOAVIRUS |
| Magic defense down | PECOAGODZERO |
| Strength up | PECOAPOWERUP |
| Defense up | PECOAGAIA |
| Spirit up | PECOASUNA |
| Magic defense up | COABARRIERNA |

Elemental barriers are an element word + NAREEM, like IGNAREEM for fire. They
halve that element's damage for a few turns.

Armor with an element halves damage of that element and doubles damage of the
opposite one, so the gear notes under each boss list what to wear and what to
take off. They only include gear you can have by then. **Buy** lines at each
town list what's worth buying there and which fight it's for. See
[Equipment](equipment.md) for stats.

## Day 0: Karn and the Forest of Rem

- After talking to Zeku, the northwest house has a **Purifier** and **Green
  Bag** in drawers. The pub teaches **RUMEHATI**, which cures all ailments.
- **Forest of Rem**, south of town:
    - Cross the bridge for a **Rem Herb** in the chest poking out of the trees
      to the north.
    - Tell the Guard the person went right. Down the middle and right for
      another Rem Herb.
    - South, fight Guards with **Garlyle**, who joins. Guards drop Medical
      Herbs.
    - East, the top path is only a mantra puzzle: **PULMORA**, absorbs MP.
    - Take the east path, 260 Ragu at the exit, then on to Babel.

## Day 1: Pure City Babel

- Guards attack as you enter. Garlyle takes you to the inn.
- Garlyle is in the slums, east of the inn past the guard at the post. There's
  a **Dirty Hat** in the pile of bowls in the middle.
- The three kids want passwords:

| Kid | Question | Answer |
|-----|----------|--------|
| 1 | In the midst of the light? | They will dance. |
| 2 | In the deepest recesses of the shadow? | It will be born. |
| 3 | The blue water? | They will protect. |

- Follow them to the hideout basement, then back to the inn to talk to
  Garlyle and rest.

## Day 2: Mayor's House

Climb the ladder in the northeast part of town. The mansion has six doors:

| Door | Contents |
|------|----------|
| 1 (top left) | Alarum |
| 2 (top middle) | Switch, pull it |
| 3 (top right) | Tasty Water |
| 4 (bottom left) | Purifier |
| 5 (bottom middle) | 500 Ragu |
| 6 (bottom right) | Way down into the house |

- Down the ladder in door 6. Right path for **Dirty Shoes** in the bowls, then
  left. Up the ladder and down the one on the roof, and search the bowls for a
  **Regulus**.
- **Equip the Regulus on Riza and move both of them to the back row.**
- Follow the path to the elevator. Drink from the jar and save on the way up.

### Boss: Shivaithan

- **HP:** 800
- **Gear:** nothing elemental matters. The back row does the work.
- No weakness. The back row makes this easy: attack, and heal with Riza.
  JESS hits hard.

Visit **Zeku** in Karn, who teaches **ANIMUS** (revive), then take the ship
from Babel to Oriab.

## Day 3: Oriab, Great Forest and Horn Cave

- **Buy in Oriab:** two **Lemnos Mail** and two **Logical Caps**. Keep the
  back-row weapons. Take the Lemnos Mail off for Soma on Day 6.
- The pub teaches **SOAMEL**. The northwest house facing out has a
  **Purifier**.
- **Great Forest:** the right path at the first fork is only a note for
  **PEACE**, so go left. Before the bridge, go south past a plant for a sealed
  chest.

### Boss: Setaviza

- **HP:** 600, plus 300 for the reptile · **Weak:** fire
- **Wear:** the **Moon Ring** (dark) from Karn. Dark gear blocks Pollution.
- Kill the reptile it's holding first.
- Polluted Mushroom stops you casting mantras. **Bring Purifiers.**

- **Pipin** the Reptile Prince joins.
- East around the bend, get the **Tasty Water** before fighting the plant.
  Cross the bridge for 700 Ragu, east past another plant for a **Purifier**,
  then north to the **Horn Cave**.
- **Horn Cave:** down the stairs, south door at the split for two **Horn
  Water**, then north over the bridge. Read the book.
- Go back to Oriab, then ship to Ompross and on to Danelf.

## Day 4: Danelf, Vad and Cryunne Castle

- **Buy in Danelf:** the **Hector Armor** (fire, Def 15) for Karla on Day 6.
  Skip the Slamath Armor (lightning), which doubles Soma's wind.
- The pub teaches AQUNA, IGNA, TOUNA, TEONA and YOUTEONA.
- Northeast house facing east: **Dirty Shoes** in a drawer.
- Go to the Coliseum, back into town, then back to the Coliseum. The king runs
  out.
- Southeast to the church. Check the goddess statue and **Miller** talks to
  you.
- West to **Vad**. Doug is in Sion's house, in the center facing out, and
  there's a **Logical Cap** in the drawer. The pub teaches the LUS mantras.
- **Cryunne Castle:** west, upstairs, west, downstairs, then down the middle
  to the prison. The sealed chest has the **Tybalt Mail**, very good for now.
- **Buy in Cryunne:** a **Meio Shield** (wind) for Soma. Take it off for
  Brusty and Karla.
- Talk to the king, take the 100 Ragu and rest.

## Day 5: Spirit Temple and Sacred Forest

- South of Danelf, answer **No**, then **To go to the mountain**.
- In the temple, pick **Water** or **Earth**:
    - **Water:** **Antares** rifle for Garlyle and a Horn Water.
    - **Earth:** Life Leaf and **Jackal Nail** for Pipin. The shop below sells
      a nail just as good.
- The guard in the lower left shows the way. Talk to **Soma** and rest at the
  inn on the left.
- **Buy from the merchant:** a **Bison Claw** (lightning) for Pipin. It hits
  Soma's weakness.
- Drop down the hole and go south.
- **Sacred Forest:**
    - Right side: **Open Helm**.
    - Behind the tree on the left: **Purifier**.
    - South, right at the split: **DOA** machine gun for Garlyle. It hits
      all enemies. Keep the rifle for bosses.
    - Through the big bushes: **Horn Water**.
    - Left at the split to the temple. Talk to **Aqua**.
- **Equip the Antares before leaving Aqua.** Demons attack on the way back.

### Boss: Evil Ghost

- **HP:** 2000 · **Weak:** holy
- **Wear:** the **Moon Ring** (dark) halves his dark mantras.
- **Weapons:** don't use the **DOA**. It's dark, so he takes half.
- All dark mantras, mostly on everyone. Heal with LEFNA every turn, attack
  with Pipin and Garlyle, and magic defense up helps.

## Day 6: Guardians, Mantra Peak and the Netherworld

Aqua didn't give permission, so you fight the guardians. Rest first.

1. Earth Guardian
2. Water Guardian
3. Three Fire Guardians
4. Soma

### Boss: Soma

- **HP:** 1670 · **Weak:** lightning · **Drops:** Soma (boots: Speed +4,
  wind)
- **Wear:** wind gear: **Ephta's Robe**, Logical Cap, **Meio Shield**. Or no elements at all.
- **Avoid:** lightning gear: Lemnos Mail, Slamath Armor.
- **Weapons:** Pipin's **Bison** (lightning) hits his weakness.
- Mantras don't do much. Attack and heal with Riza.
- Under half HP he uses SHIITAERI and Power Wave, about 120 to everyone.
  **Level up before this fight.**

- Go south through the Sacred Forest to the Ark. Dune and Cid give you the
  **Holy Grail** and **Holy Robe**. Heal in the next room. The Ark moves.
- **Mantra Peak:**
    - **Horn Water** at the start, **Green Bag** under the bridge.
    - Once it's dark, walk left into the waterfall to slide down to a
      **level 17 Mimic**, about 1000 HP, for a Horn Water.
    - Talk to **Zora** through the middle door: first option, Aqua, then
      Nothing.
    - Behind the waterfall, the mantra mistresses teach a mantra and give you
      the **Pure Ring**.
    - Talk to Zora again to go down to the Netherworld.
- **Limestone Cavern:**
    - Talk to the Cultist at the front of the line, then go back to the end.
      Beat the Cultists after the child scene and follow one down the river.
    - Talk to **Marina**. When the fake mother tells you to get up, **don't**.
      Wait for your real mother, who gives you a bow. Getting up starts a
      Ghost fight.
    - **Marina** joins. Drink from the jar, then go to the next cave.

### Boss: Brusty

- **HP:** 2428 · **Weak:** wind · **Drops:** Horn Water
- **Avoid:** wind gear (Soma boots, Ephta's Robe, Logical Cap, Meio Shield). It stops Float, and Float dodges PRATAHNA. Cast **FLOAT**.
- PRATAHNA does 100–120 to everyone, sometimes every turn. He's slow, so keep
  healing and use PECOAJINNA.

Take the **HolyTree Seed**.

### Boss: Karla

- **HP:** 2000 · **Drops:** Moon Ring
- **Wear:** fire gear like the **Hector Armor** halves her fire mantras.
- **Avoid:** wind gear, so Float can still dodge her earth mantras.
- Earth and fire mantras. Weak to physical attacks. Keep HP above 80.

Place the **Holy Grail** on the circle.

## Day 7: Ruins of Ruhle and Sodom

- **Ruins of Ruhle:** down the stairs past Surlent, top stairs at the split.
  Mantra puzzle: **VIRUS**. Next room: **Rem Herb** and **Horn Water**.
- North, plant the seed in the northwest part of the cleared pollution.
- Southwest to the prison. Rest in the south room, take the nearby steps to
  the back prison, search the gourds for a **Purifier**, and the southeast
  skulls for a child who opens the **Moon Ring** door.
- **Ruins of Sodom:** go through the hole on the left of the house and talk
  to Surlent. Now the doors open:
    - Bottom right door: 260 Ragu.
    - Up and outside to the bed with the mouse, down the steps for 240 Ragu.
    - Left path from the mouse: read the **Biotank manual**, then down the
      ladder for the **King Bear**.

## Day 8: Gomorrah City

The sky islands fall. Marina leaves the egg in the clean water at Roma.

- **Buy in Roma:** a **Flat Shield** (lightning) and an **Aegis Shield**
  (fire). Laumen casts lightning and fire. Take the Aegis Shield off for
  Abrasax on Day 10.
- Go southeast to the new land and **Gomorrah City**.

- Southwest bowls: **Horn Water** and **Purifier**. East of that doorway, a
  chest with a strength-down mantra puzzle.
- Southeast bowls: **Spirits**. Down the ladder in that house: **Purifier**.
- Northeast house: down the ladder, west along the wall for the
  **Thunder'sTail**. Pull the switch down the second ladder, then the one
  south around the wall to take the elevator.
- Beat the Mad Players. Pipin and Garlyle vanish.
- Talk to Pipin: **first answer every time.**
- Talk to Garlyle: **Help the mermaid**, **No, I can't get married**, **I
  protect people**, **I love her**.
- Heal at the upper right monitor, then pick a character at the upper left.

### Boss: Laumen

- **HP:** 5000
- **Avoid:** wind and water gear: Ephta's Robe, Logical Cap, Meio Shield, Soma boots.
- No weakness, lots of mantras. **No wind- or water-defense gear.**
- Open with spirit down, **TOUNAREEM**, **IGNAREEM**, spirit up, then
  strength up and defense down.
- After he uses Scan, a strong single-target mantra hits that character next.

- Take the **Mother Energy**. A new dog outside gives you a **Harvest Seed**.
- Back at Sodom, upstairs through the hole on the left, left path, put the
  seed in the jar. Go up past the bookcase and read the book.

### Five MahogChairs

- **HP:** 600 each
- **Wait for the one in the middle to use Deluge, and attack that one.** The
  others can't die until it does.

### Boss: KingEltina

- **HP:** 3118 · **Weak:** holy · **Drops:** Purifier
- **Wear:** the **Moon Ring** (dark).
- **Avoid:** the **Pure Ring** (holy).
- **Weapons:** not the DOA (dark).
- Dark attacks. Spirit down, **SERENAREEM** and spirit up, then PECOAHILINA
  and attacks.

Drop down the hole for the **Key to Ruins** and go back to Gomorrah City.

## Day 9: Biotanks

- Southwest house, down the ladder and elevator, then open the locked door.
- Set the knobs for peace:

| Machine | Turn |
|---------|------|
| Moon | Left |
| Planet | Right |
| Sun | Left |

- Turning the Moon right is a game over.
- Take the right path through the opened hole to the pyramid.
- Go back to the **Holy Tree** first. Take the five **Harvest Seeds** around
  it, and it heals you from now on.

## Day 10: Undersea Pyramid

Go through the center. Marina is fighting alone.

### Boss: Abrasax

- **HP:** 4387 · **Weak:** fire · **Drops:** Purifier
- **Avoid:** fire gear doubles his water mantras: Hector Armor, Aegis Shield.
- **Weapons:** Garlyle's **Antares** (fire). Not Pipin's Jackal Nail (water).
- Water mantras. Riza, Garlyle and Pipin cast PECOABITNA until out of MP.
- Stab-Throw is nearly always lethal.

- Press the button to free the Hegs. Left around the wall and up for a jar.
  North from the split, up and press the button.
- Downstairs, left past the stairs to a button, then down for the **Mist
  Gun**. Save past the boss, then go back to it.
- **Hidden room:** two floors down from the entrance, stand in front of the
  third floodgate switch and walk into the wall, third tile from the right.
  It has the **Catarina** (lightning spear). The **Hecate's Robe** (water) is
  hidden in a similar spot in this dungeon.

### Boss: Danapati

- **HP:** 4437 · No weakness
- **Wear:** the **Hecate's Robe** (water, MDef 33), if you found it here, halves his water attacks.
- **Avoid:** fire gear: Hector Armor, Aegis Shield.
- Very easy. Normal attacks do about 120.

- Ride the Hegs to **Lyla**. The elder is straight north. The southeast pub
  teaches **AQUTEUM**.
- **Buy in Lyla:** a new spear for Marina, and the **Aqua Ring** and **Alexis
  Mail** (water) for Sodom. Skip the MontanoShield (wind).
- **Cyprus:** now that you can ride the Hegs, a chief appears in the Sacred
  Forest and drops this dark spear, Marina's best weapon. Kill the chief
  first.
- The Hegs on the south side take you to the Vilshin Ruins.

## Day 11: Hidden Village and Vilshin Ruins

- **Lost Forest:** paths are a clock starting at nine: **west, north, east,
  south.**
- At the elder's house, Sion trades the **Heg's Ocarina** for the **Revive
  Herb**.
- The sages in the southwest house give you the **Vilshan Key**.
- **Buy in Len:** the **Lucius Armor** (water, Def 35) for Sodom.
- **Buy in Babel:** the **Beluga Shield** (water) for Sodom. Skip the Cassius
  Mail and Braham Shield (fire), which double his attacks.
- Skip the Hidden Village's **Large Shield** and **HeatsealCharm** (dark).
  Meifa's attacks do double through them.

- **Vilshin Ruins**, northwest: the building east of the first has a **Nuad
  Herb** and a monitor. Then down the first building's steps to the Heg
  Shrine.
- South around the bend to the looping water. A hole near the top leads to
  Mantra Peak. Zora tells you to go back to Meifa.
- From that cave go east along the south edge to the shrine near Karn, and
  take the boat in the southwest corner to the statue.

## Day 12: Statue of Meifa

Party is around level 38.

- **4F:** mantra puzzle: **PIKUGAZERO**, magic defense down. You'll want it
  for Meifa.
- **5F:** right door, south, through another door for a **Nuad Herb**. Then
  the northwest door, all the way left and down for the **Dusk 909** and
  **Queen's Coat**. Back up, drink from the jar, left past the stairs to save.

### Boss: Meifa

- **HP:** 6500 · **Weak:** dark
- **Wear:** the **Pure Ring** (holy).
- **Avoid:** dark gear: Moon Ring, **Large Shield**, HeatsealCharm.
- **Weapons:** **DOA** (dark), and Marina's **Cyprus** (dark) if you got it.
- Spirit down, magic defense up, **LUNA** and magic defense down, then
  PECOAMUNYNA. Magic defense down makes her go fast.

Meifa takes you to the moon. It's Day 15 when you land.

## Day 15: Crater Base

Party is around level 40.

- Downstairs, left, elevator to the entrance, out through the center.
- Before the bridge, right path north for the **Zeland Helmet**, then press
  the south switch. Left side for the **Amrita**, and press that south switch.
- Cross the middle bridge:
    1. Press the button twice for the **Mirage**.
    2. Press it again to reach the third button and press that.
    3. Turn the first two off, cross back and press the button.
- **Bifron** (1500 HP) fights you. Press the switch behind it.
- Press buttons 1 and 2 again, go back down the bridge, restore and save.
  The last switch opens **Sodom's Chambers**.

### Final Boss: Sodom

- **HP:** 16000 · **Weak:** fire
- **Wear:** water gear: **Lucius Armor** (Def 35) or Alexis Mail, **Hecate's Robe** (MDef 33), **Beluga Shield**, **Aqua Ring**.
- **Avoid:** fire gear: Cassius Mail, Braham Shield, Hector Armor, Aegis Shield.
- **Weapons:** Garlyle's **Antares** (fire). Not the Mist Gun or Jackal Nail (water).
- Every hit does 150–170. Keep HP above 150.
- Open with spirit down, magic defense down, defense up, magic defense up and
  strength down. That cuts his damage by about 40%, but it wears off.
- Use fire mantras and heal. Water-defense gear helps.
