# Sion

Sion is a knight of Cryunne Castle. His party is Foxy, Ture and later
Ramyleth.

Mantras below are from [New Playthrough](new-playthrough.md) where one fits:
fire PECOABITNA, water PECOAMINUNA, lightning PECOAUSNA, wind PECOAJINNA,
holy PECOAHILINA, dark PECOAMUNYNA.

## Day 0: Cryunne Castle

- Meet Foxy and Doug in the reception room, two rooms left on the second
  floor.
- Go to the prison in the western basement, then back to the entrance. The
  guard sends you to the royal chamber for the Tower of Valiant briefing.
- The Cultist escapes. Leave through the door south of the prison stairs, go
  through Vad and south to the Tower of the Giants.

## Day 1: Tower of the Giants

- At home in Vad, search the desk left of the stairs for a **Logical Cap**.
- In the tower:
    - **1F:** two chests, 30 Ragu and a Medical Herb.
    - **2F:** take the left stairs for an Alarum. The right stairs drop you
      through the floor.
    - **3F:** reach the chest by going up to 4F and back down the other
      stairs.
    - **5F:** two chests north of the hole, then the south door for the
      **Huey Ring**.
    - **6F:** Tasty Water.
    - **7F:** take the far right stairs. Search the bottle to restore HP and
      MP, and talk to the statue to save.
- Search the statue of Abilijer on the roof. Ture joins.

### Boss: Abilijer

- **HP:** 700
- **Drops:** Ogre Mace
- When he swings his club and talks about waking from his slumber, Upper
  Attack is next turn. **Defend with everyone.**
- He turns darker at half HP.
- Physical attacks are weak against him. Use fire mantras with everyone, and
  switch Sion to attacks when his MP runs out.

After the fight, get the Strength Plus to the south. Walk up to the switch on
the wall and Ture boosts you up. Take the stairs it opens, drop down the holes
for 120 Ragu, and head back through Vad to the castle.

## Day 2: Danelf and Mantra Peak

- The **barkeep in Vad** teaches mantras and how they're built. You can only
  inscribe 32, so keep a spread of elements.
- Put the Big Robe and Ogre Mace on Ture. Buy a **Buckler** for Sion for
  later.
- Go east and north over the bridges to **Danelf**:
    - Forge: an **Avenger Sword**, then a Gra Shield for Sion or a Rune Knife
      for Foxy.
    - Lamias in the grass, not the forest, are good for Ragu. AxisKnights in
      the forest drop **Axis Shields**, better than the Gra Shield.

### Coliseum

Talk to the women at the back, then take the door on the right.

| Match | Fighter | Enemy | HP | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|---:|-------|
| 1 | Foxy or Ture | Nergal | 150 | Attack. Watch for Energy Sword. |
| 2 | Sion | NergalMstr | 350 | Only Thunder Sword hurts. Heal if needed. |

You win the **Storm** sword and 500 Ragu. Sell it, buy armor for Foxy and a
helmet for Sion, and give Sion Foxy's old armor for the speed.

### Mantra Peak

- Take the boat from the east side of Danelf.
- Alarum at the start, Tasty Water further up.
- At the fork, press the button down the left path, then the button inside
  the chest on the right path. Go back left through the new door.
- At the top, **Zora** tells you to find Nurk the merchant in Ompross.

## Day 3: Ompross and Mount Delphi

### Coliseum: Amatista x2

- **HP:** 600 each
- Choose **magic**, so you can heal. They're weak to **wind**: PECOAJINNA.
- They cast lightning, so don't wear wind defense.
- Reward: 1000 Ragu and a **Magic Leaf**.

### Ompross and the Tuhla Ruins

- Take the ship from the Danelf harbor waiting room. In Ompross, cross to the
  southwest bazaar and talk to **Nurk** by the water. Surlent is here too.
- North to **Mount Delphi**:
    - Follow the water west, then south through the hole for 120 Ragu.
    - North for Tasty Water, northeast, then up the north path for a
      **Spirit Plus**.
    - Outside, cross west. South has an Alarum, north is the way on.
- In the **Tuhla Ruins**, the floating heads resist attacks and cast fire, so
  hit them with water. Rescue **Ramyleth**, who teaches **ANIMUS** (revive).
- Go back to Danelf.

## Day 4: Tower of Valiant

### Boss: Monk

- **HP:** 1000, one-on-one with Sion
- Elemental mantras do 1 damage. Use physical attacks.
- Use the **Magic Leaf** first so Sion has MP to heal. Keep HP above half.
- Once he talks about flaming fists he adds Screw Fist and uses Middle Kick
  more.

At the top an eclipse starts the 12-day countdown, and you warp to the **Sky
Islands**.

- Right of the castle, walk up the waterfall path and push into the wall for
  a **Moon Ring**.
- Town weapon shop: **Flat Shield** for Sion, **Laikas Knife** for Foxy, and
  Packet Mail if you can. The **Meio Shield** (wind) is sold here too.
- **Beze Wing:** the Bezewaign in the forest around Len sometimes drop this
  accessory: Speed +8, MDef 6, wind element. They run away once they're hurt,
  so kill them in one hit. It also goes on sale in the Ruined City on Day 10.
- Sigmund Glacier is blocked by ice for now. Go back to town.

## Day 5: Garal Tunnel and Clock Tower

Talk to Surt in the throne room, then take the train from the station near the
castle entrance.

### Boss: Bhadra

- **HP:** 1500 · **Weak:** lightning
- Use PECOAUSNA. At half HP he starts Bhadra Bound, about 70 to everyone.

After the fight a man on the train teaches **TOME**, much stronger than LUS.

- **Garal Tunnel:** east hole for a **Speed Plus**, west hole for **Garal
  Armor** (Sion).
- Rest in the Ark's healing chamber, then enter the **Clock Tower**.
- Enemies here are lightning-based. **Take off wind-element armor.**
- Treasure: 650 Ragu (left door near the entrance), **Power Mace**, **Life
  Leaf**, **Magic Leaf**, **Jaques Mail** (up the ladders past the north
  door). Drink from the jar at the far south, then save at the statue.

### Boss: Monju

- **HP:** 2000 · **Weak:** wind
- Almost everything he casts is lightning. Low on HP he uses SUUMEDACO, which
  hurts unless you have lightning defense.

## Day 6: Graham's Tomb

- Take the train to the **Ruined City**. Buy a Flat Shield for fire defense
  and give Foxy the **Huey Ring**. The **Aegis Shield** (fire) and **Jaques
  Mail** (holy) are sold here too.
- In the tomb, go to the far right tunnel. The others are dead ends.
- Get the **Hamlet** to the right of the stairs (Sion). If you get **Kemp
  Boots**, give them to Foxy and the ring to Ture.
- **Run from Kir.** They're very hard.
- **Kemp** drop the Kemp Boots: Speed +8, fire element.
- The **Ogre Shield** (fire) is in a chest here too.
- Go west, follow the stairs to the save statue, press the southwest switch,
  drop down the new hole and drink from the jar. Press the button, go down,
  then press the next button to reach a sealed chest with a shield for Ture.
- Before the big door, the room to the right has the **Aegis Shield** and
  **Gregory Armor**.

### Boss: Marica

- **HP:** 2700 · **Weak:** water
- Rex Spin does about 100. Use PECOAMINUNA.

The **Graham Sword** is upstairs, but it's only 4 attack over the Hamlet. Keep
the Hamlet with the Aegis Shield.

## Day 7: Ruins of Frey and the Sacred Temple

- Train to **Roma**. Buy two **MontanoShields**, a **Raptor Knife** and a
  **Sultan Helm**.
- **Ruins of Frey:** left stairs, south around the room, west to the next
  stairs, drink from the jar, drop down the hole and save.

### Boss: Opsis

- **HP:** 2800 · **Weak:** fire
- Mostly weak water attacks. Use PECOABITNA.

Grab the two chests he drops and take the train to the temple. The **Opsis
Boots** (Speed +4, water) are in the Ruins of Frey.

- **Sacred Temple:** right path, up two sets of stairs, east to the switch.
  It drops you to sealed chests: the **southwest and northeast are MimicL26**.
  The others are a **Large Robe** and **Large Mace** for Ture.
- Upstairs, press the left switch to drop a jar, drink from it on the right,
  and save at the green statue.

### Boss: Mayura

- **HP:** 4600 · **Weak:** dark
- Uses light attacks. Use PECOAMUNYNA.
- After about 2000 damage the background changes and she starts Star Thrust,
  about 125 to everyone.

Step on the platform and leave as the islands fall. You end up in Len.

## Day 8: Len

Rest at the inn, then go to the palace. Len now sells the **Lucius Armor**
(water) and **MontanoShield** (wind).

### Boss: Surt

- **HP:** 3357 · **Weak:** water
- **Drops:** Large Shield (Ture), sometimes
- Watch out for Pile Blaster.

## Day 9: Sigmund Glacier

- The cave is open now. Take the right cave to the fake sword and beat the
  bear. Go back one screen and the bridge appears.
- Get the chest, save north, drink from the jar on the left and read the
  sign: **go forward two screens, then back one.**
- Follow the bridge south, then all the way west.

### Boss: Diyaus

- **HP:** 3926 · **Weak:** lightning · **Drops:** Amrita
- Use PECOAUSNA, and **don't use wind**. Air Bomber and PRIONA hit hard, so
  keep HP up.

Pull out the **Sigmund Sword** and you fall into the water and get swallowed.

- Go through the opening and closing door for a chest, then up the ladder on
  the right for another.
- Through the door right of the ladder, west along the vines for a chest,
  then south and left through the door. The merperson tells you how to reach
  the Heg.
- Back in the main room, take the right vines south and through the tangle to
  the door.

### Boss: Yol-Gee

- **HP:** 4200 · **Weak:** fire
- **Take off the Kemp Boots and Huey Ring**, or his water attacks hurt a lot
  more. Use PECOABITNA.

## Day 10: Avdol and Oriab

- You're in **Avdol**. The zombies at the port start fights if you talk to
  them. The inn is free.
- Go south across the desert, through the Dragon God Ruins and west to
  **Eremia's Manor**. Foxy takes you in the back.
- Chests: **Hector Armor**, **Purifier**, **Cosmo Gun**. Upstairs, search the
  table for a letter for Foxy.
- Go to **Oriab**. Eremia is in the northwest house with the side door.
  Search the drawer behind him for a **Purifier**.
- The pub teaches **SOANATES**. Check the forge, rest, then go back to Avdol.
- Follow the Cultists down the lab elevator in northeast Avdol.

### Boss: Nagiya

- **HP:** 4200 · **Weak:** holy
- He opens with Foxy Killer, which kills Foxy.
- Uses dark attacks. He's slow, so use PECOAHILINA and heal between turns.

- Search the northwest house in Avdol for a **Magic Leaf**.
- **Optional:** take the train back to the **Ruined City**. Its shop now sells
  the **Beze Wing**, **Baker's Cap** (lightning, Spirit +8), **Rinon Helm**
  (fire) and **Large Robe** (water).
- Go to the Heg Shrine in southwest Avdol. The Heg takes you to the Vilshin
  Ruins, then head to the **Lost Forest**.

## Day 11: Hidden Village

- The man in the forest says begin at nine, end at six. The paths are a clock:
  **west, north, east, south.**
- **Riza** is with the sage in the northwest basement and gives you the
  **Revive Herb** for Foxy.
- Forge: **Ice Whip** and **HeatsealCharm** (dark). The **Axis Shield** and
  **Large Shield** are sold here too. Talk to the three sages in the
  southernmost house.
- Boat down the rapids, through the Great Forest to **Oriab**. The bulletin
  says to meet Miller the priest.
- Ship to Ompross, then Danelf, then southeast to the church. Search the
  drawer, and Miller gives you the **Taurus Sword** as you leave.

## Day 12: Danelf and Cryunne

- Equip the **Taurus Sword** on Sion. It's one-handed.
- New gear at the Danelf forge: **Cassius Mail** (fire), **Lucius Armor**
  (water), **Braham Shield** (fire), **Beluga Shield** (water) and **Zeland
  Helm** (lightning). The mantra expert in the pub basement
  teaches more.
- Talk to the king. Taurus has gone to the center of the earth.

## Day 13: Mantra Peak

A boat waits in Danelf. Cultists overrun the peak, and you have to fight any
you touch. The red ones drop **Horn Water**.

### Boss: Ravana

- **HP:** 6060 · **Weak:** water · **Drops:** Amrita
- Fire attacks, so **no water-defense gear**. Use PECOAMINUNA.
- MACROPOTIE hits hard even with defenses. The **Ice Whip** does good damage.

Zora sends you through the portal.

## Day 14: Cult Base

Party is around level 34.

- Light all the torches for the **King's Armor** and **Wind Bow**.
- **Purple Cultists** drop the **Tuval Knife** (water dagger, 85 attack) for
  Foxy. Kill the Cultist before the Inugami it travels with, or you won't get
  it.
- Follow the captain: down the center, right to a chest, then left and up.
  Drink from the jar.

### Boss: Taurus

- **HP:** 7200
- Strong physical attacks. Holy mantras and physical attacks both work.
- **Defend** when he says "so you want to play." Slayer does about 150 to
  everyone if you don't.
- Low on HP he heals himself with Dark Heal.

- West after the tower, **BoneKnights** drop the **Black Shield**, better
  than the Psycho Shield.
- In **Tarach**, the shop sells the **Marcus Armor** and **Octave Mail**
  (dark), **Hecate's Robe** (water) and **Heavy Shield** (wind).
- Talk to **Surlent** in the pub and trade the Psycho Shield for the
  **Power Shield** or the **Apocalypse**.
- North into **Shumisen**.

## Day 15: Shumisen

Party is around level 37.

- Take the left door for two chests, then talk to Gomorrah and take the
  teleporter.
- Press the switch and take the **red path**: east, up two sets of stairs, down
  the next for the **Hercules Armor**. Teleport back, take the red path again
  and the center stairs.
- **Baalzephon** drop the **Thor Ring**: MDef 12, Spirit +4, lightning.
- Two hidden chests are near the Hercules Armor: the **Dithras**, a sword
  stronger than the Apocalypse, and the **Love Whip**, Foxy's best weapon.
  The Love Whip is past a wall you can walk through in the same area.
- The teleporter with blue torches leads to the boss.

### Boss: Rudra

- **HP:** 7500 · **Weak:** water
- Fire attacks, so wear fire defense. Use water mantras or Foxy's **Ice Whip**.
- Rudra Quake hits harder than Rudra Laser. Rudra Shock is about 160 to
  everyone.

On the floating island, go through the south door, east, then south at the
fork. Inscribe **COABARRIERNA** (magic defense up) first.

### Final Boss: Gomorrah

- **HP:** 12000 · **Weak:** holy
- He summons three monsters you have to kill first:

| Summon | HP | Weak |
|--------|---:|------|
| Magician | 2500 | None |
| DarkKnight | 4250 | Holy |
| Suiryuu | 8000 | Holy |

- Suiryuu is the hard one.
- **Wear no elemental gear except dark defense**, or their mantras tear you
  apart.
