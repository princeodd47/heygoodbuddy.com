# Items

## Consumables

| Item | Effect | Sells | Where |
|------|--------|------:|-------|
| **Harvest Seed** | Restores 5 HP | 9 | R8–9 around the Holy Tree |
| **Medical Herb** | Restores 50 HP | 6 | Drop: Guard, Odio |
| **Rem Herb** | Restores 100 HP | 50 | Drop: Ice Queen, Ice King, Lazm |
| **Nuad Herb** | Restores 200 HP | 350 | Drop: Fulezast, Lazm (Dune), Chaos Zwei |
| **Nasty Water** | Restores 15 MP | 19 | |
| **Tasty Water** | Restores 30 MP | 9 | Chests early in every chapter |
| **Horn Water** | Restores 100 MP | 210 | Drop: red Cultists, Ghost, Mimics, Nataraja, Brusty |
| **Amrita** | Restores HP and MP | 1,400 | Drop: Capulet, Diyaus, Ravana, Brasnir |
| **Alarum** | Revives an ally | 100 | Drop: Kukoro, Ozunne, FireLizard |
| **Panacaea** | Cures abnormal status | 90 | Drop: SmSetavisa |
| **Purifier** | Cures Pollution, which blocks mantras | 500 | Drawers in many towns. Drop: Setaviza, KingEltina, Abrasax |
| **Flight Stone** | Escapes from battle | 420 | Drop: Gandharva |
| **Spirits** | Raises stats | 100 | R8 Gomorrah City |
| **Life Leaf** | Max HP +20 | 2,100 | Drop: Lezastes, Saizou |
| **Magic Leaf** | Max MP +10 | 3,500 | Drop: MimicL56 (always), Hausen (Dune) |

Max MP caps at 255. Farming the Mimics in Skuld's Gate for Magic Leaves is
the easy way to get there in Dune's chapter.
