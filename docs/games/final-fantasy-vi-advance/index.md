# Final Fantasy VI Advance

Notes from the Game Boy Advance version (2007), using its names and stats.
It adds four Espers, the Dragons' Den and new equipment to the original.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-book-open-variant:{ .lg .middle } __[Field Guide](field-guide/)__

    ---

    One page with every character and ability, the full bestiary, all
    equipment and Espers, a chapter-by-chapter walkthrough with bosses,
    steals, chests and missables, the Colosseum's best wagers, and tips.
    It has its own look and opens on a separate page.

</div>

## Sources

The stats, items and enemy data come from
[Caves of Narshe](https://www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/) (GBA mode). The
walkthrough follows the chapter order of Djibriel's
[Caves of Narshe walkthrough](https://www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/walkthrough.php),
with treasure locations from [Gamer Corner](https://guides.gamercorner.net/ffvi/),
and its steps and strategies are written in our own words. Rage and Dance
lists come from the [Final Fantasy Wiki](https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/).
