# Final Fantasy VI Advance

Field guide for the Game Boy Advance version (2007), using its names and
stats: every playable character and their special ability (Magitek, Steal,
Tools, Blitz, Bushido, Throw, Rage, Dance, Lore, Sketch and Slots), the full
bestiary, all equipment and Espers, a chapter-by-chapter walkthrough with
bosses, steals, chests and missables, the Colosseum's best wagers, and tips.
