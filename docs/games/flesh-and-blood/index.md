## Getting to Level 1

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Account | Sign in to JudgeHub at [judge.fabtcg.com](https://judge.fabtcg.com) with your GEM details. |
| 2. Level 0 | Exams › My Exams › *Flesh and Blood Level 0*. It's an open quiz, you can retake it, and you can save and come back to it. |
| 3. L1A | The **rules** exam. You need 85% or better. |
| 4. L1B | The **policy** exam. You need 85% or better. |
| 5. Checklist | Checklists › My Checklist › *Level 1 – Create New Draft*. It's auto-reviewed and approved right away. |

- Both exams have to be passed **within the last 120 days** when you submit
  the checklist.
- You don't need to have judged any events to take L0 or L1.
- Becoming a judge is free.
- Level 1 judges have to pass an **update quiz** after each major set release
  to stay certified.

??? info "What Level 1 expects"
    A Level 1 judge knows the basic rules and policy. They can resolve common
    card interactions and handle simple judge calls with the right infraction
    and penalty. That lines up with the two exams: L1A is the
    [Comprehensive Rules](rules.md), and L1B is the
    [Procedure and Penalty Guide](policy.md) and Tournament Rules and Policy.

??? info "After Level 1"
    Level 2 needs at least 120 days at Level 1, a recommendation review from a
    Level 2, 2 or more competitive events in the past 365 days, and the L2A and
    L2B exams at 85%. A person reviews the Level 2 application. Other
    certifications are L2P-Basic, L2P-Adv and TLC.

## Study plan

1. Read [Rules](rules.md) a section at a time and take the matching quiz
   topic after each one.
2. Read [Policy & Penalties](policy.md) until you can recite the penalty
   ladder and the upgrade rules from memory. The quick table at the top is
   the part to memorize.
3. Skim [Card Interactions](interactions.md). The release notes show how the
   rules apply to real cards. Exam questions often look like them.
4. Run the full quiz. Keep using **Review missed** until it's empty, and
   aim for 85% or better before you book the real exam.

## Official documents

When documents disagree, the newer one wins. Tournament rules beat card text,
and card text beats the Comprehensive Rules (CR 1.0.1).

| Document | Covers | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comprehensive Rules (CR) | How the game works | [rules.fabtcg.com](https://rules.fabtcg.com/en/cr/) |
| Procedure and Penalty Guide (PPG) | Infractions, fixes and penalties | [rules.fabtcg.com](https://rules.fabtcg.com/en/ppg/) |
| Tournament Rules and Policy (TRP) | Events, formats and player conduct | [rules.fabtcg.com](https://rules.fabtcg.com/en/trp/) |
| Release notes | Card-by-card rulings for each set | [fabtcg.com](https://fabtcg.com/rules-and-policy-center/release-notes/) |
| Card Vault | The official text of every card | [cardvault.fabtcg.com](https://cardvault.fabtcg.com) |
| Rules and Policy Center | Everything above, plus the Casual Procedure Guide | [fabtcg.com](https://fabtcg.com/rules-and-policy-center/) |

Rule numbers on these pages (like *CR 7.5.2* or *PPG 2.3*) point into
those documents. Everything here was checked against them on October 27,
2026.
