# Tips

What to know before you start, and how to get money early.

## Pick the Shaman

You choose Joshua's archetype at the start: Street Samurai, Decker or Gator
Shaman.

| Archetype | Starts with | Good at |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Street Samurai | Hand Razors | Guns and melee. Cyberware costs him nothing. |
| Decker | Datajack | The Matrix, and little else early on |
| Gator Shaman | Totem, 1 Fetish | Magic. Can still use guns and the Matrix with a Datajack. |

- **The Shaman is the strongest pick.** He can do everything a Decker can
  once he has a Datajack, and he has spells on top.
- The Samurai is the easier first playthrough. The Shaman is slower to make
  money with at the start.
- A Shaman gives up one item slot for his totem.
- Where to spend Karma: see [Attributes and Karma](#attributes-and-karma).

## Paying for Michael's Things

Your first job is to pay **Stoker 250 nuyen** at Stoker's Coffin Motel in
the Redmond Barrens for Michael's belongings. You get:

- A credstick with **500 nuyen**, so you make the fee back straight away.
- A **DocWagon wristband**. If you "die," DocWagon takes you to Seattle
  General Hospital for 10% of your nuyen, up to 5,000.
- An Allegiance Alpha cyberdeck.
- A scented letter from Tabatha Shale and two holopix (Dr. Haversheen,
  Caleb Brightmore). These start the story.

### Sell the Fetish for the Fee (Shaman)

The Shaman starts with 1 Fetish. It **sells for 257 nuyen**, just enough
for the 250 nuyen fee, so you can get Michael's things without doing a
single run.

- A Fetish absorbs the drain from one spell, then crumbles. You only need
  one when you cast at high Force or an aggressive posture, so you won't
  miss it early on.
- Buy more later at Riannon's Magic Shop (Puyallup Barrens) or the
  Medicine Lodge (Council Island), 350–500 nuyen.

If you'd rather keep it, take Gunderson's easy runs at the Jump House
(Redmond Barrens). They pay enough for the fee and earn Karma.

## Cyberware on a Shaman

Your **Magic** attribute is your **Essence rounded down**. Essence starts
at 6 and every piece of cyberware lowers it **for good**: you can't remove
it.

| Cyberware | Essence | Where | Nuyen |
|-----------|---------|-------|-------|
| Hand Razors | 0.1 | Dr. Bob's, Puyallup Barrens | 690–1,000 |
| Datajack | 0.2 | Little Chiba's Chop Shop, Redmond Barrens | 280–400 |
| Cybereyes | 0.2 | Council Island Hospital | 1,240–1,800 |
| Smartlink | 0.5 | Wire-Masters, Renraku Arcology | 1,040–1,500 |

- Any cyberware drops Magic from 6 to 5, but **everything up to 1.0 Essence
  costs only that one point.** If you take one piece, you might as well take
  a few small ones.
- Magic sets the **highest spell level you can learn**. You keep and can
  cast every spell you already have.
- Players report the one point barely matters in practice, and some say it
  costs nothing at all. Sources disagree, so **save before surgery** and
  check your Magic afterward.
- **The Datajack is the one worth it.** Matrix runs are the best early
  money, and the Shaman can't jack in without one. Little Chiba's is the
  cheapest at 400. Elsewhere it's 500–1,500. See [Matrix Runs](#matrix-runs).

## Gear Before Ghoul Runs

Gunderson offers a ghoul run early. **Turn it down** until you have armor
and a heavy pistol: Joshua's starting gun and armor are weak. Buy **armor
first**.

| Item | Type | Nuyen | Where |
|------|------|-------|-------|
| Vest w/ Plates | Armor | 600 | Ares Weapon Emporium, Redmond Barrens |
| Armor Jacket | Armor | 1,080 (900 at the Crime Mall) | Ares Weapon Emporium, Redmond Barrens |
| Warhawk | Heavy pistol, 6 shots | 500 | Ares Weapon Emporium, Redmond Barrens |
| Predator | Heavy pistol, 15 shots | 675 (540 at Weapons World) | Ares Weapon Emporium, Redmond Barrens |
| Medkit | Healing | 200 | Ares Weapon Emporium, Redmond Barrens |

- Prices are with Negotiation 0. They drop as it rises.
- **Sell your starter pistol** toward a Predator. It's cheaper to cab to
  Weapons World in the Renraku Arcology and buy it there.
- Universal gun clips are 25 nuyen each at Ares. You can carry 20.
- **Firing an unsilenced gun indoors sets off the alarm.** A Silencer for
  pistols is 400–500.
- Later: Light Combat Armor (10,000, Crime Mall, Puyallup Barrens), then
  Heavy Combat Armor (30,000, from the contact Agira Tetsumi).

## Making Money

- **Hire Ricky and sell his gear.** Ricky, a dwarf Shaman, is at the
  **Jackal's Lantern** in the Redmond Barrens. Hire him, give his items to
  Joshua, and sell them.
- **Sell datafiles.** With a Datajack, download files from Matrix
  datastores and sell them to the fence in the bottom-left house in
  Downtown Seattle. See [Matrix Runs](#matrix-runs).
- **Ghoul bounties.** Once you have a good pistol and decent armor, take
  ghoul runs from Gunderson. Negotiate up to 50 nuyen a ghoul, and turn
  down low offers to get a new one. Only ghouls count, not gargoyles or
  vampires.
- **Free cabs.** Once your reputation is decent, buy the **Ork Armband**
  from Roadrash at the Ork gang hideout in the Puyallup Barrens, 2,000
  nuyen. Every cab ride is free, and Orks attack you less.
- **The CHERNOBYL payday: 50,000 nuyen.** Once you're a decent decker, see
  the [Walkthrough](walkthrough.md#chernobyl).
- Give the character with the **highest Negotiation** the big purchases.
  It saves thousands of nuyen.

## Matrix Runs

The **Datajack is the only cyberware you need**. Michael's Allegiance Alpha
deck gets you started. Prices are with Negotiation 0.

### First Trip

1. **Crime Mall** (Puyallup Barrens): buy **Deception** and **Attack** at
   level 1, **60 nuyen each**. Only the Crime Mall sells Deception.
2. Jack in at a **terminal**, the ATM-like consoles around the city. The
   menu also lets you call a cab or a contact.
3. Download files from datastores.
4. Sell them to **Roscoe the Fixer**, in the bottom-left house in Downtown
   Seattle. Your deck's storage caps how much you can carry, so sell after
   every run.

- **Farm it:** you can download data without finishing the run, sell it,
  then jack back in with the **same passcode** and do it again. Data often
  pays more than the run itself.
- Cabs cost "a little nuyen" but no guide gives a number. Leave some spare.
- Raise **Computer to about 5** before easy runs. 7 handles even hard ones.

### Terminal Menu

| Option | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| Enter Passcode | Pick a system you have the passcode for |
| Run Passcode | Jack into the system for your current Matrix run |
| System Search | Jack into a random system |

From outside a building you start at the system's **SAN**. From a terminal
inside one you start at an **IOP**.

### Matrix Run Pay

| Run | Johnsons | Nuyen | Karma |
|-----|----------|-------|-------|
| Easy | Mortimer Reed, Vigore and Jarl | 400–500 | 2 (1 from Vigore and Jarl) |
| Medium | Caleb Brightmore, Julius Strouther, Vigore and Jarl | 2,400–2,600 | 3 (2 from Vigore and Jarl) |
| Hard | Caleb Brightmore, Vigore and Jarl | 6,000–6,200 | 5 (4 from Vigore and Jarl) |

Hard runs are against Corp systems such as Mitsuhama, Fuchi, Renraku and
Lone Star. Later you get passcodes to the big named systems and can farm
them without a run.

### Programs

| Where | Programs |
|-------|----------|
| **Crime Mall**, Puyallup Barrens | **Deception**, Attack, Sleaze, Relocate, Medic, Shield, Mirrors, Slow, Smoke |
| **Microtronics**, Renraku Arcology | Attack, Sleaze, Analyze, Medic, Shield, Mirrors, Slow |
| **Wilma Temmenhoff**: 1,000 to meet, through Gunderson at the Jump House | Rebound and Degrade, level 3, 5,000 each |
| **Kipp David**: 2,000 to meet, through Caleb Brightmore at the Icarus Descending, Downtown | Rebound and Degrade, level 6, 30,000 each |

Attack and Deception matter most. Sleaze, Rebound and Degrade have some
use. Skip the rest. Programs carry over when you buy a new deck.

**Prices** depend on the program's size. With Negotiation 0–2:

| Size | Programs | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 | L5 | L6 |
|------|----------|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| Small | **Attack, Deception**, Relocate, Smoke | 60 | 480 | 1,620 | 3,840 | 7,500 | 12,960 |
| Medium | Sleaze, Mirrors, Analyze | 90 | 720 | 2,430 | 5,760 | 11,250 | 19,440 |
| Large | Medic, Shield, Slow | 120 | 960 | 3,240 | 7,680 | 15,000 | 25,920 |

Higher levels also take more deck memory. A level 1 Small program uses 2,
level 2 uses 8, level 3 uses 18. Each point of Negotiation above 2 knocks
a little off (Small L2 is 330 at Negotiation 12).

- Try **Deception before attacking**. ICE that Deception works on only
  attacks if the program fails.
- Most files are 30–40 Mp. With under 200 Mp of storage you'll struggle to
  fit five.

| ICE | Use |
|-----|-----|
| Access, Gray | Deception or Sleaze |
| Barrier, Black | Attack. Deception doesn't work. |
| Trace | Relocate |

Medic repairs your persona. **Tar Pits delete programs for good.**

| Deck | Nuyen | Where |
|------|-------|-------|
| Allegiance Alpha | Free | Michael's belongings |
| Cyber Shack PCD-500 | 5,000 | Microtronics, Renraku Arcology |
| Fuchi Cyber-5 | 25,000 | Microtronics, Renraku Arcology |

## Attributes and Karma

- **Spend Karma at a hotel.** You can't spend it anywhere else. Stoker's
  Coffin Motel is one.
- Raising an attribute costs Karma equal to the new level: Quickness 4
  costs 4 Karma. Joshua is human, so every attribute tops out at **6**.
- Skills cost twice that and top out at 12. Pistols, SMGs and Shotguns cost
  less than other skills, but **Firearms covers every gun**. A Pistols
  rating below your Firearms is ignored.
- **Karma comes from runs.** You also get 1 for roughly every 15–20 kills
  or ICE crashed, and some for story steps. You never lose it.
- **Early Karma:** escort and courier runs from Gunderson, 1 each.
  **Medium corp runs** (4 Karma, from Julius Strouther, Caleb Brightmore
  or Vigore and Jarl) are the best Karma later. Vigore and Jarl pay more
  nuyen but 1 less Karma.

### What Each Attribute Does

| Attribute | Does | Priority |
|-----------|------|----------|
| **Body** | Resists damage from guns, melee, physical spells and Black ICE | **First.** Get it to 4 early, 6 by the end. |
| **Quickness** | Movement and attack speed, hit chance, Matrix combat | High. Max it by the end. |
| **Intelligence** | Attack speed, hit chance, Matrix combat | High. Max it by the end. |
| **Willpower** | Lowers spell drain, resists mana spells, a little hit chance | **High for the Shaman.** Low for others. |
| **Charisma** | Cheaper runners, talking past guards and Lone Star | Medium. 5 early, 6 later. Only Joshua's counts. |
| **Strength** | Melee damage and grenade accuracy | Only for fists, Hand Razors and Spurs |
| **Essence** | Magical healing. Only cyberware changes it. | Can't be raised |
| **Magic** | Highest spell Force. Equals Essence rounded down. | Can't be raised |

- **Attack speed** comes from Quickness and Intelligence together, so
  raise them as a pair. One guide says attack speed is their average
  rounded down, so even totals waste nothing.

### Skills Worth Raising

| Skill | Does | How high |
|-------|------|----------|
| **Sorcery** | Spell success | 5 early, 8 or more late |
| **Firearms** | Every gun | 5 for most of the game, 7–8 late |
| **Computer** | Matrix | 5 for easy runs, 7 for hard ones |
| **Electronics** | Security terminals and Maglocks on corp runs | 4–6. A level 5 Maglock needs about 8. |
| **Biotech** | Medkit healing | 4–5 on one runner |
| **Negotiation** | Run pay and shop prices | 4–5. Raise it early. |
| **Reputation** | Gets you into clubs; cheaper runners | 4 for Club Penumbra, then leave it |
| Melee | Fists, Hand Razors, Spurs | Only with Spurs |
| Throwing | Grenades | Skip |

- Pistols, SMGs and Shotguns: pick one instead of Firearms only if you'll
  never switch guns.
- Only the runner you control talks to guards, so their Charisma is the one
  that counts there.

### Shaman Build

Joshua starts with Body 2, Quickness 4, Strength 1, Charisma 2,
Intelligence 3, Willpower 4, Sorcery 3 and Negotiation 3. Raise things in
this order:

| Step | Raise | Karma |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | **Body** 2 → 4 | 7 |
| 2 | **Sorcery** 3 → 5 | 18 |
| 3 | **Willpower** 4 → 6 | 11 |
| 4 | **Reputation** 1 → 4, for Club Penumbra | 18 |
| 5 | **Charisma** 2 → 5 | 12 |
| 6 | **Negotiation** 3 → 5. You buy your own spells. | 18 |
| 7 | **Body**, **Quickness** and **Intelligence** to 6 | 37 |
| 8 | **Sorcery** to 8, Charisma to 6 | 48 |

- Leave **Strength** alone. A Shaman who casts can skip guns too, or raise
  only Pistols as a backup.
- A full build takes about 230 Karma over the game.
- Skip stat cyberware. Each piece costs 0.5–2 Essence, and that costs
  Magic. See [Cyberware on a Shaman](#cyberware-on-a-shaman).

### Samurai and Decker

- **Buy stat cyberware before you max the attribute.** Dermal Plating
  (+1 Body) and Muscle Replacement (+1 Strength and Quickness) only add a
  point if the attribute is below 6 when installed. Raising it to 6
  afterward shows as "6 c7". Install it at 6 and you get nothing.
- **Samurai:** starts with Body 4 and Firearms 4. Raise **Firearms**,
  Quickness and Intelligence, then Charisma to 5 and Biotech to 4.
  Strength only if you fight with Spurs. Wired Reflexes is the best
  cyberware: two levels roughly double attack speed.
- **Decker:** starts with Computer 3, Quickness 5 and Intelligence 4. Get
  **Computer to 5** before easy Matrix runs and Body up early, since Black
  ICE hits Body. Electronics to 5.

## Spells for a Shaman

| Spell | Shop | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Flame Bolt | Riannon's Magic Shop, Puyallup Barrens | Best attack spell, except against fire-immune enemies |
| Mana Blast | Merlin's Lore, Renraku Arcology | Solid default attack |
| Rockskin | Riannon's or Merlin's Lore | More armor. Cast before boss fights. |
| Heal Wounds | Riannon's, Merlin's Lore or Medicine Lodge | Level 1 heals the whole team |
| Sleep | Medicine Lodge, Council Island | Knocks enemies out |
| Invisibility | Medicine Lodge, Council Island | Useful on Corp runs |

- Skip Mana Zap and Flame Dart. They're the weakest attacks.
- **Hellblast and Mana Storm hit your own team.**
- A **spell focus** makes one spell stronger. Rating 2 foci cost 4,000.
- **Shop discounts from contacts:** Prof. Jefferson (free, through Trent at
  the Space Needle, Downtown) gets you a discount at Riannon's for 1,500.
  Gregory Wilns gets you one at Merlin's Lore for 4,000.

## Random Encounters

Fights on the street are random. They happen as you walk around town,
whether or not you're on a run, so sometimes you get jumped and sometimes
you don't. No guide gives the actual odds or rules for when enemies leave.

- **Runs don't spawn street enemies.** Courier and escort runs just mean
  more walking, so more chances to be jumped. On an escort, attackers go
  after you, not your client.
- **Ghoul runs** are different: the ghouls live inside the abandoned
  building. Walk around in there until they attack, up to 5 at a time.
- **Warning sign:** if you suddenly walk slowly and can't enter buildings,
  an attack is starting. Turn and fight.
- **Running away** from Halloweeners is often easier than fighting them
  early on.
- **Don't leave the screen** while a runner is knocked out: they go back
  to where you hired them. If Joshua is down, leaving counts as dying.

### Lone Star

A patrol may stop you in the street. Choose **Talk**. Higher Charisma
helps.

- With an illegal weapon and no permit, they ask for a **500 nuyen**
  "on-sight fee" and leave. Fight or run instead, and you're fighting cops.
- If you get busted, they take your illegal gear and fine you heavily.
- Siding with the Mafia makes patrols always leave you alone.
- A [Weapon Permit](#other-tricks) stops the illegal-weapon busts.

### Choice Events

Sometimes a short scene stops you and you pick A, B or C. The outcome is
random, and some are traps:

- A man lying hurt in the street may be a **vampire** playing dead, or a
  real person. Attacking a real one sends Lone Star after you.
- A grenade dealer in an alley may be **Lone Star undercover**. Buying or
  attacking can get you busted.
- "Doctors" chasing an escaped patient may be vampires. Helping them pays,
  but you've helped them catch an innocent man.
- If you're unsure, **Walk Away** is usually safe.

### Inside Corp Buildings

Runs into corp buildings have their own random events:

- Guards asking for ID. Talking your way out may work, and a badge helps.
- A hidden floor panel: a 50-50 chance of tripping the alarm.
- Logged-in terminals that may give your target's floor, turn off cameras,
  or trip the alarm.
- In the Renraku Arcology, Renraku security spotting you brings a strike
  team at once.

## Other Tricks

- **Gang protection:** each gang sells an item that makes its members
  attack you less.

    | Item | Gang | Seller | Nuyen |
    |------|------|--------|-------|
    | Ork Armband | Orks, Puyallup Barrens | Roadrash | 2,000 (also free cabs) |
    | Halloweener Patch | Halloweeners, Redmond Barrens | Ratspike | 3,000 |
    | Eye-Fiver Ear Cuff | Eye-Fivers, Penumbra District | Mauler | 4,000 |

- **Weapon Permit:** stops Lone Star arresting you for illegal weapons.
  8,000 from Henry J. Culver (Ork gang leader) or 10,000 from Sgt.
  Chillicut.
- **Free ally:** once you're strong, look for a hut out in the Salish-Shidhe.
  It starts the quest to save **Stark**, who then joins for free. See the
  [Walkthrough](walkthrough.md#starks-run).
- **Debug menu:** on the title screen, after the logo starts scrolling and
  before "Press Start" shows, press **A, B, B, A, C, A, B**. "Run Faster"
  is handy for grinding. The rest spoils the game.
