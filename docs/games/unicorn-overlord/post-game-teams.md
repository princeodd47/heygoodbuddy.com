# Post-Game Teams

Ten full squads to field together for post-game content on the hardest
difficulty. No two squads share an item you only have one of. Every unit
listed is in exactly one squad.

- **Squads:** your army caps at 10 units (squads), with 5 characters each.
- **Class-change items:** none of these squads uses one. Everyone stays in
  their default class.
- **One start-of-battle skill per unit.** Only one *Limited* skill that
  activates at the start of battle fires per squad side. If two units have
  one (Alain's Rapid Order, a Swordmaster's Hastened Strike, a Divine
  Blessing), the one you want may not go off. Each squad below says which one
  to keep on. Turn the others off in Tactics.
- **Ring of the Maiden** goes on Alain's bonded partner. The squads assume
  that's Scarlett. If you married someone else, swap the Ring to them and give
  Scarlett a Lapis Pendant.
- Test every squad in a mock battle before a hard fight. The forecast tells
  you whether the combo kills before the enemy acts.

## The Ten Squads

### 1. Alain: Trinity Rain

A magic nuke that hits the whole enemy side three times before most enemies
act. Yahna starts the battle with Quick Cast, Ramona's White Cat-Ear Hood lets
her act again right away so the charge skips a turn, and Scarlett's
Familiar's Choker adds +50% magic attack to the cast. Three hits burn through
evasion passives on Thieves and Werefoxes. Alain and Celeste clean up and
tank.

| Row | Unit | Class | Equipment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Front | Alain | High Lord | Holy Unicorn Blade, Holy Unicorn Shield, Ring of the Unicorn, Lapis Pendant |
| Front | Celeste | Gryphon Master | Boreas' Axe, Heavenwyvern Reins, Elite Beret, Royal Scarf |
| Back | Scarlett | High Priestess | Kingstaff Albiore, Familiar's Choker, Ring of the Maiden |
| Back | Yahna | Sorceress | Millennium Scepter, Sorcerer's Medallion, Lapis Pendant, Carnelian Pendant |
| Back | Ramona | Wereowl | Euros' Staff, White Cat-Ear Hood, Lapis Pendant, Holy Unicorn Signet |

**Start-of-battle skill:** Yahna's Quick Cast. Turn off Alain's Rapid Order
and Scarlett's Divine Blessing.

??? note "Tactics"

    - **Yahna:** Trinity Rain first, with no condition. Quick Cast at the start
      of battle. Turn off her other active skills so nothing else uses her AP
      before the cast. Quick Cast lowers her crit rate by 50%. That's fine, the
      cast still clears most squads.
    - **Ramona:** Quick Impetus targeting Yahna, with no condition. Keep her
      other passives off so she always has the PP for it. Limited Heal as her
      active skill for when the rain doesn't finish the job.
    - **Scarlett:** Sorcerous Connection (from the Familiar's Choker) on
      Yahna. Heals on anyone below 75% HP after that.
    - **Alain:** Spinning Edge or his row attack to clean up survivors.
      Defensive passives on.
    - **Celeste:** Attacks on the lowest-HP enemy. Her Gryphon passives on.
      She keeps the squad mobile next to Alain's cavalry.
    - If the enemy has a Thief with Sneaking Edge, check that Ramona's barrier
      doesn't eat the PP she needs for Quick Impetus. Turn the barrier off if
      it does.

### 2. Hilda: Dragoon Dive

Hilda's Dragoon Dive with a fast charge. The Celestial Talisman gives her
Hastened Charge, so the dive comes out on her first turn. Ochlys's Dancer's
Bracelet buffs her attack before each dive, Raenys's Tailwind adds more, and
Lex blocks the archers that would ground the flyers.

| Row | Unit | Class | Equipment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Front | Ochlys | Feathersword | Heavenswing Sword, Heavenswing Shield, Dancer's Bracelet, Royal Scarf |
| Front | Lex | Vanguard | Meteorite Sword, Unyielding Shield, Cloth Gauntlets, Lapis Pendant |
| Back | Hilda | Wyvern Master | Dragoon's Warspear, Celestial Talisman, Sapphire Pendant, Carnelian Pendant |
| Back | Raenys | Featherbow | Apeliotes' Bow, Raven Plume, Lapis Pendant, Clearsight Amulet |
| Back | Sanatio | Featherstaff | Papal Crosier, Lapis Pendant, Gold Bangle |

**Start-of-battle skill:** Lex's Provoke is the only one on. Turn off
Hilda's Dragon's Roar and Sanatio's Hastened Heal.

??? note "Tactics"

    - **Hilda:** Dragoon Dive first, with no condition. Then Diving Thrust on
      the lowest-HP enemy. Passive: Hastened Charge (Celestial Talisman). It
      costs 4 PP and the Talisman lowers her AP by 1, so the two pendants are
      there to keep her PP up.
    - **Ochlys:** Powerful Call (Dancer's Bracelet) on Hilda. Shield Smite on
      armored enemies to lower their defense before the dive. Spiral Sword
      otherwise.
    - **Lex:** Arrow Cover on flying allies. Provoke at the start of battle.
      Attacks on the lowest-HP enemy.
    - **Raenys:** Photon Arrow first, so Tailwind triggers before Hilda
      dives. Then attacks on flying or cavalry targets.
    - **Sanatio:** Row heal when anyone is below 75% HP. Barrier on the
      lowest-HP ally when magic is coming.

counter to this squad, so watch for enemy Clerics and Bishops.

      Eye before her attacks, Triple Counter off (she's in the back).
    - **Travis:** Black Cat-Ear Hood's skill on Yunifi. Attacks on the

    - **Bryce:** Cover on the lowest-HP ally. Row attacks otherwise.

      attack otherwise.

Corvie Indo's squad, kept as built. Everything inflicts or punishes
afflictions. Berengaria's Death Spin stuns a row and does 75% more to
afflicted enemies, so she snowballs once anything is burned, frozen, stunned
or blinded. Virginia tanks and smashes armor, Dinah blinds whole columns and
Monica heals.

**Start-of-battle skill:** Berengaria's Eye of the Warrior Princess. Turn off

??? note "Tactics"

    - **Virginia:**
        - Vertical Edge on flying enemies.
        - Iron Crusher on armored enemies.
        - Brandish otherwise.
        - Iron Crusher when she doesn't have the AP for Brandish.
        - Passives: Vengeful Impetus at 3 AP or less, Royal Guard when an

    - **Berengaria:**
        - Death Spin on rows with 2 or more enemies.
        - Vicious Torment on afflicted enemies.

        - Shadow Thrust on a full column with no blinded enemies, then on

        - Piercing Lance otherwise.
        - Passives: Shadow Pursuit on enemies that aren't blinded, Nocturnal

    - **Railanor:**
        - Nature's Wrath on cavalry in full columns.
        - Lightning Blade, armored enemies first.
        - Mirage Stab when you need it.
        - Passive: Sylphic Barrier on the ally with the lowest evasion.
    - **Monica:**
        - Row Heal when anyone is below 75% HP.
        - Saint's Blade on the lowest-HP enemy.
        - Row Heal on anyone not at full HP.
        - Passives: Quick Heal at 75% HP or less when she has 3 PP or more,
          Row Barrier and Magic Barrier against magic attacks, First Aid.

## Other Strong Post-Game Teams

Well-known builds from the community. Several overlap with the ten squads
above, so you can't field all of these alongside them.

- **Hilda Dragoon Dive (original).** Hilda with the Celestial Talisman and two
  pendants, Raenys with the Raven Plume for Tailwind, a Dancer's Bracelet
  holder like Amalia, and Rosalinde with a Ruby Pendant. Squad 2 is a version
  of this that frees up Amalia and Rosalinde.
- **Hilda and Primm.** Hilda, Primm with the Lyrical Wand, Liza with Skiron's
  Bow for Hastened Cover, a mage, and a Feathershield or Vanguard to tank.
- **Trinity Rain (Sorceress).** A Sorceress with the Millennium Scepter and
  Quick Cast, a Wereowl with the White Cat-Ear Hood, a second Sorceress with
  the Familiar's Choker, a Sergeant with Keen Call, and Alain. Squad 1 uses
  Scarlett for the Choker and Celeste for the Sergeant.
- **Alain multi-nuke.** Yahna's Trinity Rain with Hastened Action, Quick
  Impetus and the Magia Soul's Recast, so she casts it twice.
- **Glacial Rain.** Yunifi with Travis (Black Cat-Ear Hood), Govil
  (Gambler's Coin), Berengaria with the Mirage Shield, and Gloucester.
- **Yunifi Triple Counter dodge.** Yunifi in the front with the Lucky Coin,
  Retaliation Earrings and Royal Scarf. Raenys with the Featherbow, a Druid
  with the Dustbound Staff and Lapis Bell, Dinah and Nigel.
- **Yunifi crit.** Yunifi with Eltolinde, a Wereowl, the Dirty Gambler's Coin
  and a Swordmaster.
- **Super Tax Brothers.** A Legionnaire tank and three Landsknechts with
  Following Slash and Golden Eggs. Every hit on the tank gets three
  counters, and the Golden Eggs bring in extra gold.
- **Bruno and Braves.** Bruno's Grand Smash with a squad built to feed him
  buffs and actions. Squad 7 is a version of this.
- **Doom Knight cavalry (Ajax).** A Doom Knight leading Great Knights and
  cavalry for fast map pressure.
- **Freezer Burn.** A Doom Knight with a Frostbrand conferral and a Druid
  with the Dustbound Staff. Frozen enemies take burn damage on top.
- **Bruno burn team.** Bruno with units that set the enemy on fire first,
  so every follow-up hits burning targets.
- **Affliction Angels.** Albion's winged units stacking afflictions on the
  enemy so the rest of the squad hits harder.
- **Corvie's affliction squad.** Squad 4 above.
- **Rosalinde counter squad.** Rosalinde's Elemental Roar with a Frostbrand
  conferral, Virginia covering, a Landsknecht, and an Elven Fencer with the
  Necromancer's Lantern. Squad 5 swaps in Ilenia so Virginia can stay in
  squad 4.
- **Amalia and Berenice.** Amalia with Berenice holding the Elite Standard.
- **Berengaria and the Doom Knight.** Berengaria, a Doom Knight, a Viking
  with a War Horn, an Elven Archer, and a Bishop with the Lyrical Wand.
- **Alain and Berengaria all-rounder.** Alain and Berengaria together with
  support. Good on any map.
- **Josette werefox dodge team.** Werefoxes and other evasion tanks that waste enemy
  attacks.
- **Nina and Gilbert.** Nina, Gilbert, Rosalinde, a Sainted Knight and a
  Vanguard.
