# Post-Game

Once you finish the story, you can reach **Slimeland Island**, and colored
orbs and caravan members start appearing around the world.

## Colored Orbs

- Orbs turn up in **camps** (shown as tents) and **dungeons** on the world
  map.
- Give the **pond fairy** a World Leaf for a random orb.
- Use them on the pedestals in the Slimeland shrine to enter an
  [orb dungeon](orb-dungeons.md).

## Respawning

Finishing an orb dungeon respawns the colored orbs and the caravan members
in towns. **One orb on a single pedestal** opens a short dungeon, which is
the fastest way to refresh both.
