# Caravan

After the story, new caravan members appear in towns each time you finish
an [orb dungeon](orb-dungeons.md). Who appears is random, but they only
show up in certain buildings.

## What to Aim For

1. A low-weight **True Seer**.
2. A low-weight **True Mapper**.
3. A **True Sage**, whatever the weight. **Return** makes farming towns for
   new members much faster. Two or three lower sages do the same job.
4. Three **True Fencers**. They hit spirit bosses four times, and give your
   lead monster Falcon Slash for the first round.
5. Three **True Warriors**. Big hits, and they double your lead monster's
   attack for the first round.

Once you have these, farm the spirit bosses for stat boosts and Wagon Orbs.

## Example Caravan

20 members: 12 in wagons and 8 in camp.

| Wagon | Members |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | True Fencer, True Warrior, True Fighter, True Mapper |
| 2 | True Fencer, True Warrior, True Fighter, Magic Master |
| 3 | True Fencer, True Warrior, True Seer, True Sage |
| Camp | Cleric, King Merchant, Master Thief, Play Master, Gambler King, True Rebirther x3 |
