# Orb Dungeons

Place colored orbs on the pedestals in the **Slimeland shrine** to enter a
randomly generated orb dungeon. Floor-by-floor notes are on
[Dungeon Floors](dungeon-floors.md).

## Orbs

| Orb | Spirit | Monster family |
|-----|--------|----------------|
| Yellow | Earth Spirit | Animal |
| Green | Wind Spirit | Nature |
| Blue | Water Spirit | Slime |
| Red | Fire Spirit | Beast |
| Silver | Light Spirit | Material |
| Purple | Dark Spirit | Demon |

- **The color you use most decides the spirit.** Ties are settled at
  random, so use two of the color you want plus two *different* others
  (e.g. Green + Green + Red + Silver for Wind Spirit). Two pairs, like
  Yellow + Yellow + Red + Red, is a coin toss.
- Random encounters come from the dungeon's monster family, and get
  stronger the deeper you go.
- **The number of orbs decides how many floors the dungeon has.** With 4
  orbs there are 14 floors, and the spirit waits on 15F.
- **A single orb on one pedestal opens a short dungeon.** It's the quickest
  way to respawn orbs and caravan members if you don't want to fight a
  spirit.

## The Spirit Boss

The spirit waits at the bottom of a 4-orb dungeon. How fast you beat it
decides the reward:

| Turns | Reward |
|-------|--------|
| 1–8 | The spirit's special orb |
| 9–12 | The spirit's elemental orb |
| 13+ | Nothing |

Once you already have a spirit's elemental orb, it offers a wish instead:
its special orb, one of its three hearts, or a stat boost (+30 to that stat
for every monster in your party).

Collect all four elemental orbs (Earth, Wind, Water and Fire) to open the
**Gisvarg** dungeon.

| Spirit | Orbs | Stat boost | Hearts | Special orb |
|--------|------|------------|--------|-------------|
| Earth Spirit | Yellow | Max HP | Earth Spirit, Infergon, King Leo | Wagon Orb |
| Wind Spirit | Green | Speed | Wind Spirit, Scarabator, Rosevine | GreatTree Orb |
| Water Spirit | Blue | Defense | Water Spirit, Gran Slime, Master Slime | GreatLog Orb |
| Fire Spirit | Red | Attack | Fire Spirit, Divinegon, Rain Hawk | Holy Beast Orb |
| Light Spirit | Silver | Wisdom | Light Spirit, DrakMachine, Roboster 2 | Metal Orb |
| Dark Spirit | Purple | Max MP | Dark Spirit, Night Lich, Grakos | Evil God Orb |

## Special Orbs

Each special orb opens its own dungeon with a boss. Beat the boss fast
enough and you get its heart.

| Orb | Boss | Heart if you win within |
|-----|------|-------------------------|
| Wagon Orb | — | No dungeon. The old wagon man remodels one wagon: **+1 weight**. |
| GreatTree Orb | Watabou | 13 rounds |
| GreatLog Orb | Warubou | Unknown |
| Holy Beast Orb | Master Dragon | 17 rounds |
| Metal Orb | Gold Slime | About 15 rounds |
| Evil God Orb | Durran | 14 rounds worked |

## Floor Events

In a 4-orb dungeon, floors 2–12 sometimes have an event instead of a normal
floor:

- **Merchant:** buys and sells items.
- **Warrior:** more battles on the next floor.
- **Player:** no battles on the next floor.
- **Medic**
- **Chest room:** 8 chests of seeds, gold and Tiny Medals.
- **Monster blocking the way:** a fight you can't skip. You always get the
  center monster's heart, as long as you have an empty slot.
