# Characters

Every recruit, in the order they can join. **Optional** recruits need you to
do something, and most can be missed for good if you move on first. Classes
show the class before and after promotion. A few are given as the game's own
abbreviations.

## Recruits

| Character | Class | Joins | How |
|-----------|-------|-------|-----|
| **Max** | Swordsman → Hero | Start | Must survive every battle. |
| **Ken** | Knight → Paladin | Guardiana, Chapter 1 | Starting team. |
| **Luke** | Warrior → Gladiator | Guardiana, Chapter 1 | Starting team. |
| **Tao** | Mage → Wizard | Guardiana, Chapter 1 | Starting team. Learns Blaze 1–4. |
| **Lowe** | Healer → Vicar | Guardiana, Chapter 1 | Starting team. |

| **Gort** | Warrior → Gladiator | Guardiana bar, after Battle 2 | **Optional.** Hear his story in the bar on your first visit. |

## Missable items

| Item | Where | How not to miss it |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| **Heat Axe** | Battle 12 | One Lizardman carries it. Kill it with a unit that has a free slot. You can leave and replay the battle for another try. |
| **Sword of Light, White Ring, Evil Ring, Black Ring** and more | Mishaela's Lair, after Battle 21 | Open the chest on Mishaela's platform **last**. |
| **Doom Blade** | Battle 22 | One Durahan carries it. Kill it with a unit that has a free slot. |
| **Turbo Pepper** | Tower of the Ancients, upstairs | One per game. Give it to a slow unit you'll keep using. |
| **Valkyrie** | Battle 23 | Open the chest at the bottom of the long ladder during the battle. |
| **Demon Rod** | Battle 23 | The Demon Master carries it. Land the last hit with a unit that has a free slot. |
| **Atlas, Halberd** | Battle 26 | Chests on the platforms to the right. Open them during the battle. |
