# Walkthrough

The story in order, with what to buy, steal and pick up in each area, the
bosses, and the hunts and side quests that open along the way.

- **Buy:** lines list what's worth buying in that town.
- **Chests:** lines list fixed chests worth a detour. Most other chests are
  random, so they aren't listed.
- **Steal:** lines name the rare steal. The Thief's Cuffs (from Vinuskar)
  make rare steals more likely.
- **Missable:** marks anything you can lose for good.

Magick and technicks are bought once and then need the license too. Hunts
are listed where they first become sensible to do, not where the bill first
appears. Check the Clan Primer to see which ones you've done.

See [New Playthrough](new-playthrough.md) for the early-game plan,
[Party](party.md) for when people join and leave,
[Monographs](monographs.md) and [Bazaar](bazaar.md).

## Prologue: Nalbina Fortress

You play as Reks. It's a tutorial.

### Boss: Air Cutter Remora

- **HP:** 2,200
- Your allies win it for you. Heal with Potions if needed.

## Rabanastre

- Kill the three Dire Rats in the Garamsythe Waterway, then go to Migelo,
  then the Sandsea tavern. Tomaj gives you the **Writ of Transit** and the
  first hunt.
- The Desert Merchant by the Southern Plaza fountain gives you a **Small
  Package** for a friend outside the Eastgate. Selling it pays 150 gil,
  delivering it pays nothing.
- **Buy:** Potions. Save the rest of your gil for after the hunt.

## Dalmasca Estersand

- Steal from everything. Sleeping Cactites give Earth Stones without a
  fight.
- Avoid the Wild Saurian (the T-rex). It one-shots you for now.
- Level up a bit here.

### Hunt: Rogue Tomato (Rank I)

- **Petitioner:** Tomaj, the Sandsea
- **Where:** on a ledge near the Estersand entrance
- **HP:** 134
- It runs at half HP. Follow it down and finish it.
- **Reward:** 300 gil, 2 Potions, Teleport Stone

## Rabanastre, Second Visit

- After the parade, claim the Rogue Tomato reward from Tomaj.
- Pick up the **Thextera** bill on the board. Leave it until Penelo joins.
- **Join Clan Centurio:** talk to the Conspicuous Bangaa in North End, then
  Montblanc upstairs in the Clan Hall. The Clan Provisioner in Muthru Bazaar
  opens.
- **Buy:** Cure, Fire, Blindna and Slow at Yugri's Magicks, and Libra at
  Batahn's Technicks.
- Buy the Giza Plains map from the moogle in the South Plaza (30 gil).
- Start talking to shop owners and reading notice boards for the
  [Monographs](monographs.md).
- Go to Old Dalan in Lowtown. He sends you to the Giza Plains for a
  Sunstone.

## Giza Plains (The Dry)

- Talk to Masyua in the Nomad Village. **Penelo joins.** She gives you
  3 Potions and 2 Phoenix Downs.
- **Chests:** **Blind** in the Toam Hills. Get it now.
- Find Jinn in the Crystal Glade (south). Take the **Shadestone** and hold it
  up to the Dark Crystals until it's charged.
- **Buy in the Nomad Village:** Dark, once the Sunstone is made.
- **Trial Mode, stages 1–3:** do them now, with Penelo. See
  [New Playthrough](new-playthrough.md#trial-mode-stages-13). The Diamond
  Armlet from Stage 1 and the Karkata from Stage 3 carry you a long way.

### Hunt: Thextera (Rank I)

- **Petitioner:** Gatsly, the Sandsea
- **Where:** Dalmasca Westersand, Galtea Downs
- **Reward:** 500 gil, Headguard, Teleport Stone. Unlocks the Hunter's
  Monograph.

### Hunt: Flowering Cactoid (Rank I)

- **Petitioner:** Dantro, South Bank Village (Estersand)
- **Where:** Dalmasca Estersand, Yardang Labyrinth
- **Chests:** **Silence** is in the Yardang Labyrinth. There's a second
  Silence chest in the Lhusu Mines; opening either one removes the other.
- Get to level 5 or 6 first. Steal from it in Trial Mode Stage 3 instead
  if you want the Mythril Blade or Karkata.
- Afterward, take the **Cactus Flower** to Dantro's wife in South Bank
  Village. That starts **Desert Patient**, which you finish later.

Take the Sunstone to Old Dalan. **Penelo leaves** outside his house, and
he gives you the **Crescent Stone**.

## Garamsythe Waterway and the Royal Palace

- Enter from Storehouse 5 in Lowtown. Kytes gives you 2 Potions and
  4 Eye Drops.
- **Royal Palace:** call the guards over to pull them off their posts. Use
  the Crescent Stone on the Lion Signet, then go through the lit doorway.
  The palace can't be revisited.
- **Balthier and Fran join**, and "Amalia" joins as a guest. You can now
  edit Gambits. Spend LP on Gambit slots.
- Pick a job for each character. See
  [New Playthrough](new-playthrough.md#jobs).
- Get 3 **Demon Eyeballs** from Garchimacera before you leave (Bazaar).

### Boss: Flans (x4)

- **HP:** 360–420 each
- **Weak:** fire
- **Steal:** Sagittarius Gem (rare)
- Gang up on one at a time.

### Boss: Firemane

- **HP:** 3,571
- **Weak:** water · **Absorbs:** fire
- **Steal:** Grimoire Togail (rare)
- Turn off any Fire gambits. Lure it into the water with Flee; it's weaker
  there. Bushfire hits everyone and poisons, so keep Antidotes handy.

## Nalbina Dungeons

- Fist-fight the three Seeqs. Kill **Gwitch** first; he heals.
- Get your gear back in the Confiscatory.
- In the Black Watch, the soldiers all chain together, so it's a good spot
  for loot.
- **Basch joins** as a guest. The Dungeons can't be revisited.

## Barheim Passage

- **Buy from Burrogh** at the bottom of the first room: Thunder,
  Immobilize, Dark, Poisona, Vox, and a Leather Shield for your tank.
- Keep the lights on. Battery Mimics drain the charge gauge, so kill them
  first. They're weak to ice.
- Mimic chests glow blue.
- Basch arms himself at the North-South Junction.

### Boss: Mimic Queen

- **HP:** 4,073
- **Weak:** ice
- **Steal:** Rose Corsage (rare), Storm Magicite
- Ignore the Tiny Mimics; she keeps summoning more. Blizzard her, and Slow
  helps.

Barheim locks behind you. **Desert Patient** opens it again later.

## Dalmasca Estersand and Rabanastre

- A Cockatrice sometimes spawns a **Nekhbet** in the Sand-Swept Naze. It's
  tough for now. Steal its **Rainbow Egg** for the
  [Hi-Potion Pack](bazaar.md#hi-potion-pack) if you can.
- South Bank Village has an orange save crystal that teleports.
- Back in Rabanastre the **Moogling** shuttles are free.
- **Montblanc** pays for the Flans, Firemane and Mimic Queen, and gives you
  two elite hunts: **Cluckatrice** and **Rocktoise**.
- **Buy:** Decoy from the Clan Provisioner if it's in stock. It makes a
  real tank possible.
- **Gambit shop** opens with everything for sale. Get the "Self" gambits.
- Take the Sword of the Old Order to Azelas in North Sprawl.
  **Basch joins for good.** Spend his stored LP.

### Ktjn, Question 1

The Viera on the stairs in south Muthru Bazaar. Her answers decide your
reward at the end, and each question can only be answered in its window.

- **Answer:** "Yes, you should be ashamed."

### Hunt: Wraith (Rank I)

- **Petitioner:** Milha, Rabanastre
- **Where:** Garamsythe Waterway
- It casts Doom, so kill it fast.
- Afterward, take the **Dusty Letter** from the table in the private
  residence. It starts **Three Medallions**.

Talk to Balthier in the Aerodrome to fly to Bhujerba.

## Bhujerba and the Lhusu Mines

- "Lamont" joins as a guest. He uses Potions from your inventory, so switch
  his gambit to Cure.
- Buy the Bhujerba and Lhusu Mines maps.
- **Buy:** Protect, Shell, Raise, Water and Charge if you have the gil.
- **Chests:** **Numerology** in Transitway 1, northeast passage. If you
  didn't take Silence in the Yardang Labyrinth, it's at the Shaft Entry,
  right of the path down.
- Keep Libra on; the side paths are trapped.
- **Grind on the Shunia Twinspan.** Chain the Skeletons for Bone Fragments
  (193 gil each). Two areas away resets them. Level 15–20 is easy here.

### Boss: Ba'Gamnan (optional)

- **HP:** 3,983
- **Steal:** Round Shield (rare)
- You can just run back to the entrance. Beating him gives nothing. He
  starts with Protect and Shell, and his attacks can Silence.

### Back in Bhujerba

- Spread the word that Basch is alive: testify near people with ledgers
  (Parijanahs) and avoid the guards (Sainikahs).
- After the hideout, read the Cloudborne notice board.
- **Buy:** new weapons and armor are in stock now.

#### Hunt: Nidhogg (Rank I)

- **Petitioner:** Aekom, Bhujerba
- **Where:** Lhusu Mines, Transitway 1

#### Hunt: Rocktoise (Rank I)

- **Petitioner:** Pilika, Bhujerba
- **Where:** Lhusu Mines, Site 2
- Afterward, talk to Pilika again for **Pilika's Diary**: fetch it from
  Clio's Technicks and bring it back before she leaves town.

#### Hunt: Cluckatrice (Rank I)

- **Petitioner:** Dania, Nomad Village (Giza Plains)
- **Where:** Giza Plains, with three Chickatrice
- Bring about 10 Gold Needles; it petrifies.

When you're ready, talk to the Sainikah at the red X on Travica Way.

## Dreadnought Leviathan

- Vossler joins as a guest. You get the ship's map. The Leviathan can't be
  revisited.
- Red laser walls call guards. Nothing worse happens.
- **Chests:** **Sleep** in the Starboard Section and **Infuse** in the
  Sub-control Room.
- Beat the two judges at the Central Brig for the **No. 1 Brig Key**.
- **C-201:** the Systems Access Key. **C-203:** Tetran the moogle's shop.
  **C-202:** **Ashe joins for good.**
- **Buy from Tetran:** Shell, Protect, Raise, Water and Disable if you
  don't have them, plus Gold Needles and Prince's Kisses.
- Reset the alarm at a security station in the Large Freight Stores for a
  quiet run back.
- **Penelo joins for good** in the Port Section. Larsa gives her the
  Manufactured Nethicite.

### Boss: Judge Ghis

- **HP:** 4,120
- **Steal:** **Germinas Boots** (rare). They block Immobilize and Disable,
  and are worth the effort.
- Kill the three soldiers first. Blind and Slow Ghis, Disable the soldiers.
  He has Protect and Shell. Keep Blindna on a gambit.

## Westersand, Before the Sandsea

You land in the Westersand.

- **Buy from Lohen:** Cura, Aero, Gravity, Poach and Horology. Black Belts
  and a Rose Corsage too.
- **Buy the Monographs** you've unlocked if you have the gil.
- Rabanastre now sells **Stop**, and new weapons and armor.
- Talk to Masyua in the Nomad Village for **Sunstones** (repeatable side
  quest).

### Ktjn, Question 2

- **Answer:** "You should've thought it through."

### Hunt: Wyvern Lord (Rank II)

- **Petitioner:** Sherral, upstairs in Amal's Weaponry
- **Where:** Nam-Yensa Sandsea, Simoon Bluff
- Easier once you've reached the Tomb's save crystal and shop.

## Ogir-Yensa and Nam-Yensa Sandsea

- Keep Libra on; traps everywhere. Vossler joins as a guest again.
- **Chests (Ogir-Yensa):** **Infuse** next to the first platform, **Sleep**
  at the top of it, a **Golden Amulet** on the northeast oil platform, and
  **Reflect** in the northwest.
- **Grind** the Alraunes on Platform 1 for Succulent Fruit (363 gil each).
- Enter Nam-Yensa by the north route. Help the Urutan-Yensa kill the
  **Urutan Eater** (weak to wind), then follow up with the moogle for the
  **Eksir Berries**. They make Garuda much easier.
- **Chests (Nam-Yensa):** **Balance** in the Demesne of the Sandqueen and
  an **Embroidered Tippet** on the Simoon Bluff platform.

## Tomb of King Raithwall

### Boss: Garuda

- **HP:** 6,754
- **Weak:** dark, fire, thunder, water
- **Steal:** Lightning Fang (rare)
- It flies, so melee can't hit it. Use magick, bows and guns. Use the Eksir
  Berries to lower its attack and calm it down.

### Valley of the Dead

- **Buy from Dyce** at the foot of the stairs. Get 10 Prince's Kisses.

### Boss: Demon Wall (optional)

- **HP:** 22,187
- **Weak:** wind
- **Steal:** Quality Stone (rare)
- It pushes you back along the bridge. Run for the far door if it's going
  badly. Set gambits for Prince's Kiss and Blindna.
- **Reward:** an Azure Jewel in the wall opens paths to a chest with a
  **Scathe Mote**. Reload if it's only gil.

### Boss: Demon Wall

- **HP:** 10,332
- **Weak:** wind
- Same fight, with a longer bridge. Ignore the braziers.

### Royal Passage and the Cloister of Flame

- **Chests:** **Dispel** at the end of the north passage that stays level
  (not the stairs down), and **Vanish** at the bottom of the south stairs.
  Leave and come back if Dispel isn't there.
- Hit the switches at the ends of the Northfall and Southfall Passages.
- Buy Handkerchiefs and set a gambit for Oil.
- **Chests:** **Shades of Black** in the Cloister of Flame, after Belias.

### Boss: Belias

- **HP:** 15,943
- **Weak:** water · **Absorbs:** fire
- **Steal:** High Arcana (rare)
- Shell up, Slow him, use Water. Remove Oil.
- **Belias** goes on the license board. Only one character can take each
  Esper.
- **Second jobs open.** Each character can now take a second job.

## The Shiva

### Boss: Vossler

- **HP:** 9,318
- **Steal:** Black Belt (rare)
- Kill the four soldiers first. He enrages under 40% and goes after Basch.
  Fran is berserk if she's in the party, which helps.

## Rabanastre, After the Shiva

- **Montblanc** pays for the Demon Walls and Belias. He offers the elite
  hunt for the **Gil Snapper**.
- **Buy:** Stop, Cura, Aero and Gravity if you skipped them. New gear at
  Amal's.
- New bills: Croakadile and Enkelados can be done soon. White Mousse,
  Marilith and Ring Wyrm are tougher; talk to their petitioners now
  anyway (the Tavernmaster, Balzac in Lowtown, Sorbet at the Westgate).

### Ktjn, Question 3

- **Answer:** "It'll never happen."

### Side quests

- **Lost Cactoid:** rescue Ruksel in the North Bank Village (Estersand),
  then finish **Desert Patient** for the **Barheim Key**.
- **Bhujerban Madhu:** find 14 bottles and sell them to Rabanastre locals
  for 1,000 gil each.
- **July:** go to Nalbina Fortress for her scene. **Missable:** start it
  before you reach the Mt Bur-Omisace temple. You finish it in Archades for
  the **Salamand Halcyon**.

## Giza Plains (The Rains)

- **Chests:** **Regen** in the Cockatrice pen in the Nomad Village.
- **Storm Elementals** wander here. They attack if you cast magick nearby.
  Leave them alone for now.
- **Withered Trees:** strike all six (Throne Road, Nomad Village, Gizas
  North Bank, Crystal Glade, Starfall Field, Toam Hills) to open the Tracks
  of the Beast.

### Hunt: Croakadile (Rank II)

- **Petitioner:** Sadeen, Nomad Village
- **Where:** Giza Plains, Starfall Field

### Hunt: Gil Snapper (Rank III)

- **Petitioner:** Nanau, Crystal Glade
- **Where:** Tracks of the Beast
- Easier once Larsa joins. The rewards are in the Dry season.

## Ozmone Plain and Jahara

- Set gambits for flying enemies.
- Don't go into the Henne Mines cave yet.
- Three optional Espers can now be reached: **Adrammelech** (Zertinan
  Caverns), **Zalera** (Barheim Passage) and **Cúchulainn** (Garamsythe
  Waterway). Leave them until later.
- **Buy in Jahara:** Fira, Stona, Bleed and Hi-Potions. **Magick Gloves**
  and **Blazer Gloves**.
- Give the **Jaya Stick** to Supinelu. Don't sell it. He gives you a
  **Recurve Crossbow**, Onion Bolts and a free chocobo ride.
- **Larsa joins** as a guest. He uses your Hi-Potions; edit that gambit if
  you'd rather he didn't.

### Hunt: Enkelados (Rank II)

- **Petitioner:** Low-Chief Sugumu, Jahara
- **Where:** Ozmone Plain, The Shred. Kill all the Wus to make it appear.

## Golmore Jungle and Eruyt Village

- Panthers petrify, silence and sleep at once. Set Esuna or item gambits
  on two people. Run from the Hellhounds.
- **Buy from Tetran in Eruyt:** Esuna, Thundara, Break, Fira, Bleed,
  Stona. **Demonsbane**, Flame Shield and **Agate Ring** too.
- **Chests:** **Haste** in the Fane of the Path, after you talk to Jote.
- Judge Hausen in Jahara gives you 3 Hi-Potions and an Ether.

### Before the Henne Mines

- **Montblanc** gives you Headhunter and Ward of Justice rewards, and the
  **Orthros** hunt (Contrite Thief, Lowtown). Orthros can wait.
- **Viera Matchmaking:** help the wandering Viera in Rabanastre.

#### Hunt: Ring Wyrm (Rank III)

- **Petitioner:** Balzac, Lowtown
- **Where:** Dalmasca Westersand, Windtrace Dunes, during a sandstorm
- Use Decoy on your tank. Larsa helps.

## Henne Mines

- Borrow the wounded soldier's chocobo at the Sunlit Path (give him
  Potions) to cross the tall grass.
- The gate switchboards: the switch color is the color of the closed gates.
- **Chests:** **Bonecrusher** in the northeast room of the Phase 1 Dig.
- **Grind** the Jellies at Pithead Junction B. Flip the switch, fight, go
  two areas away and back. Yellow Liquid sells for 531 gil.

### Boss: Tiamat

- **HP:** 49,993
- **Steal:** Leo Gem (rare)
- It casts Disablega, so equip **Black Belts**. Its breath saps and its
  hits petrify. Tank with Decoy, Protect and Shell, and keep Esuna ready.

You get **Lente's Tear** back in Eruyt.

## Golmore Jungle to Mt Bur-Omisace

- **Montblanc** pays for Tiamat.

### Hunt: Ixtab (Rank II)

- **Petitioner:** High-chief Zayalu, Jahara
- **Where:** Henne Mines, Phase 1 Shaft. Bill No. 11 in the Sandsea.

### Side quest: Earth Tyrant

- **Petitioner:** Rimzat, Dalmasca Westersand
- Hard for now, but doable with Larsa.

### Boss: Elder Wyrm (optional)

- **HP:** 71,692
- **Weak:** fire
- **Steal:** Emperor Scale (rare)
- Kill the two Treants first. Sporefall confuses, oils, poisons and blinds.
  Have two Esuna users, a Handkerchief gambit and a Rose Corsage on your
  healer. You can avoid it by going around through the Feywood, but that's
  slower.

## Mt Bur-Omisace

- **Buy:** Curaga, Blizzara, Berserk, Darkra and Souleater. **Three
  Nishijin Belts** for Mateus, and a **Bowline Sash**.
- Buy the Paramina Rift map.
- **Larsa leaves** once you go up to the temple. Do the hunts below first.
- **Montblanc** pays for the Earth Tyrant and Elder Wyrm. He gives you the
  elite hunts **Trickster** and **Antlion**. Both can wait.
- **Buy:** Bubble at the Clan Provisioner when it's in stock.

### Ktjn, Question 4

- **Answer:** "I think it's a good idea."

### Hunt: Feral Retriever (Rank III)

- **Petitioner:** Hymms, Sand-strewn Pass on Mt Bur-Omisace
- **Where:** Paramina Rift, Spine of the Icewyrm. Bill No. 12 in the
  Sandsea.

## Paramina Rift

- Keep Libra on.
- **Chests:** **Revive** in the Spine of the Icewyrm and **Death** in the
  Karydine Glacier.

## Stilshrine of Miriam

- Equip the **Dawn Shard** on someone who doesn't need MP to use the
  Pedestals of the Dawn, then take it off.
- **Chests:** **Countdown** in the Ward of Velitation.
- **Life Crystals** are Crystalbugs. Kill them and a real save crystal
  appears.
- Through the hidden wall in the Ward of the Sword-King, the chest at the
  end has a **Shell Shield** or Dark Matter. Reload for the shield.
- Make sure everyone has the **Accessories** license for the Nishijin Belt.
- Turn the three **Stone Brave** statues until their eyes glow pink. The
  colossus then lifts its sword.

### Boss: Vinuskar

- **HP:** 15,138
- **Steal:** **Thief's Cuffs** (uncommon). Get them.
- Its field slows anyone in metal gear. Light armor, bows and guns help.
- **Chests:** a **Sage's Ring** in the room after.

### Boss: Mateus

- **HP:** 34,259
- **Absorbs:** ice
- **Steal:** High Arcana (rare)
- Equip **Nishijin Belts** and an Ice Shield. Turn off magick attacks; it
  has Reflect. Kill the five Ice Azers first (they sleep you). Protect,
  Shell, Haste and Decoy.

## Mt Bur-Omisace, Return

### Boss: Judge Bergan

- **HP:** 17,200
- **Steal:** Ruby Ring (uncommon)
- Kill the three judges while Bergan is still walking over. He's physical,

- **Missable for now:** talk to the Acolyte in front of the temple for the

## Sochen Cave Palace

Keep Libra on.

### Boss: Mandragoras

- **HP:** 9,069 each
- Five of them. Take them one or two at a time. They inflict most status
  effects. Steal from all of them; it sells well.
- The Wayfarer pays 1,000 gil and 3 Remedies. Not worth the walk back.

### The Pilgrim's Door

Going through a door between the Mirror of the Soul and the Falls of Time
toggles the waterfalls. Number the lower doors 1–5 and the upper doors A–E,
left to right. From the Falls of Time:

1. Down 5, round to 1, up.
2. Up A, down D.
3. Down 4, round to 2, up.
4. Up B, down C. The Pilgrim's Door (3) opens.
