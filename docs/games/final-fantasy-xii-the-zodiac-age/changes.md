# Changes from the PS2 Version

The Zodiac Age is a remaster of **International Zodiac Job System** (IZJS), a
Japan-only 2007 rerelease of Final Fantasy XII. Most of the big changes came
from IZJS, so if you played the original PS2 release outside Japan, everything
below is new to you.

## Jobs and License Boards

- **12 jobs instead of one shared License Board.** In the original, every
  character shared the same board and could learn everything. Now each board
  belongs to a job: Archer, Black Mage, Bushi, Foebreaker, Knight, Machinist,
  Monk, Red Battlemage, Shikari, Time Battlemage, Uhlan and White Mage.
- **Two jobs per character.** After defeating Belias you can unlock a second
  job, and the two boards' licenses combine. Each job can only be taken by one
  character, so a party of six covers all 12.
- **Jobs can be reset.** Talk to Montblanc in Rabanastre to reset a party
  member's jobs. This came with the Switch and Xbox release.
- **Quickenings use their own gauge,** not MP, and characters gain max MP from
  leveling up.

## Battle

- **Speed mode.** Toggle 2x or 4x speed for combat and exploration (set the
  speed in the menu). Music and cutscenes play at normal speed.
- **Guests and Espers can be controlled.** You can take direct control of
  them and set their Gambits. Guests also level up, but you can't change their
  equipment.
- **No spell queue.** In the original, spells waited for earlier effects to
  finish. Now they cast as soon as they're ready.
- **No 9,999 damage cap.**
- **More Gambits.** 16 new Gambit conditions, and you can store **three
  Gambit sets** to switch between.
- **Rebalancing.** Some character stats, enemy stats, item and equipment
  effects, shop stock and Hunt rewards changed, and a few new enemies were
  added.
- **New ultimate weapons.** One for each character.

## Treasure and Magick

- **Chests were reshuffled.** Most treasure contents changed, and chests
  respawn after you move one area away, instead of three.
- **Some magicks moved to chests.** Some magicks that were sold in shops are
  now found in chests, often later in the game.
- **Some magicks were recategorized.** For example, Bravery moved from Green
  to White Magick.
- **The Zodiac Spear is easier to get.** In the original, opening four
  specific chests ruined your chance at it. That's gone, and the spear is in a
  different place.
- **Monographs.** New Bazaar items that improve drops from certain enemy types.
  See [Monographs](monographs.md).

## Exploration

- **Map overlay.** Show the area map on screen while you explore.
- **Autosave** on every area change, and faster loading.
- **Camera axes** can be inverted.

## Modes

- **Trial Mode.** 100 stages of fights against enemies and bosses, using
  characters from your main save. Some rewards can be carried back into your
  story save.
- **New Game Plus** starts at level 90. On Switch, it also carries over some
  of your items, weapons, magicks and gil.
- **New Game Minus** keeps characters at level 1 for the whole game.

## Presentation

- **HD graphics** in widescreen, with improved textures.
- **Remastered soundtrack** with 8 new tracks. You can switch between it and
  the original soundtrack.
- **Japanese or English voices.**
- **Improved audio**, with 7.1 surround sound.
- **Trophies and achievements**, on the platforms that have them.

## The Switch Version

- Runs at **30 fps**, docked and handheld.
- Renders at about **864p docked** and **576p handheld**, below the screen's
  native resolution.
- Some textures, cutscenes and audio were compressed to fit the game in 16 GB.
- Has the job reset, the three Gambit sets and the improved New Game Plus,
  which first came with the Switch and Xbox release and were added to PS4 and
  PC in 2020.
