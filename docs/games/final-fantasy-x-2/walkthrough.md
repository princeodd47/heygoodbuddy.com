# Walkthrough

From the opening concert to the point where the Fiend Arena is worth
playing: the Luca prologue, the first Celsius visit, the Mt. Gagazet
Floating Ruins and your first three catches. The Creature Creator opens the
first time you board the Celsius, but the Black Mage Dressphere from the
Gagazet mission is what your Red Elemental will want, so this page stops
just after it.

**Steal:** lines list what Rikku can take from each boss. Steals here are
early accessories, so they're worth the turn.

## Luca

### Boss: ???? and two Goons

- The concert fight. Plain attacks win it. Rikku can steal a Hi-Potion
  from ????.

### The docks

- Chase the impostor through the docks. Goons keep spawning, so don't
  hang around.
- **Dock 2:** talk to the person in the Moogle suit before going on. It
  restores HP and MP and sets up a scene later in the chapter that counts
  toward 100%.

### Boss: Logos and Ormi

- **HP:** Logos 86, Ormi 97
- **Steal:** Silver Glasses (Logos), Iron Bangle (Ormi)
- Both just attack. Steal from each, then attack.

### Boss: Leblanc

- **HP:** 130
- **Steal:** Silver Bracer
- Yuna is locked into Songstress for this fight. Keep Darkness Dance up
  and Leblanc will barely land a hit. Yuna gets the First Steps Garment
  Grid.

## Aboard the Celsius

- **Brother and Buddy:** talk to both for Al Bhed Primers. Brother also
  gives you the **Festivalist Dressphere** (new in the HD Remaster).
- **Rikku, Paine and Shinra:** talk to each. At Shinra, watch the
  "Journey's Start" Treasure Sphere.
- **Shinra's Garment Grid tutorial** gives you the **Vanguard Garment
  Grid**. Vanguard gives a creature Armor Break and Strength and Magic +10,
  so you'll want it for your Tonberry.
- **Shinra's Creature Creator** is now open. Watch or skip the tutorial;
  either way **Brother** joins as your first creature, and you start with
  one **Trap Pod S**.
- **Cabin:** talk to Barkeep and rest upstairs. You have to rest before
  Buddy's next scene triggers. Barkeep's shop is worth 5 to 10 Phoenix
  Downs if you can afford them.
- **Engine room:** four chests with 4 Potions, 3 Remedies, 4 Phoenix Downs
  and an Ether. They refill every chapter.
- Equip the Silver Glasses, Iron Bangle and Silver Bracer you stole. Save,
  then talk to Buddy on the bridge. The only destination is Mt. Gagazet.

!!! tip "Don't use your Trap Pod S yet"
    The Fiend Arena's Standard Cup is open already, but you have nothing to
    put in it except Brother. Save the pod for the Chocobo, Tonberry or Red
    Elemental at the end of this page.

## Mt. Gagazet: Floating Ruins

Save at the Celsius before you go. Part of this mission is timed and you
only get one try.

- **Upper Tier:** hold the jump button at gaps rather than tapping it.
  At the second jump, drop to the lower ledge for a **Yellow Ring**.
- Save at the sphere at the bottom of the stairs, then ride the elevator
  down. At the bottom, go left for an **Elixir**, then climb the ledge on
  the right.

### Boss: Leblanc, Logos and Ormi

- **HP:** Leblanc 120, Logos 100, Ormi 130
- **Steal:** Tiara (Leblanc), White Cape (Logos), Gauntlets (Ormi)
- Leblanc's Love Tap hastes an ally, so take her out first once you've
  stolen everything.

### Mission: Outrun the Leblanc Syndicate

You have six minutes. The timer stops in battle, in the menu and while
paused, so don't skip healing.

- Grab the **Mega Phoenix** past the pillar on the north side, then climb
  the fallen pillar.
- At the next fallen pillar, drop to the lower platform on its left
  instead of climbing it. Jump the gap south. Logos leaves you two Goons
  and two Recoils.
- **Inside:** press the switches at the end of the left and right
  corridors. Drop into the pit ahead for a **White Ring**.
- After the stone platforms, the path on the right is a detour outside to
  a **Red Ring**. It costs time, but there's room for it.
- Back on the main path, Ormi stops you for a fight. At the end of the
  next fallen column, jump right onto the stone path. It circles south to
  a **Star Pendant**.
- Jump across at the front of the ruins to finish the mission. Save, then
  climb on for a **Muscle Belt**. You only get this chest if you won the
  race.

### Boss: Boris

- **HP:** 480
- **Weak:** fire, ice
- **Steal:** Remedy
- **Drops:** Star Pendant
- Sticky End hits everyone for about 50 and causes Stop. Power Break and
  Armor Break from a Warrior make it a short fight. The Red and White
  Rings let you cast Fire and Blizzard.

## Back on the Celsius

- **Talk to Brother first**, on the bridge floor, and choose "Comfort
  him". If you talk to Shinra first, you lose the scene.
- **Shinra** gives you the **Black Mage Dressphere**.
- Two Hotspots open: Besaid Island and the Zanarkand Ruins. This is where
  the page hands off to the Creature Creator.

## Your first catches

Open the Creature Creator at Shinra and pick **Trap Creature**. Choose a
location and a pod, then **Round Up** to see what you caught. A Shinra
face in the pod means nothing was caught and the pod is gone.

| Creature | Pod | Best place to set it now |
|----------|-----|--------------------------|
| **Chocobo** | S | Mi'ihen Highroad |
| **Tonberry** | S | Mushroom Rock Road |
| **Red Elemental** | S | Kilika Island |

Luca can also give all three, but it has nearly every creature in its
list, so the odds are worse there. See [Creatures](creatures.md) for
every location.

- **More pods:** every tournament gives one, win or lose. Releasing a
  creature whose story is finished gives one too.
- **Out of pods:** with zero pods, back out of Trap Creature and the game
  hands you a free Trap Pod S or M. You can never run out, so keep rolling
  until the three you want turn up. If you get an M, set it somewhere and
  round it up to clear it.
- **Wrong catch:** you can hold eight creatures. Release the ones you
  don't want to make room.
- A creature joins at about your party's average level. Its Fiend Tale
  only moves forward when it gains levels, and you get a free Fiend Tale
  level at the end of each of the first four chapters. Catch these three
  early.

Before you send a creature into a fight, give it a Garment Grid and a
Dressphere from the main menu. The [team guide](creature-team.md) covers
what to give each one and the cup order from here.
