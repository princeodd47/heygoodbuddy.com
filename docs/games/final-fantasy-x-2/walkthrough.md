# Walkthrough

Chapter 1 from the opening concert to the YSLS-Zero fight, in an order
that keeps 100% Story Completion on track. It also covers the Creature
Creator from the moment it opens, so you can start on the Fiend Arena
whenever you like.

- **100%:** marks a scene or choice that counts toward 100% Story
  Completion. Most of these can be missed for good.
- **Checkpoint:** the game rounds your percentage down, so it only
  proves you're on track when the total lands on a whole number. These
  are the spots where it does. Check it on your save file or the NavMap.
- **Steal:** and **Drop:** lines list the items worth taking. See the
  [Bestiary](bestiary.md) for every enemy.
- **Creatures:** lines say what's worth trapping at that location. Pods
  are set from the Creature Creator menu on the Celsius, so you don't
  have to be there. See
  [Creatures](creatures.md) and [Building the Team](creature-team.md).

**Order matters.** Each chapter ends when you finish its last Hotspot, and
anything you skipped stays skipped. Zanarkand sends you straight into the
Kilika finale, so finish everything else first.

## Luca

### Boss: ???? and two Goons

- **Steal:** Hi-Potion (????)
- The concert fight. Plain attacks win it.

### The docks

- Chase the impostor through the docks. Goons keep spawning, so don't
  hang around.
- **100%:** on **Dock 2**, talk to the person in the Moogle suit before
  you go on. It restores HP and MP and unlocks a scene when you return to
  Luca later in the chapter.

### Boss: Logos and Ormi

- **HP:** Logos 86, Ormi 97
- **Steal:** Silver Glasses (Logos), Iron Bangle (Ormi)

### Boss: Leblanc

- **HP:** 130
- **Steal:** Silver Bracer
- Yuna is stuck in Songstress. Keep Darkness Dance up and Leblanc barely
  lands a hit. Yuna gets the **First Steps Garment Grid**.

## Aboard the Celsius

- **Brother and Buddy:** talk to both for Al Bhed Primers. Brother also
  gives you the **Festivalist Dressphere** (new in the HD Remaster).
- **100%:** talk to **Rikku**, **Paine** and **Shinra**, and watch the
  **"Journey's Start"** Treasure Sphere at Shinra. Each is worth 0.2%.
- **Checkpoint:** 3% once you've watched "Journey's Start".
- **Shinra's Garment Grid tutorial** gives the **Vanguard Garment Grid**.
- **Shinra's Creature Creator tutorial** gives a **Trap Pod S, M and L**.
  **Brother** joins as your first creature whether you watch it or not.
- **100%:** in the cabin, talk to **Barkeep** and rest upstairs. Buddy's
  next scene won't trigger until you've rested.
- **Rest in the cabin once every chapter.** Each rest counts toward a
  scene in Chapter 5 that's worth 100%. In Chapters 2 to 4 nothing
  happens when you rest, so it's easy to forget.
- **Engine room:** four chests with 4 Potions, 3 Remedies, 4 Phoenix Downs
  and an Ether. They refill every chapter.
- Equip the Silver Glasses, Iron Bangle and Silver Bracer. Save, then talk
  to Buddy. The only destination is Mt. Gagazet.

!!! tip "Creatures"
    The Fiend Arena is open now, but hold on to your pods for the moment.
    The Trap Pod L is best spent on a **Flame Dragon** in Besaid (only
    Chapters 1 to 4, and needed for the Aeon Cup), and the S pod on a
    Chocobo, Tonberry or Red Elemental. See
    [Your first catches](#your-first-catches).

## Mt. Gagazet: Floating Ruins

Save first. Part of this mission is timed and you only get one try.

- **Upper Tier:** hold the jump button at gaps rather than tapping it.
  At the second jump, drop to the lower ledge for a **Yellow Ring**.
- **Checkpoint:** 4% at the Save Sphere at the bottom of the stairs.
- Ride the elevator down. Go left at the bottom for an **Elixir**, then
  climb the ledge on the right.
- **Steal:** Divebeaks sometimes give an Ether.

### Boss: Leblanc, Logos and Ormi

- **HP:** Leblanc 120, Logos 100, Ormi 130
- **Steal:** Tiara (Leblanc), White Cape (Logos), Gauntlets (Ormi)
- Take Leblanc out first once you've stolen everything; her Love Tap
  hastes an ally.

### Mission: Outrun the Leblanc Syndicate

- **100%:** you have to win the race. You have six minutes, and the timer
  stops in battle, in the menu and while paused.
- Grab the **Mega Phoenix** past the pillar on the north side, then climb
  the fallen pillar.
- At the next fallen pillar, drop to the platform on its left instead.
  Jump the gap south; Logos leaves you two Goons and two Recoils.
- **Inside:** press the switches at the end of the left and right
  corridors. Drop into the pit ahead for a **White Ring**.
- After the stone platforms, the path on the right leads outside to a
  **Red Ring**. It costs a little time but there's room for it.
- Ormi stops you for a fight on the main path. At the end of the next
  fallen column, jump right onto the stone path. It circles south to a
  **Star Pendant**.
- Jump across at the front of the ruins to finish. Save, then climb for a
  **Muscle Belt**, which is only there if you won.

### Boss: Boris

- **HP:** 480
- **Weak:** fire, ice
- **Steal:** Remedy
- **Drop:** Star Pendant and Faerie Earrings, both from one fight
- Sticky End hits everyone for about 50 and causes Stop. Power Break and
  Armor Break make it short. The Red and White Rings cast Fire and
  Blizzard.

### Back on the Celsius

- **100%:** talk to **Brother** on the bridge floor *before* Shinra, and
  choose "Comfort him". Talk to Shinra first and the scene is gone.
- **Shinra** gives you the **Black Mage Dressphere**.
- Two Hotspots open: Besaid Island and the Zanarkand Ruins. Do Besaid
  now and leave Zanarkand for last.

## Your first catches

Open the Creature Creator at Shinra and pick **Trap Creature**. Choose a
location and a pod, then **Round Up** to see what you caught. A Shinra
face in the pod means nothing was caught and the pod is gone.

| Creature | Pod | Best place to set it now |
|----------|-----|--------------------------|
| **Chocobo** | S | Mi'ihen Highroad |
| **Tonberry** | S | Mushroom Rock Road |
| **Red Elemental** | S | Kilika Island |
| **Flame Dragon** | L | Besaid Island |

Luca can give the first three as well, but it has nearly every creature
in its list, so the odds are worse there.

- **More pods:** every Fiend Arena tournament gives one, win or lose.
  Releasing a creature whose story is finished gives one. Finishing
  Chapters 1 to 4 gives three SP pods each time.
- **Out of pods:** with zero pods, back out of Trap Creature and the game
  hands you a free Trap Pod S or M, so you can keep rolling until the ones
  you want turn up.
- **Wrong catch:** you can hold eight creatures. Release the ones you
  don't want.
- A creature joins at about your party's average level. Its Fiend Tale
  moves forward when it levels, and the end of Chapters 1 to 4 gives each
  creature a free Fiend Tale level. Catch early.

Give each creature a Garment Grid and a Dressphere before it fights.

## Besaid Island

- **100%:** walk north from the Save Sphere into the village for the
  reunion with Wakka, then talk to Lulu in her hut (middle door, east
  side). Yuna and Lulu go up to the Promontory in a scene. When you're
  back, talk to Lulu again and answer "Yes, I'd love to" to stay the
  night. Talk to her outside in the morning to start the mission.
- The shop offers the **Besaid Key** for 900,000 gil. Skip it; the door
  it opens waits until Chapter 3.

### Mission: Where's Wakka?

Find four ciphers to open the cave. They're random each game, marked on
the minimap, and shown on the door panel once you have all four.

- **Promontory:** the stone structure on the right.
- **Next area:** climb onto the structure on the right before you pass
  under it.
- **Waterfall Way:** drop off the back of the wooden bridge to the
  riverbed for **2 Chocobo Wings**.
- **Beach:** follow the child left and climb the ruins. Around the back
  of the ruins are chests with a **Hi-Potion** and **1,000 gil**. The last
  cipher is up the cliff in the far southwest of the beach.
- **Achievement:** change each girl's Dressphere once in any battle here
  for Dressed for the Occasion.
- **Cave:** talk to Wakka. Ignore the second locked door until Chapter 3.
  The west chest has **2 Potions**. Equip the Red Ring, save, then touch
  the sphere on the pedestal.

### Boss: Flame Dragon

- **HP:** 980
- **Weak:** ice. **Absorbs:** fire.
- **Steal:** Hi-Potion
- **Drop:** Red Ring
- Flame Breath hits everyone for 150 to 200, so keep the Red Ring on.
  Its normal attack always goes for whoever has the most HP. Blizzard
  from a Black Mage is your best damage.
- You get the **White Mage Dressphere**, then the **Besaid Sphere** and
  the **Protection Halo Garment Grid** when you show Wakka.
- **Creatures:** Besaid Island is where to set your **Trap Pod L** for a
  Flame Dragon.
- **Steal:** Sallets sometimes give a Light Curtain, Purpureas a Remedy.

## Kilika Island

- **Checkpoint:** 9% when you land.
- **Port chests:** Ether, **Mana Tablet**, 2 Antidotes, 2 Eye Drops,
  Phoenix Down. Two are on the boats near the Save Sphere.
- **Squatter Monkeys:** talk to the woman with the child and monkey
  across the bridge to start this side quest. It isn't needed for 100%.
- **100%:** go to Dona's hut. You see Barthello thrown out, then talk to
  Dona inside. Both scenes are only in this chapter.
- **Roof of Dona's hut:** 1,500 gil.
- **Camera operator** on the east docks: say "Okay, thanks!" to his offer
  to look. Doing this in Chapters 1, 3 and 5 leads to the **Invincible**
  (Break Damage Limit).
- **North port chests:** Light Curtain, 2 Holy Waters, Star Curtain,
  Lunar Curtain.
- **Creatures:** Red Elemental (Trap Pod S).

## Luca, second visit

### Mission: Behind the Scenes

- Yuna is in the Moogle suit. Talk to the worker by the vendor booth in
  the Square and hand his ten balloons to everyone in the plaza except
  the running kid and the patrolling soldier. Two are behind the glass in
  the northwest. You get the **Healing Wind Garment Grid**.
- **100%:** Rikku's scene on the docks only plays if you talked to the
  Moogle on Dock 2 in the opening.

### After the mission

- **Dock 5:** chest at the end with the **Thunder Spawn Garment Grid**.
  Chapters 1 and 2 only.
- **Near the Square:** jump to a chest for **2 Lunar Curtains**.
- **100%:** talk to **Rin** in the stadium's Basement A. He also gives an
  Al Bhed Primer. Missed it? Rin's entry in Shinra's Dossiers plays the
  same line.
- **Shinra** in the other stadium basement explains Sphere Break and gives
  you some coins to start.
- **Creatures:** Luca can trap almost anything, but the odds are spread
  thin.

## Mi'ihen Highroad

- **100%:** the short scene as you arrive is all you need here.
- **Talk to the Al Bhed** along the road. It sets up an event in
  Chapter 4.
- **Chests:**
    - **Oldroad South End:** 2 Phoenix Downs, 500 gil, **Circlet**
    - **Oldroad South:** 2 Antidotes
    - **Oldroad Central:** **Iron Bangle**, 2 Potions, 2 Holy Waters
    - **Travel Agency:** 2 Eye Drops
    - **Newroad:** Potion, 2 Phoenix Downs, 2 Budget Grenades, **Mana
      Spring**, 2 Echo Screens, 2 Softs
- The Travel Agency sells a Silver Bracer, Tiara, Iron Bangle and
  Gauntlets.
- **Steal:** Shantak, Remedy or a rare **Black Choker**; Divebeak, rare
  Ether; Bully Cap and Purpurea, rare Remedy.
- **Drop:** Fly Eye, rare **Black Choker**.
- **Creatures:** Chocobo (Trap Pod S).

## Mushroom Rock Road

- **Alcove near the start:** 1,000 gil and a Hi-Potion. Grab them before
  you talk to Yaibal.
- **100%:** Yaibal asks you to run the gauntlet. Answer **"Sure, we're
  game."** Saying "Not right now" loses the scene for good.
- **100%:** talk to **Clasko** next to Yaibal until he sighs.
- **Checkpoint:** 12% after Clasko.

### Mission: Foggy Fiend Frenzy

- Cross the road, fighting through the fog. **Tonberries** here have
  9,999 HP; just escape.
- **Steal:** a Tonberry always has an **Ether**, which is worth one steal
  before you run. Agamas and Bully Caps sometimes give a Remedy.
- Take the path down to the lower level and into the **Ravine**: **Turbo
  Ether**, Phoenix Down, Hi-Potion. Keep the Turbo Ether; three teach a
  creature Vigor.
- **100%:** go into the tunnel north of the Save Sphere to the **Den of
  Woe**. A scene plays at the entrance. Examine the door, then answer the
  person behind you with "Of course I do!" for a second scene.
- **Crimson Sphere 9:** Ormi drops it here. It's lost for good if you skip
  the Den of Woe now.
- Back up on the road, take the lift at the end. Elma gives a **Glass
  Buckle** and the **Heart of Flame Garment Grid**.
- **Chest south of the lift:** **Favorite Outfit**.

### Youth League Headquarters

- **Chest off the cliff edge** (look for the nub on the minimap):
  **Restless Sleep Garment Grid**. Chapter 1 only.
- **100%:** talk to **Lucil** twice.
- **100%:** talk to **Maechen** by the entrance. Say "By all means", then
  **don't press anything** until his story ends. Any choice mid-story
  costs you the percent. Shake his hand at the end ("Of course."). This
  one conversation is worth 1.8%.

### Back to the Celsius and down again

- **100%:** at Shinra, watch **"Crimson Report 1"** under Treasure
  Spheres.
- **100%:** talk to Buddy, go back down, find **Clasko** by the Save
  Sphere and answer **"The more the merrier!"**
- **100%:** talk to Clasko in the Celsius cabin.
- **Creatures:** Tonberry (Trap Pod S). Red Elemental also turns up here
  in Chapters 1 and 2.

## Djose Temple

- Wait for the line outside the tent to clear, then tell the receptionist
  "I want to dig!"
- **Chest outside:** Phoenix Down. **Inside the temple:** an Al Bhed
  Primer on the floor of the Great Hall and an Echo Screen in the Living
  Quarters.
- Talk to Gippal on the Pilgrimage Road and confirm. You get the **Letter
  of Introduction**. A chest further south has 2 Potions.

## Moonflow

**Warp in from the Celsius.** If you walk in from Djose you can start the
mission without meeting Tobli and lose his scene.

- **100%:** go east to the South Bank and talk to **Tobli** first.

### Mission: Shave the Hypello?

- Talk to the Hypello on the South Bank Road and escort the wagon. Three
  bandit ambushes: one bandit, then two from the front, then two from
  front and back.
- **Don't let a bandit get away with a box.** If one does, reload and
  redo it. A clean run changes Tobli's later scenes and rewards.
- Tobli gives the **Gun Mage Dressphere**, plus a **Circlet** and the
  **Helios Guard Garment Grid** for a perfect run.
- Start the Gun Mage on **Mech Destroyer** for the Kilika boss.
- **Steal:** Shell Shocker, **Iron Bangle** (rare **Mythril Gloves**);
  Protochimera, rare **Yellow Ring**.
- **Drop:** Shell Shocker, **Black Ring** (rare **Titanium Bangle**).

## Guadosalam

- **100%:** go to the top level and try to enter the Farplane.
- Walk down the hall from the Save Sphere to the Thunder Plains.

## Thunder Plains

- The arrival scene is all you need.
- **South chests:** 3 Echo Screens, 2 Potions, **Black Choker**.
- **North chests:** Phoenix Down, 2 Grenades, Ether.
- **Steal:** Ochu, rare **Twist Headband**; Stalwart, rare **Gauntlets**.
- **Drop:** Ochu, rare **Twist Headband**.
- Walk on north to Macalania Woods.

## Macalania Woods

The Amorphous Gels here can drop one girl to 1 HP and hit everyone with
magic. Escaping is fine.

- **100%:** **Bayra**'s scene plays as you enter.
- **100%:** at the Spring (east, then east, then south), talk to
  **Donga**.
- **100%:** on the regular (non-sparkling) path through Central and
  North, talk to **Pukutak**.
- **Chests:** **White Ring** (Central), **Turbo Ether** (Central, on the
  sparkling path), Hi-Potion (Lake Macalania).
- **Steal:** Amorphous Gel, **White Ring** (and it drops a **Blue
  Ring**); Deep Haizhe, **Gold Anklet** (rare **Dragonfly Orb**); Haizhe,
  rare **Pretty Orb**.
- **100%:** at the Spring off the Lake Road, talk to **Tromell four
  times**. He gives Paine's **Full Throttle** Special Dressphere and the
  **Unerring Path Garment Grid**. Full Throttle can still be had in
  Chapter 2, but the percent can't.

### Mission: Follow That O'aka!

- Go north to the Agency Front for the O'aka scene and an Al Bhed Primer.
- Follow him by the sparkling path to skip most fights (one forced
  Amorphous Gel at the old Celestial Mirror junction, which you can
  escape). Past the Save Sphere go east, east again, then north. He's up
  in the trees.
- **100%:** find **O'aka**.
- **Checkpoint:** 20% once you've found him.
- **Let him hide aboard the Celsius.** Later 100% scenes depend on it.
  You get the **Ice Queen Garment Grid**.
- **100%:** open **O'aka's shop** in the Celsius cabin. Buy from him to
  chip away at his 100,000 gil debt, which you want paid off before
  Chapter 3.

## Bikanel Desert

- Follow Rikku until the team collapses. You wake up in the Al Bhed camp
  with an Al Bhed Primer.
- Talk to the people around camp until **Nhadala** arrives, then give her
  the Letter of Introduction.
- Take the hover to the **Western Expanse**.

### Mission: Can You Dig It?

- Dig up the **Scrap Metal** and get back to the hover. You get the
  **Still of Night Garment Grid**.
- **Steal:** Bolt Drake, rare **Yellow Ring**. **Drop:** Bolt Drake, rare
  **NulShock Ring**; Fly Eye, rare **Black Choker**.
- **Don't do extra digging yet.** Dig treasure powers up a later boss, and
  for 100% you'll want to beat it at a low level first.

## Bevelle

- Walk the Highbridge for the Baralai scene.
- Ride the elevator in New Yevon HQ to the second floor and talk to
  Baralai again for a **Tiara**.

## Calm Lands

- Talk to the Open Air, Inc. or Argent, Inc. worker by the hover, open
  the Publicity menu and sign up. Either company is fine.
- The man in the middle starts the **Marriage Campaign**. Say "Why not!?"
- Both campaigns run on pressing the talk-about button with people around
  Spira. They're side quests, not 100%.
- **Chests:** **2 Chocobo Feathers**, Phoenix Down, Ether, 2 Potions (Calm
  Lands Bridge), Phoenix Down (Gorge Bottom).
- **Steal:** Amorphous Gel, **White Ring** (drop **Blue Ring**); Divebeak,
  rare Ether.
- **Creatures:** Chocobo (Trap Pod S).

## Mt. Gagazet

- To Garik's "Ronso not saved yet", answer **"We'll take care of Lian and
  Ayde."**
- Talk to **Kimahri** (more than once if needed) and answer "You have to
  learn to deal with these things!"
- Answer the other Ronso this way. None of it is 100% yet, but it decides
  scenes in later chapters:

    | Ronso says | Answer |
    |------------|--------|
    | Find Lian and Ayde | "Leave it to the Gullwings!" |
    | Many Ronso died to defend Yuna | "I will never forget their sacrifice." |
    | The Guado are scheming | "I'm sure you're right." |
    | Ronso grief is deep | "I can imagine how you must feel…" |
    | Never forgive Seymour or the Guado | "I wouldn't forgive them either." |
    | The fayth's power is fading (in the Fayth Scar, by the teleporter) | "A change for the better, I hope." |

- **Creatures:** this is the last chance before the chapter ends to put
  creatures in your roster for the Chapter 1 Fiend Tale bonus.

## Zanarkand Ruins

- Talk to **Isaaru** up the hill; your answer doesn't matter.
- **Dome chests:** **Mega Phoenix**, Phoenix Down.
- **Drop:** Behemoth, **Circlet** (rare **Yellow Ring**).
- In the Chamber of the Fayth, **Cid** asks if you have a bone to pick.
  Answer **"You bet I do!"** It gives you two chances later (Thunder
  Plains, Chapters 2 and 3) at a 100% scene instead of one.
- Talk to the researcher by Cid to free the monkeys. In the Great Hall,
  don't touch the monkeys (they steal gil), but open the chests:
  Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, **Elixir**, 3 Remedies, Ether.
- **The Beyond:** to "What is the meaning of life?" answer **"Is that
  you, Isaaru?"** for the **Heart Reborn Garment Grid**.

### Boss: Guardian Beast

- **HP:** 2,886
- **Weak:** most elements, so Black Magic works well
- **Steal:** **Defense Veil**
- **Drop:** **Amulet**
- Three swipes, then Curse. Below about 960 HP it swaps Curse for Damocles
  Photon, which halves everyone's HP. A White Mage spamming Pray keeps up.

## Kilika: the Awesome Sphere

The game takes you straight here and the chapter ends after the boss.

- The Squatter Monkey quest is easiest now. The woods close again until
  Chapter 3.
- **Kilika Woods:** climb the second of the two trees leaning over the
  water in the east for a **Megalixir**.
- **Steal:** Stalwart, rare **Gauntlets**; Protochimera, rare **Yellow
  Ring**.
- At the north end, answer "Just a little peek" and then "More, more!" to
  hear both passwords.

### Mission: Awesome Sphere Heist!

Odd number of guards: **"Carved Monkey"**. Even number: **"Craven
Monkey"**. Each correct answer gives a reward.

1. Four guards across the bridge: "Craven Monkey"
2. Three guards (one starts behind a pillar): "Carved Monkey"
3. Four guards: "Craven Monkey"
4. Three guards, then two more join: "Carved Monkey" both times

Getting every one right gives the **Menace of the Deep Garment Grid**.

### Boss: YSLS-Zero

- **HP:** 1,935
- **Steal:** **Mythril Gloves**
- **Drop:** **Wristband**
- Gun Mage's **Mech Destroyer** does heavy damage. Blast Punch halves a
  target's HP, so keep healing. Power Break blunts its punches.

### Chapter 1 ends

- You get the Chapter 1 **Fiend Tale bonus** (every creature you hold
  gains a Fiend Tale level) and **three Trap Pod SP**. Accept them.
- **Checkpoint:** you should be at 25%.
