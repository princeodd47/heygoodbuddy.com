# Building the Team

A step-by-step path from a Chapter 1 starter team to three Impale Machina.
The starter team wins the early cups and the Mascot Dressphere. Almighty
Shinra and Mushroom Cloud clear the late cups. Then they farm the Iron
Dukes that make the Machina the strongest team in the arena.

!!! tip "Tournaments give Trap Pods"
    Every Fiend Arena tournament you play gives you a Trap Pod, whether you
    win or lose. If you're short on pods, enter a cup, even one you can't
    win yet.

| Steps | When | Team | Goal |
|-------|------|------|------|
| 1 to 4 | Chapter 1 | Chocobo, Tonberry, Red Elemental | Catch, equip and reach level 10 |
| 5 to 11 | Chapter 1 on | The same three | Every early cup, Adamantite |
| 12 to 13 | Mid-game | The same three, level 50 or more | Youth League and Mascot |
| 14 | Chapters 2 to 5 | Trap the Machina as they appear | Machina Hunter, Ranger and Leader in hand |
| 15 to 17 | Chapter 5 | Almighty Shinra and Mushroom Cloud | Every cup, then Major Numerus |
| 18 to 19 | Endgame | Machina Ranger, Hunter and Leader | Iron Dukes, then the finished team |

## Chapter 1: the starter team

### Step 1: Trap the starter three

Use a Trap Pod S for each, as soon as you're on the Celsius. The
[walkthrough](walkthrough.md#your-first-catches) covers pods and the
free-pod trick.

| Creature | Trap it in | Role |
|----------|------------|------|
| **Chocobo** | Mi'ihen Highroad (or Luca, Calm Lands) | Fast attacker. High Agility and Magic |
| **Tonberry** | Mushroom Rock Road (or Luca) | Hard hitter. High Strength |
| **Red Elemental** | Kilika Island (or Luca, Mushroom Rock Road and Macalania Woods in Ch. 1 and 2) | Caster with high defenses |

- **Chocobo:** you need one in the party to enter the Chocobo Cup. Players
  report it learns Auto-Haste at level 40, and it can learn Choco Meteor.
- **Tonberry:** players report it has Death Strike from the start.
- **Red Elemental:** a pure caster. Players report it learns an MP-saving
  ability at high levels.
- **While you're in Besaid,** set your Trap Pod L there for a
  **Flame Dragon**. It's only there in Chapters 1 to 4, and the Aeon Cup
  needs it ([step 17](#step-17-open-the-farplane-cup)).

### Step 2: Collect White Mage and the Chapter 1 grids

Creatures get their abilities from their Garment Grid, so a better grid
helps more than a few levels. You start with **First Steps** (Cure, and +5
to Strength, Defense, Magic and Magic Defense) and Shinra's tutorial gives
**Vanguard** (Armor Break, Strength +10, Magic +10). Pick these up next:

1. **Besaid mission, "Where's Wakka?":** beating the Flame Dragon gives
   the **White Mage Dressphere**. Showing Wakka the sphere afterwards gives
   **Protection Halo** (Esuna, and Defense and Magic Defense +10).
2. **Moonflow mission, perfect escort:** **Helios Guard** (Protect and
   SOS Protect).
3. **Macalania Woods:** talk to Tromell at the spring off the Lake Road
   four times for **Unerring Path** (HP and MP +30%, all stats +20,
   Crackdown). It's the best creature grid in Chapter 1.

### Step 3: Equip and feed them

| Creature | Dressphere | Garment Grid |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| **Chocobo** | Thief | Unerring Path |
| **Tonberry** | Thief, or Festivalist against high Defense | Vanguard |
| **Red Elemental** | Black Mage, or White Mage as the healer | Helios Guard or Protection Halo (First Steps until you have them) |

**Thief** on the attackers gives them Mug, which steals while it hits,
and stolen items are what you feed them.

Feed on the main menu's **Creature** screen (see [Feeding](feeding.md)).
Creatures choose their own moves, so make them forget anything you don't
want them wasting turns on. Teach these now if you have the items:

| Ability | Feed | Who | Where to get the items |
|---------|------|-----|------------------------|
| **Life** | 3 Phoenix Down | Red Elemental | Barkeep, engine room chests |
| **Hastega** | 5 Chocobo Wing | Chocobo | Besaid (two on Waterfall Way), steals |
| **Vigor** | 3 Turbo Ether | Everyone | One in a Mushroom Rock chest in Chapter 1; the rest come in [step 9](#step-9-grand-cup-hard-once) |

**Vigor** heals half the creature's max HP for no MP. It's the single best
thing to teach.

### Step 4: Level to 10 on the Mi'ihen Highroad

Put the three creatures in your normal party and fight on the Mi'ihen
Highroad until they're around level 10. They get the EXP from field
battles. The cups are rough before that.

## The early cups

### Step 5: Standard Cup ×3

The first win gives the **Psychic Dressphere**, the third the
**Intrepid Garment Grid**. From about level 12 this cup is easy. Three
wins open Standard Cup: Hard.

### Step 6: Standard Cup: Hard, then the Grand Cup

1. Win **Standard Cup: Hard** once to open the **Grand Cup**.
2. Keep coming back to Standard Cup: Hard. At 3 wins it adds
   **Black Chandelier** and **No Mercy**, and at 4 wins **Hellbreeders**,
   which you want in the Battle Simulator next. The Grand Cup also has
   Black Chandelier.

You only have to *meet* a team in a cup to add it to the Simulator, not
beat it.

### Step 7: Grind in the Battle Simulator

| Team | Creatures | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| **Black Chandelier** | Dark Elemental | 600+ EXP, dies fast |
| **Hellbreeders** | Gug, 2 Tindalos | 2,500+ EXP, good until about level 50 |
| **No Mercy** | Machina Panzer | Decent EXP, drops a Crystal Bangle every time (+50 HP each when fed) |

**Psychic shortcut (test it first):** one video farms Black Chandelier
with all three creatures in the **Psychic** Dressphere. It shows
Telekinesis killing the Dark Elemental in one hit, almost every time from
about level 12. Jegged says a creature's Dressphere doesn't change its
abilities, so check that your creatures get Telekinesis before you rely on
it.

### Step 8: Grand Cup ×3

Three wins open Grand Cup: Hard. The prizes along the way are feed for
Red Elemental:

| Ability | Feed | Prize |
|---------|------|-------|
| **White Wind** | 3 Mega-Potion | 2nd win (4 Mega-Potions) |
| **Full-Life** | 3 Mega Phoenix | 3rd win (4 Mega Phoenixes) |
| **Esuna** | 3 Remedy | 5th win (30 Remedies) |

### Step 9: Grand Cup: Hard once

1. The first win gives the **Abominable Garment Grid** (Ribbon, and +10
   to Strength, Defense, Magic and Magic Defense) and opens the
   **Chocobo Cup**. Put Abominable on the Chocobo.
2. After 3 wins, four teams join:
    - **Hellbreeders** shows up here more than anywhere. If the bracket
      shows Shady Duo or Tonberry the Ripper reaching you first, set the
      stars to 0 in your next fight and run to restart the cup.
    - **Elephant Eater** (Humbaba): kill it in the Simulator with a
      high-level Chocobo in the team. It counters with Meteor as it dies,
      and the Chocobo learns **Meteor**.
    - **Shady Duo** (Mushroom Cloud and Black Elemental): realistic in the
      high 40s to 50s. Darkness helps a lot. Once you can beat it, it
      drops Mana Tonics (+20 MP each when fed).
    - **Tonberry the Ripper** (Mega Tonberry): hits for 10,000 to 20,000.
      Before level 50, steal its **Turbo Ether** for Vigor and accept the
      loss.

### Step 10: Get Adamantite early

**Adamantite** gives constant Protect and Shell, doubles HP and adds 120
Defense and Magic Defense, for -30 Agility. It's the prize at the end of
Machina Panzer's Fiend Tale, and you can reach it in Chapter 1:

1. Trap a **Killer Hound** (Trap Pod S, Bikanel Desert in Chapters 1 and
   2, or Luca). Level it on Black Chandelier until its tale finishes,
   then release it. You can skip the ending.
2. Now trap a **YSLS-Zero** (Trap Pod M, Zanarkand Ruins). Finish its
   tale and release it.
3. Now trap a **Machina Panzer** (Trap Pod L, Thunder Plains). Finish
   its tale, release it, and watch the ending for the Adamantite.

### Step 11: Chocobo Cup

Bring a Chocobo. Prizes in order: **Regal Crown**, **Champion Belt**,
**Rabite's Foot**, then **Minerva's Plate**. It's winnable in the high
30s. Watch for **Today's Friends** (Anything Eater can one-shot early) and
**Chocobo Kings** (three Chocobos with Choco Meteor).

1. Farm it for extra Regal Crowns and Rabite's Feet.
2. Feed a Regal Crown each to Tonberry and Chocobo for **Darkness**. It
   hits all enemies using Strength and ignores Defense; one player reports
   about 9,000 a hit from a Tonberry.
3. Put Adamantite and Minerva's Plate on Red Elemental, and a Crystal
   Bangle and Rabite's Foot on each attacker. Take Minerva's Plate off
   when you're levelling, since it blocks EXP.

## Mid-game: the Youth League

### Step 12: Grand Cup: Hard ×6

Six wins open the **Youth League Tournament**. Play it a few times even
before you can win, so its fighters show up in the Simulator. Three of
them carry spells for Red Elemental:

| Ability | Feed | Where |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Flare** | 1 Black Lore, or 40 Shining Gems | Lulu's rare drop (she joins after your first win) |
| **Turbo Black Magic** | 2 Black Tome | Yaibal's rare drop |
| **Ultima** | 60 Supreme Gem | Elma's rare drop or Lucil's rare steal. It takes a lot of fights |

### Step 13: Win the Youth League

Aim for the mid-50s. One player's team was 52, 54 and 52 after six to
eight hours. **Nooj** is the biggest threat, then Baralai and Lucil. Keep
Protect up; for Lulu, use Reflect or Shell instead.

1. The first win gives the **Mascot Dressphere**. Put Mascot on every
   creature from here on. It gives the biggest stat boost of any
   Dressphere.
2. Later wins give a **Tetra Bracelet**, **Defense Bracer**,
   **Speed Bracer** and **Shmooth Shailing**. Put them away instead of
   feeding them now. Each Machina needs 3 Speed Bracers for Auto-Haste,
   and Shmooth Shailing (+5 to every stat) is the only Agility source
   besides Iron Dukes. Feeding is per creature, so anything the starter
   team eats is lost to the Machina. Defense Bracers teach Auto-Wall,
   which the Machina shouldn't learn ([step 19](#step-19-set-up-the-impale-team)).

## Chapters 2 to 5: the Machina

### Step 14: Trap the Machina as they appear

Machina Ranger, Machina Hunter and Machina Leader all have **Impale**: a
physical attack that **ignores Defense**, with no charge time and a short
animation. All three are Small, so they fill a whole party. Machina
Soldier is caught in the same place as the Leader but doesn't have Impale.

| Machina | When | Where |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Machina Hunter** | Chapter 2 on | Bikanel Desert, Trap Pod S or SP (Machina). In Chapter 5 the S pod stops working there once Cactuar Hollow starts. Also Luca (S). |
| **Machina Ranger** | Chapter 3 on | Mi'ihen Highroad, Trap Pod SP (Machina), or S in Chapters 3 and 4. Also Luca (S). |
| **Machina Leader** | Chapter 5, after the New Cave | Thunder Plains, Trap Pod S or SP (Machina) |

Use a Trap Pod SP set to Machina where you can. It narrows the catch to
machina. Keep them in a spare slot. They level while you play, and they
collect the free Fiend Tale levels at the end of each chapter.

Don't make them your main team yet. Impale only gets strong once their
**Agility** is high, and Agility barely moves without Iron Dukes.

## Chapter 5: clearing the late cups

The Iron Dukes come from Major Numerus, the hardest fight in the arena. It
only appears after four wins of the Farplane Cup, and that cup only opens
when every other cup is won. The usual answer is
**Almighty Shinra and Mushroom Cloud**.

### Step 15: Trap Mushroom Cloud

Trap Pod S in Bevelle, Chapter 5, after you start the Via Infinito. It's
Small. **Pernicious Powder** lowers every enemy's Strength and Magic ten
times. Bosses can't resist that part. The same move also tries a long
list of ailments. It also casts **Ultima**, so the team can hurt
physical-immune enemies.

### Step 16: Recruit Almighty Shinra

The strongest creature in the game. He joins with very high stats and his
Clione hits many times.

1. Trap a **Daeva** (Trap Pod M, Kilika or Luca, Chapter 3 on). Finish
   its tale and release it. It comes back as **Aka Manah**, then
   **Aeshma**, then **Jahi**. Finish and release each.
2. Releasing Jahi starts a Shinra mission and lets you trap a **Tomb**
   (Luca or Djose). Finish it, then trap a **Dolmen**, then (after
   entering the Farplane) a **Monolith** at Djose.
3. Releasing Monolith gives **Ultima Weapon**. Finish and release it to
   open the **Fiend Portal**. A loss there is a Game Over, and HP doesn't
   refill between rounds.
4. Win the Fiend Portal and recruit **Omega Weapon**. Finish its tale and
   release it. Shinra becomes **Almighty Shinra** and the cup becomes the
   **Almighty Shinra Cup**. Win it and recruit him.

### Step 17: Open the Farplane Cup

Win every other cup. Two need extra work:

- **Cactuar Cup:** needs a Cactuar, Trap Pod S in Bikanel after Cactuar
  Hollow starts in Chapter 5.
- **Aeon Cup:** needs all eight Fayth-fragment fiends finished and
  released, including the Flame Dragon from Besaid. See
  [Creatures](creatures.md#fiend-tales-and-releasing).

Almighty Shinra and Mushroom Cloud beat nearly everything. The exception
is **Trema** in the Farplane Cup, whose Defense makes the fight slow. It's
also where the Impale team shines.

## Endgame: the Impale team

### Step 18: Farm Iron Dukes

An eaten **Iron Duke** adds 10 to HP, MP, Strength, Defense, Magic, Magic
Defense, Agility, Luck, Evasion and Accuracy. It's the only real source of
Agility. Shmooth Shailing gives +5, and nothing else feeds Agility at all.

- **Trema:** one Iron Duke in the Via Infinito, Cloister ∞, for beating
  Trema.
- **Farplane Cup:** the fourth win's prize. Later wins give one of the four
  prizes at random.
- **Major Numerus:** each of its four heads (Unum, Duo, Tria, Quattour)
  can drop one. It's the **Outer Plane Envoy** team in the Farplane Cup
  after four wins. Once you've met it, it's in the Battle Simulator.

To farm Major Numerus, put two things on creatures in the fighting team:

1. **Ray of Hope Garment Grid** (Episode Complete in Macalania, Chapter 5):
   gives a creature **Butterfingers**, so every drop is the rare one.
2. **Key to Success** (Farplane Cup second win, or Tobli Productions in
   Chapter 3): doubles drops when a creature wears it.

One player reports about 20 Iron Dukes a fight with both. Without Ray of
Hope, Numerus drops **Invincible** (Break Damage Limit) and **Enterprise**
(Break HP Limit) instead. Farm those first if you need them.

Feed the Iron Dukes into the three Machina, Agility first, along with the
Shmooth Shailings you saved.

### Step 19: Set up the Impale team

Keep each Machina's ability list short so it spams Impale.

| Teach | Feed | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| **Vigor** | 3 Turbo Ether | Free self-heal |
| **Auto-Haste** | 3 Speed Bracer | More turns |
| **First Strike** | 2 Sprint Shoes | Act before the enemy |
| **Break Damage Limit** | 1 Invincible | Impale past 9,999 |
| **Break HP Limit** | 1 Enterprise | Survive big hits |
| **Critical** | 1 Hero Drink | Guaranteed critical hits |
| **Life Preserver** | 3 Safety Bit | No instant KO or petrify |
| **Black Sky** | 1 Arcane Lore | On **one** Machina only. Its long animation doesn't stop the other two from acting. |

- **Dressphere:** Mascot.
- **Garment Grid:** Abominable for Ribbon and stats, Unerring Path for
  HP, MP and stats, or Higher Power for Break HP Limit and HP +50%
  (Chocobo Ranch dungeon, Chapter 5).
- Don't teach **Auto-Wall** to a creature that relies on Vigor.
- **Weakness:** Impale is purely physical. Against a physical-immune
  enemy, swap one Machina for Mushroom Cloud.
