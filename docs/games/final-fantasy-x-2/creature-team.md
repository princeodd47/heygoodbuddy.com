# Building the Team

The road from a Chapter 1 starter team to three Impale Machina. Starter
creatures win the early cups and get you the Mascot Dressphere. A
stronger late-game pair clears the rest of the cups. That pair then
farms the Iron Dukes the Machina need.

| Stage | Team | Goal |
|-------|------|------|
| 1. Chapter 1 | Chocobo, Tonberry, Red Elemental | Standard and Grand Cups, Chocobo Cup |
| 2. Mid-game | The same three, at level 50 or more | Grand Cup: Hard six times, Youth League (Mascot) |
| 3. Chapters 2 to 5 | Trap the Machina as they appear | Machina Hunter, Ranger and Leader in hand |
| 4. Chapter 5 | Almighty Shinra and Mushroom Cloud | Every cup, then Major Numerus |
| 5. Endgame | Machina Ranger, Hunter and Leader | Iron Dukes until their Agility is high |

## Stage 1: the starter team

Trap these with a Trap Pod S as soon as you're on the Celsius. The
[walkthrough](walkthrough.md#your-first-catches) covers pods and the
free-pod trick.

| Creature | Trap it in | Role | Dressphere | Garment Grid |
|----------|------------|------|------------|--------------|
| **Chocobo** | Mi'ihen Highroad (or Luca, Calm Lands) | Fast attacker. High Agility and Magic | Thief | Unerring Path, later Abominable |
| **Tonberry** | Mushroom Rock Road (or Luca) | Hard hitter. High Strength | Thief, or Festivalist against high Defense | Vanguard |
| **Red Elemental** | Kilika Island (or Luca, Mushroom Rock Road and Macalania Woods in Ch. 1 and 2) | Caster with high defenses | Black Mage | First Steps, later Helios Guard |

- **Chocobo:** you need one in the party to enter the Chocobo Cup. Players
  report it learns Auto-Haste at level 40, and it can learn Choco Meteor.
- **Tonberry:** players report it has Death Strike from the start.
- **Red Elemental:** a pure caster. Players report it learns an MP-saving
  ability at high levels.
- **Thief** on the attackers gives them Mug, which steals while it hits,
  and stolen items are what you feed them.
- **Garment Grids give creatures their own abilities:**
    - **Vanguard** (Shinra's Garment Grid tutorial): Armor Break,
      Strength +10, Magic +10.
    - **First Steps** (your starting grid): Cure, and +5 to Strength,
      Defense, Magic and Magic Defense.
    - **Unerring Path** (comes with your first Special Dressphere): HP
      and MP +30%, all stats +20, Crackdown.
    - **Helios Guard** (Chapter 1 Moonflow mission): Protect and SOS
      Protect.
    - **Abominable** (first win of Grand Cup: Hard): Ribbon, and +10 to
      Strength, Defense, Magic and Magic Defense.
    - **Protection Halo** (Chapter 1 Besaid mission): Esuna, and Defense
      and Magic Defense +10.

### What to teach

Feed these on the main menu's **Creature** screen (see
[Feeding](feeding.md)). Creatures choose their own moves, so make them
forget anything you don't want them wasting turns on.

| Ability | Feed | Who | Where to get the items |
|---------|------|-----|------------------------|
| **Vigor** | 3 Turbo Ether | Everyone | Mushroom Rock chest in Chapter 1, then steal from Mega Tonberry (Tonberry the Ripper, Grand Cup: Hard) |
| **White Wind** | 3 Mega-Potion | Red Elemental | Grand Cup prize (2nd win) |
| **Life** | 3 Phoenix Down | Red Elemental | Barkeep, engine room chests |
| **Esuna** | 3 Remedy | Red Elemental | Grand Cup prize (5th win gives 30) |
| **Hastega** | 5 Chocobo Wing | Chocobo | Besaid (two on Waterfall Way), steals |
| **Darkness** | 1 Regal Crown | Tonberry | Chocobo Cup prize (1st win) |
| **Flare** | 1 Black Lore, or 40 Shining Gems | Red Elemental | Black Lore is an accessory; see [Accessories](accessories.md) |

**Vigor** heals half the creature's max HP for no MP. It's the single best
thing to teach. **Darkness** hits all enemies using Strength and ignores
Defense; one player reports about 9,000 a hit from a Tonberry.

## Stage 2: cup order and grinding

1. **Standard Cup ×3.** The first win gives the **Psychic Dressphere**,
   the third the **Intrepid Garment Grid**. Below level 10, level on the
   Mi'ihen Highroad first. From about level 12 this cup is easy.
2. **Standard Cup: Hard ×1** opens the Grand Cup. Keep coming back to it:
   at 3 and 4 wins it adds the grinding teams below.
3. **Grind in the Battle Simulator** against these teams once you've met
   them:

    | Team | Creatures | Why |
    |------|-----------|-----|
    | **Black Chandelier** | Dark Elemental | 600+ EXP, dies fast |
    | **Hellbreeders** | Gug, 2 Tindalos | 2,500+ EXP, good until about level 50 |
    | **No Mercy** | Machina Panzer | Decent EXP, drops Crystal Bangles (feed for max HP) |

4. **Grand Cup ×3** opens Grand Cup: Hard.
5. **Grand Cup: Hard ×1** gives the **Abominable Garment Grid** and opens
   the **Chocobo Cup**. Put Abominable on the Chocobo.
6. **Chocobo Cup** (Chocobo in the party): **Regal Crown**, **Champion
   Belt**, **Rabite's Foot**, then **Minerva's Plate**. Winnable in the high
   30s. Watch for **Today's Friends** (Anything Eater can one-shot early)
   and **Chocobo Kings** (three Chocobos with Choco Meteor).
7. **Grand Cup: Hard ×6** opens the **Youth League Tournament**. After
   three wins two dangerous teams join:
    - **Shady Duo** (Mushroom Cloud and Black Elemental): realistic in the
      high 40s to 50s. Darkness helps a lot.
    - **Tonberry the Ripper** (Mega Tonberry): hits for 10,000 to 20,000.
      Before level 50, steal its **Turbo Ether** and accept the loss.
8. **Youth League Tournament.** Aim for the mid-50s. One player's team
   was 52, 54 and 52 after six to eight hours. **Nooj** is the biggest
   threat, then Baralai and Lucil. Keep Protect up; for Lulu, use Reflect
   or Shell instead. The first win gives the **Mascot Dressphere**. Later
   wins give a **Tetra Bracelet**, **Defense Bracer**, **Speed Bracer** and
   **Shmooth Shailing**.

Put **Mascot** on every creature from here on. It gives the biggest stat
boost of any Dressphere.

## Stage 3: trapping the Machina

Machina Ranger, Machina Hunter and Machina Leader all have **Impale**: a
physical attack that **ignores Defense**, with no charge time and a short
animation. All three are Small, so they fill a whole party. Machina
Soldier is caught in the same place as the Leader but doesn't have Impale.

| Machina | When | Where |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Machina Hunter** | Chapter 2 on | Bikanel Desert, Trap Pod S or SP (Machina). In Chapter 5 the S pod stops working there once Cactuar Hollow starts. Also Luca (S). |
| **Machina Ranger** | Chapter 3 on | Mi'ihen Highroad, Trap Pod SP (Machina), or S in Chapters 3 and 4. Also Luca (S). |
| **Machina Leader** | Chapter 5, after the New Cave | Thunder Plains, Trap Pod S or SP (Machina) |

Use a Trap Pod SP set to Machina where you can. It narrows the catch to
machina. Trap the Hunter and Ranger as soon as they appear and keep them
in a spare slot. They level while you play, and they collect the free
Fiend Tale levels at the end of each chapter.

Don't make them your main team yet. Impale only gets strong once their
**Agility** is high, and Agility barely moves without Iron Dukes.

## Stage 4: a team to clear the late cups

The Iron Dukes come from Major Numerus, the hardest fight in the arena. It
only appears after four wins of the Farplane Cup, and that cup only opens
when every other cup is won. The usual answer is
**Almighty Shinra and Mushroom Cloud**.

- **Mushroom Cloud** (Small): Trap Pod S in Bevelle, Chapter 5, after you
  start the Via Infinito. **Pernicious Powder** lowers every enemy's
  Strength and Magic ten times. Bosses can't resist that part. The same
  move also tries a long list of ailments. It also casts **Ultima**, so
  the team can hurt physical-immune enemies.
- **Almighty Shinra:** the strongest creature in the game. He joins with
  very high stats and his Clione hits many times. Getting him takes this
  chain:
    1. Trap a **Daeva** (Trap Pod M, Kilika or Luca, Chapter 3 on).
       Finish its tale and release it. It comes back as **Aka Manah**,
       then **Aeshma**, then **Jahi**. Finish and release each.
    2. Releasing Jahi starts a Shinra mission and lets you trap a
       **Tomb** (Luca or Djose). Finish it, then trap a **Dolmen**, then
       (after entering the Farplane) a **Monolith** at Djose.
    3. Releasing Monolith gives **Ultima Weapon**. Finish and release it
       to open the **Fiend Portal**. A loss there is a Game Over, and HP
       doesn't refill between rounds.
    4. Win the Fiend Portal and recruit **Omega Weapon**. Finish its tale
       and release it. Shinra becomes **Almighty Shinra** and the cup
       becomes the **Almighty Shinra Cup**. Win it and recruit him.
- **The other cups the Farplane Cup needs:**
    - **Cactuar Cup:** needs a Cactuar, Trap Pod S in Bikanel after
      Cactuar Hollow starts in Chapter 5.
    - **Aeon Cup:** needs all eight Fayth-fragment fiends finished and
      released. Flame Dragon is Chapter 1 to 4 only in Besaid, so trap it
      early. See [Creatures](creatures.md#fiend-tales-and-releasing).

Almighty Shinra and Mushroom Cloud beat nearly everything. The exception
is **Trema** in the Farplane Cup, whose Defense makes the fight slow. It's
also where the Impale team shines.

## Stage 5: the Impale team

### Iron Dukes

An eaten **Iron Duke** adds 10 to HP, MP, Strength, Defense, Magic, Magic
Defense, Agility, Luck, Evasion and Accuracy. It's the only real source of
Agility. Shmooth Shailing gives +5, and nothing else feeds Agility at all.

- **Trema:** one Iron Duke in the Via Infinito, Cloister ∞, for beating
  Trema.
- **Farplane Cup:** the fourth win's prize. Later wins give one of the four
  prizes at random.
- **Major Numerus:** each of its four heads (Unum, Duo, Tria, Quattour)
  can drop one. It's the **Outer Plane Envoy** team in the Farplane Cup
  after four wins. Once you've met it, it's in the Battle Simulator.

To farm Major Numerus, put two things on creatures in the fighting team:

- **Ray of Hope Garment Grid** (Episode Complete in Macalania, Chapter 5):
  gives a creature **Butterfingers**, so every drop is the rare one.
- **Key to Success** (Farplane Cup second win, or Tobli Productions in
  Chapter 3): doubles drops when a creature wears it.

One player reports about 20 Iron Dukes a fight with both. Without Ray of
Hope, Numerus drops **Invincible** (Break Damage Limit) and **Enterprise**
(Break HP Limit) instead. Farm those first if you need them.

Feed the Iron Dukes into the three Machina, Agility first.

### Setup

Keep each Machina's ability list short so it spams Impale.

| Teach | Feed | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| **Vigor** | 3 Turbo Ether | Free self-heal |
| **Auto-Haste** | 3 Speed Bracer | More turns |
| **First Strike** | 2 Sprint Shoes | Act before the enemy |
| **Break Damage Limit** | 1 Invincible | Impale past 9,999 |
| **Break HP Limit** | 1 Enterprise | Survive big hits |
| **Critical** | 1 Hero Drink | Guaranteed critical hits |
| **Life Preserver** | 3 Safety Bit | No instant KO or petrify |
| **Black Sky** | 1 Arcane Lore | On **one** Machina only. Its long animation doesn't stop the other two from acting. |

- **Dressphere:** Mascot.
- **Garment Grid:** Abominable for Ribbon and stats, Unerring Path for
  HP, MP and stats, or Higher Power for Break HP Limit and HP +50%
  (Chocobo Ranch dungeon, Chapter 5).
- Don't teach **Auto-Wall** to a creature that relies on Vigor.
- **Weakness:** Impale is purely physical. Against a physical-immune
  enemy, swap one Machina for Mushroom Cloud.
