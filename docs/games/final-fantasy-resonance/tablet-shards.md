# Tablet Shards

!!! info "Page status"
    Last checked **{{ page.meta.verified }}** on the **{{ page.meta.game_version }}**.
    Shard locations come from third-party guides and haven't all been
    checked in-game yet.

Tablet Shards aren't in chests. Each one is a **golden glow on the ground**,
so they're easy to walk past.

## What they're for

Hand Tablet Shards to the **princess at Oblivion Palace**. She opens reward
boxes once your running total reaches each box's threshold. Totals go up to
99 shards across 25 boxes.

In the demo, 12 shards open the first four boxes in the 1-Star Chamber. The
fourth box needs all 12 and reportedly holds a **Stagger Ring**. Your shard
count carries over to the full game with your save.

## All 12 demo shards

| Area | # | Where |
|------|---|-------|
| **Siren's Tower** | 3 | Ground floor, far right, before the stairs up · right of the northern hallway · just before the final stairs |
| **Grandport Harbor** | 2 | By the tree on the west side of town, before the docks · southern pier, right side, before the barrels |
| **Town of Felicitas** | 2 | Northeast of town, right of the item shop · through a secret passage left of the inn, up the ladder at the end |
| **Kolobos Ruins** | 2 | Behind two tanks on the left, after the first stairs · behind a fallen pillar, after you fall from the bridge and climb back up |
| **Commercial District** (Grandshelt) | 1 | North of the weapon shop, beside the boxes in the corner |
| **Village of Kol** | 1 | Southwest edge of the oasis pond, beside a cactus |
| **Cavern of Swords** | 1 | Back of the cavern, straight in from the entrance |

!!! tip "Easiest to miss"
    The **Felicitas secret passage** next to the inn, and the **Kolobos Ruins
    shard behind the fallen pillar** after the bridge collapses.

??? note "Is it 12 or 17?"
    Most guides count 12. One guide counts 17, adding 2 in the Colosseum,
    2 in Grandshelt Castle and 1 in the Earth Shrine, but gives no exact
    spots and no other guide lists them.
