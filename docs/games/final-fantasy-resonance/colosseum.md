# Colosseum

The Colosseum is a district of **Grandshelt**, through the **west exit** of
the plaza. It's closed early in the game and reopens in Chapter 4. The
bulletin board in Zoldaad, the Imperial Capital, announces it.

There's also a **Tablet Shard** inside. Head all the way west on the first
path.

## How it works

- Sign up with the **Colosseum Receptionist**. Fights come in **leagues** of
  five battles. Win a battle to open the next one.
- The **first win** of each battle gives a one-time reward. Later wins give
  a **Hi-Potion**.
- If you lose, you can try again. Cleared battles can be fought again.
- Each battle's detail screen shows its recommended level and first-win
  reward.
- Win all five battles in a league to get its **Proof of Victory**. It opens
  the next league and adds stock to the shop.

## Fledgling League

Enemy levels are higher than the recommended level, especially in Battle 5.

| Battle | Rec. level | Enemies | First win |
|-------:|-----------:|---------|-----------|
| 1 | 25 | Wyvern (lv 26), Magma Tortoise (lv 26), Coeurl (lv 27) | **Magicite Shard** |
| 2 | 27 | Chimera (lv 28) | **Adamantite** |
| 3 | 29 | Balaur ×2 (lv 32), Simurgh (lv 32), Blood Worm (lv 32) | **Ancient Greatsword** |
| 4 | 31 | Ice Lizard (lv 34), Gray Bomb (lv 33), Lieutenant (lv 34) | **Magicite Shard** |
| 5 | 32 | Allosaur (lv 37), Ice Sculpture (lv 37), Ahriman (lv 37) | **Gaia Hammer** |

The **Gaia Hammer** is a hammer "that can pulverize boulders."

limited to one each. Armor is limited to four each.
