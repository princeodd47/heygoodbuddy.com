## How articles work

Articles are Markdown files in `docs/blog/posts/`. Each one needs a `date`
in its frontmatter, and can have `categories` and `tags`.

Everything above the `<!-- more -->` line shows as the preview on the
Articles page. Everything below it only shows on the full article.

## What's on the site

- **[Games](../../games/index.md):** guides and reference notes, one section
  per game.
- **[Tags](../../tags.md):** every page and article, grouped by topic.
